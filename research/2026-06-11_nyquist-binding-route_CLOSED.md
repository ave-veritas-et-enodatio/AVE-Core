## §0 TL;DR

A two-part route was proposed: **(a)** a binding *principle* — "the trap agent must propagate faster than the trapped content; only `c_bulk` can bind transverse waves"; and **(b)** a *cash-out* — "`c/ℓ_node = ω_Compton` exactly → the electron sits AT the lattice sampling limit → its mass is the sampling-rate readout, which dissolves the `ℓ_node` circularity flag." Both parts fail. The cash-out is a **radians-vs-cycles unit identity inflated into a Nyquist statement**: the canonical lattice Nyquist edge is `k_max = π/ℓ_node`, while the electron carrier is `k_C = 1/ℓ_node = k_max/π` — a **factor π BELOW** the zone edge, with the residual π irrational and unclosable. The binding *principle* contradicts canon three ways: inside the saturated core `c_eff → ∞` (trapped content is *faster* than the wall; trapping is a `Γ→−1` impedance mismatch, not a speed race); canon's binder is the nucleated `(2,3)` winding (not a speed condition); and the substrate master clock ticks **two-to-three orders of magnitude slower** than the bound carrier. The mass spectrum supplies a direct counterexample (the muon binds at `≈207·ω_C`, above the carrier, on the same fixed-pitch lattice). And the circularity is **relabeled, not dissolved**: `{m_e, ℓ_node}` remain one input scale whichever wears the label. **The route is CLOSED.** What survives is named in §3; the open successor is named in §4.

## §1 The route that was proposed (and why it was seductive)

- **The binding principle.** "A trap can only hold content that is slower than the trap agent. Transverse waves can therefore only be bound by `c_bulk` (the fastest channel)." Seductive because it reads as a clean dispersion-relation gate, and it correctly identifies that *some* channel must out-run the trapped field.
- **The cash-out.** "`c/ℓ_node = ω_Compton` is an *exact equality*, so the electron is the unique bound state whose carrier coincides with the lattice sampling rate. Its rest mass is then the **readout** of that sampling rate, not an independent input — which dissolves the standing `ℓ_node`-vs-`m_e` circularity flag." Seductive because it promises to convert a known input-scale degree of freedom into an emergent quantity for free, by re-reading an existing identity as a measurement.

The seduction is the same shape as the α-"1/4" coincidence-magnet (precedent doc §5): an existing **identity** (`c/ℓ_node = ω_C`) is re-narrated as a **derivation**. §2 shows the arithmetic does not survive the unit-check and the mechanism does not survive canon.
