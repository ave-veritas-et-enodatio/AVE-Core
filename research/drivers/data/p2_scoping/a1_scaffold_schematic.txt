3. BOX S1 — WHAT THE INTERIOR SEES LOOKING **OUT** AT EACH SCAFFOLD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    rho = 0   ANECHOIC.  Z_s = Z_b exactly (the bond's OWN graded impedance).
              The interior cannot tell the scaffold from an infinite continuation
              of its own medium, except that the continuation carries no s_hat.
              No cavity is created. NO POLES on the real-theta axis.
              CONSEQUENCE: a source-idle solution here must be confined by the
              MEDIUM, not by the boundary. This is the discriminating scaffold.

    rho = -1  A SHORT (Gamma_s = -1).  Z_s = 0.  This is the literal reading of
              "source-terminated = ideal AC voltage source, zero source impedance".
              It makes the cut plane a MIRROR. Two mirrors -> a closed lossless
              cavity -> the region has eigenmodes for purely geometric reasons.
              MEASURED (cold, uniform S=1, 400-point theta sweep):
                 10 poles in theta in (0, pi); peak transfer 143.6 vs a
                 matched-scaffold maximum of 3.28.

    rho = +1  AN OPEN (Gamma_s = +1).  Z_s = infinity.  This is the literal
              reading of "injection-lock = weak source through a large series
              impedance".  It is ALSO a mirror, of the opposite sign.
              MEASURED (same sweep): 24 poles; peak transfer 102.5.

    I_u       NO PORT AT ALL.  An ideal current source in parallel with the
              junction adds zero admittance: the junction's own sum_k Y_k is
              unchanged, so the scatter S_ij is unchanged, so M is unchanged.
              There is nothing for a wave to reflect from.
              (An ideal VOLTAGE source at a node would instead clamp V_u and
               short the whole junction out — the destructive dual.)

4. BOX S2 — THE PORT-SPACE MODE SPLIT AT A z=3 VERTEX  (DERIVED-HERE, exact)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    S = (2/sum Y) * 1 Y^T - I     acting on the z incident-port phasors.

        eigenvector          eigenvalue      circuit reading
        -----------------    ----------      ---------------------------------
        1 = (1,1,...,1)          +1          COMMON mode  -> an OPEN
        {v : sum Y_j v_j = 0}    -1          DIFFERENTIAL modes -> a SHORT
                                             (multiplicity z-1)

    MEASURED (junction_modes.py): eigs = [+1, -1, -1] for z=3 and
    [+1, -1, -1, -1] for z=4, COLD **and** GRADED, to 1e-16. S^2 = I to 1e-16.

    Single-port excitation decomposes with common-mode weight Y_0/sum Y, so

        Gamma_vertex = (Y_0/sum Y)(+1) + (1 - Y_0/sum Y)(-1)

    which at cold equal admittance is (1/z)(+1) + ((z-1)/z)(-1) = (2-z)/z = -1/3.
    MEASURED to 1e-16 against S[0,0] for z=3 and z=4, cold AND graded.

    ***  THE GRADING NEVER MOVES THE EIGENVALUES.  It moves only the mixture
         WEIGHT.  Every bit of graded-network physics lives in the re-weighting,
         not in any new reflection channel.  ***

5. BOX S3 — THE TWO THINGS CALLED "COMMON MODE"  (they are NOT the same object)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    CM-mult (the tube phase, varpi):   s_j  ->  e^{i phi} s_j     (multiplicative)
        NOT a circuit element. It is the phasor time origin — where you triggered
        the scope. M is REAL, so v -> e^{i phi} v and A_b (which reads |v|) is
        untouched. MEASURED: dA_inf = 1.6e-12, phase spread 6.2e-11 over the
        WHOLE self-consistent solve.  ==> guard 8 is discharged BY CONSTRUCTION.
        A "project out varpi" receipt CANNOT FIRE. It is not a gate.

    CM-add (the A1 breathing channel):  s_j  ->  s_j + c          (additive)
        IS a circuit condition: every boundary port driven with the same incident
        voltage = the junction's +1 eigenvector = the A1 monopole/dilatation
        drive. MEASURED: c = 0.10 moves the converged A-field by 23% relative.
        This is REAL PHYSICS in the sector the solver runs in.
        ==> projecting THIS out deletes the mass channel's drive. DO NOT.

    Common-mode choke analogy: holds mechanically for CM-add (a series element
    opaque to the no-return-path channel, transparent to differential), and
    BREAKS for CM-mult three ways — (i) no two-wire pair / no distinguished
    return on a z=3 graph, (ii) a real choke's CMRR is finite and engineered
    whereas this rejection is algebraic (common/relational-cancellation-identity),
    (iii) you cannot build a component that blocks a choice of time origin.

6. BOX S4 — THE SOURCE-IDLE CRITERION AS A BENCH MEASUREMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The three coded observables (harmonic_balance_srs.source_idle_report):

      source_amp    = max |s_hat|                     the generator's output
      exchange_amp  = max |V_ref arriving at T|       what the absorber eats
      r_auto        = ||e^{i th} v - M_full v|| / ||v||   BARKHAUSEN RESIDUAL

    P_in - P_out is IDENTICALLY ZERO at any converged lossless steady state.
    MEASURED: P_net = -2.0e-11 on P_in = P_out = 2.458.  It is a convergence
    receipt, not a physics observable.

    What a bench technician actually reads:

      NOT "the ammeter reads zero".
      IT IS  "the ammeter reads zero WHILE the tank voltmeter reads full scale"
                    ==>  the RATIO  drive / response  ->  0.

      On a VNA: the driven transfer |v|/|s| diverges — an infinitely tall,
      infinitely narrow resonance. The autonomous mode is a POLE of the driven
      problem: (e^{i th} I - M_FF) becomes singular.

    MEASURED trap: sweeping the drive 0.30 -> 0.01 (30x),
        source_amp    tracks the drive exactly    0.30 -> 0.01
        A_max         tracks it too               0.547 -> 0.019   (de-rails!)
        source_amp/||v||  is PINNED at            0.0687 -> 0.0676
    An ABSOLUTE idle threshold is bought by turning the drive down, which
    de-rails the core at the same rate. The ratio is the scale-free reading,
    and it says plainly: NO POLE ANYWHERE NEAR these tones on this net.
