## What the solver is

Phasor-domain Kirchhoff fixed point on the graded srs TLM network: posit a tone set {θ_m} (rad/step; harmonic balance posits tones, not tube phases — epic guard 8), solve

> e^{iθ_m} v_m = M(S) v_m + sources,  M(S) = C · blockdiag(S_u),  S_ij = 2Y_j/ΣY_k − δ_ij,  Y = Y₀/√S(A)

with the S-field a self-consistent unknown read from the DP-1 cycle-average envelope of the tone phasors (default C-state arm: A_b² = Σ_m |v_m,b|²/2 per yield²; the cross-tone COMBINE rule is derived — distinct canonical tone lines average out — while the NORMALIZATION is an OPEN fork on **two** independent axes, the arm (C-state vs full-tank) and the aggregation (per-bond vs canon's per-node); see the caveats and the G2 freeze list). No time axis; no damping device; Ax3 untouched by construction — the graded scatter conserves the Y-weighted energy exactly (tested to 1e-12), and the only sink is a declared matched termination (a boundary condition, checkpoint-10 class, never a bulk loss term). Source-idle machinery measures scaffold-removability: source amplitude, scaffold exchange, the autonomous defect r_auto = ‖e^{iθ}v − M_full v‖/‖v‖, and the power ledger — all computed, thresholds always caller-declared.

## The coordinate map (phase-space-coordinate-check, declared)

The measured Class-C Γ(A) ([`2026-08-24_engine-gamma-meanstest_result.md`](2026-08-24_engine-gamma-meanstest_result.md)) is the **time-window-isolated front-face echo** of a pulse. A steady state has no time axis, so the same physical quantity is reached by a different isolation: the interface is measured as a linear two-port in the single-Bloch-mode basis — fit traveling waves on both sides, repeat with the absorbing load at ≥3 positions, and solve b = Γa + T′d, c = Ta + Γ′d for the interface's own (Γ, T, Γ′, T′). **Γ with the far side matched (d = 0) is exactly the front-face quantity the time-windowed measurement reports.** The two-port model's validity is a computed overdetermination residual, never an assumption. This de-embedding is also what removes the scaffold's own artifact: a bond-matched termination is an imperfect absorber of the Bloch wave — the committed receipt `gate2.cold_single_load_gamma_raw` records the raw single-load |Γ| at A=0 (the artifact-only composite, ~0.10 per load plane at θ=0.15), and single-solve Γ readings without de-embedding carry a load-reflection contamination of that order (at finite A the raw reading is a complex composite of the interface and load reflections, not an additive magnitude bias). Sign convention mapped to the measurement's signed-real one (Γ < 0 = polarity-inverted echo); the solver's Γ phase is a computed receipt.

**The Γ-phase deficit is sub-cell-sized and NOT adjudicated here — read this before comparing phases or Re(Γ)** *(added 2026-08-25, adversarial round 2, as a REFERENCE-PLANE mechanism claim; **DEMOTED in round 3** — the mechanism was asserted past its evidence and the note's own receipts contradict the pure-offset reading. Before round 2 the note said "≈ π" and left a 9° deficit unexplained.)* `gamma_at_plane` returns Γ = (b/a)·e^{2ikx_ref} referenced at the **nominal** interface plane x_ref = x_I·a_cell. A reference-plane shift Δx rotates arg Γ by exactly 2kΔx and leaves |Γ| **exactly invariant** — that is an identity of the de-embedding code, not an empirical claim, and it holds whatever the deficit turns out to be. The committed gate-2 phases are 2.9746–2.9881 rad, i.e. a deficit from π of 0.1670–0.1535 rad (9.6°–8.8°); at the cold-side k = θ√3 = 0.2598076 rad/cell that is 2kΔx with **Δx = 0.295–0.321 cells** — sub-cell, the scale of the lattice's own bond x-extent (max |b_dx| = 0.25 cells, measured).

**What that does and does not establish.** Writing the deficit as 2kΔx is *not* a discriminator: **any** phase deficit can be written as 2kΔx for some Δx, so the conversion is arithmetic, not evidence. The available discriminator is whether the implied Δx is **constant**, since a geometric reference-plane offset (x_ref = x_I·a_cell in `gamma_at_plane`) is A-independent by construction. It is not. Recomputed per point from the committed `gamma_solver_phase` at the fixed cold-side k (the incident side is cold at every A, so k does not move): Δx = 0.29531 (A=0.3), 0.29591, 0.29674, 0.29791, 0.29958, 0.30213, 0.30401, 0.30672, 0.30865, 0.31135, 0.31432, 0.31726, **0.32145 (A=0.99)** — strictly monotone in A, 13/13, +8.9% end to end, tracking saturation. So the measured deficit is **consistent with** a sub-cell reference-plane offset *plus* an A-dependent component that a pure offset cannot produce; the two are **not discriminated by this run**, and the round-2 instruction to read the deficit "not as a residual reactance" is **withdrawn** — nothing here rules a residual reactance in or out. Discriminating it needs a receipt this stage does not have (e.g. the same sweep at a second θ, where a geometric Δx must hold fixed while a reactive term rotates with k). Routed as an open observation, not a Stage-2 claim.

**The load-bearing consequence is unaffected, because it rests on the code identity alone:** |Γ| (what this gate compares, and what the measurement reports) is reference-plane invariant; Re(Γ) and the raw phase are **not**. A future lane that compares Re(Γ) or arg Γ instead will see ~6× worse deviations and must not attribute them to interface physics without first fixing the reference plane.
