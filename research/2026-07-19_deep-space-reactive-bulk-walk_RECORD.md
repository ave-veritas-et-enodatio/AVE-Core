### (b) The three-option walk

**[orchestrator-walk]** With the resistor flagged, three moves were on the table:

1. **DEMOTE** — the prose asserts a sub-yield **bulk** `Re(Z)` resistor; Ax3 forbids exactly that ("*any apparent loss must be a boundary-radiation or mode-conversion channel, **never a bulk resistive one***", `eq_axiom_3.tex:24`). Demote the mechanism; the magnitude that rides on it (the `1.04` TW, the stall-band positions) does not survive as a bulk-dissipation result.
2. **RE-READ-AS-WAKE** — reinterpret the "stall" as an Ax3-legal **radiative/mode-conversion leak** (a Cherenkov/Mach-type wake that becomes a *propagating* mode above a threshold speed). This is the audit's own item-89 re-read (ii): rewrite "Joule heating/dissipates" → "mode-conversion decoherence into lattice reactive modes." But deep-space slow matter sits ~20 orders below the lattice band edge, so **no wake exists there** — the wake re-read rescues only an above-threshold regime, not the sub-band deep-space claim.
3. **DEFEND** — keep the bulk resistor. Rejected: it contradicts both Ax3 (`eq_axiom_3.tex:24`) and the corpus's own `06:203` "0 Watts, Regime I, lossless."

### (c) ★ THE RULING — the deep-space bulk coupling is lossless/pure-reactance; effects live in frequency passbands

**[Grant-verbatim]** `[sic]`:

> "it rings but i think theres a bulk reaction from the lattice that makes it lossless/pure reactance, and that there dofferent passpa danof frequencies for effects, like the rings of saturn vs electron orbitals"

**[orchestrator-walk] — reading of the ruling (two clauses).**

- **Clause 1 — "a bulk reaction from the lattice that makes it lossless/pure reactance."** The bulk *does* react to a moving slow body ("it rings"), but the reaction is a **bulk reaction that returns the ringing energy** — purely reactive, no resistive stall, no bulk Joule. The energy that goes into the bulk on approach comes back out; there is no `Re(Z)` sink. This is **Option 1 (demote the resistor)** plus a **positive replacement**: the bulk coupling is real and reactive, not absent.
- **Clause 2 — "different passband of frequencies for effects, like the rings of Saturn vs electron orbitals."** Effects are **band-structured**: which motions couple, and where structure appears, is set by a **frequency passband** — a coupling that resonates at specific frequencies and is otherwise sub-band/evanescent. Saturn's rings and electron orbitals are the two-scale illustration of the *same* passband grammar: structure sits at the resonances of the coupling, not smeared across a resistive continuum.

### (d) THE ORCHESTRATOR FORMALIZATION (orchestrator-walk; ratified-in-substance by the ruling's own words)

Everything in this subsection is **[orchestrator-walk]**. It is ratified *in substance* by the ruling's "lossless/pure reactance" (clause 1) and "passband of frequencies" (clause 2); the *specific mechanism names* (d'Alembert, Cherenkov) are candidate-framing where they have no canonical home. Each anchor is labeled.

- **(i) Bulk reaction = ADDED MASS / d'Alembert's paradox.** A body in steady motion through an **ideal (lossless) medium** feels a purely reactive **added-mass** back-force — a virtual inductance that stores kinetic energy on acceleration and returns it on deceleration — and **zero steady drag** (d'Alembert's paradox: no dissipation ⇒ no net force opposing uniform motion). This is the mechanical dual of Ax3-lossless: `eq_axiom_3.tex:24` (the bond-LC tank "stores and returns energy but does not dissipate it"). *("Added mass" is used in the corpus's vortex sector — `hollow-vortex-binding.md`, `temporal-saturation-regime-classifier.md` — as a related reactive-inertia concept; cited only as prior use, not as a canonical derivation of this claim.)*
- **(ii) Deep-space slow matter sits ~20 orders below the lattice band edge ⇒ deeply evanescent/sub-band ⇒ reactive by construction.** The lattice band edge is `f_max = c/(π ℓ_node)` (`translation-circuit.md:154,:353`, the Casimir/below-cutoff-waveguide row). A slow body's coupling frequencies are ~20 OOM below it: **below cutoff**, the waveguide row's "long-`λ` modes ... excluded/**evanescent**" regime — the field "enters a region it cannot propagate through" and decays evanescently (`δ↔Γ` conjugate, `translation-circuit.md:556`). A sub-band, evanescent coupling is **reactive by construction**: it cannot radiate, so it cannot dissipate — it can only store-and-return. This is the field-theoretic statement of (i), and it is exactly the `A_gm ≪ 0.121` Regime-I operating point of `06:203`.
- **(iii) Drag turns on only at a Cherenkov/Mach-type radiation threshold.** Real drag requires the wake to become a **propagating** mode — the vacuum analog of Cherenkov emission (source outruns the medium's phase speed) or a Mach cone. Below threshold: evanescent, reactive, zero drag. Above threshold: a radiative **port** opens (Ax3-legal — the loss is at a radiation channel, not in the bulk). Deep-space slow matter is far below threshold, so the demoted "stall" cannot be an above-threshold wake either. This is the honest home of the audit's Option-2 re-read: it lives **above** threshold, not at the sub-band deep-space operating point.
- **(iv) Replacement prediction shape = structure-at-resonances (Kirkwood-gap grammar), NOT a smooth `∝v` stall.** If the coupling is band-structured (clause 2), detritus organizes at the **resonances** of the coupling — discrete, band-structured features — not smeared into a stall-band at a single impedance isocline. The corpus **already carries this grammar, reactive and closed**: `kirkwood-gaps-cavity-modes.md:12` models the asteroid-belt gaps at mean-motion resonances as "**cavity modes in the gravitational impedance field** ... all five major gaps (4:1, 3:1, 5:2, 7:3, 2:1) ... reproduced to `<0.3%` error." The Kirkwood-cavity-mode account is the reactive structure-at-resonances sibling; the demoted "topological Joule stall" account is its dissipative competitor. The ruling adjudicates **for** the reactive-resonance grammar.

## §3 — The discriminating handles

**[orchestrator-walk]** — the two observables that separate the demoted mechanism from the ruling.

1. **Zero-drag-below-threshold (`v`-independence vs the dead `∝v` stall).** A reactive added-mass exerts **no steady drag** (d'Alembert). So a deep-space probe crossing the Oort transition shows **no velocity-dependent stall** — `v`-independence. This directly **flips the vol4 boundary-trapping falsification target** (`clm-h55fy1`, the Pioneer-transit "sudden spike in transit drag" test, `boundary-trapping-test.md:23`): the demoted mechanism predicts a drag-spike scaling with transit velocity; the ruling predicts **no energy-shedding drag spike** (at most a reactive reflection at the impedance step, which does not thermalize the body). A measured, `v`-scaling drag-spike would falsify the *reactive* ruling; its absence falsifies the *resistive* stall. This is the KEEP-BOTH discriminator the demotion opens.
2. **Structure-at-resonances (band-structured, discrete — NOT a smooth stall-band).** The replacement predicts accumulation at the **resonances** of the coupling (mean-motion resonances / cavity modes), discrete and band-structured — already realized reactively at `<0.3%` by `kirkwood-gaps-cavity-modes.md:12`. The demoted mechanism predicts a smooth stall-band at a single `τ_yield` isocline. The two make **different spatial predictions** for where detritus sits.
