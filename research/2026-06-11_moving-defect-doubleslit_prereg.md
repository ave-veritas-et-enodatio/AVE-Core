## §0 — THE FORK BEING DISCRIMINATED (Grant-gated in canon — this design ANSWERS it, does NOT assume it)

The corpus carries **two readings of the matter-wave / pilot field** that predict **different slit physics**. Both are canonical-on-`main`; the fork is Grant-gated (unresolved). This prereg is built to discriminate them, not to pick one.

**Reading T — TRANSVERSE-INDUCTIVE WAKE.** `ohmic-decoherence-born.md:11` (verbatim, re-grepped `origin/main` this session):
> In the Double Slit Experiment, the topological defect (particle) passes through Slit A, but the continuous **transverse inductive wake** generated by its motion passes through *both* slits. The particle deterministically navigates the resulting **transverse ponderomotive gradients** ($\mathbf{F} \propto \nabla|\Psi|^2$) into the quantized standing-wave troughs.

Consolidated in `double-slit-ee-mapping.md` §2/§3: the wake is "a continuous **transverse inductive wake** — a ponderomotive pressure field $\propto\nabla|\Psi|^2$ … low-amplitude … passes through **both** apertures," **near-field, regime-distinct from the far-field $\tau^{far}_{zx}$ dark-wake.** The pilot rides the **shear / EM channel** ($w$ / shear; native $c_T=1$).

**Reading B — LONGITUDINAL-BULK COMPRESSION.** `de-broglie-standing-wave.md:50` (verbatim, re-grepped `origin/main` this session):
> By contrast, an electron ($0_1$ unknot) is a massive topological defect … It does not travel as a shear wave at $c_0$; instead, **its motion displaces the lattice, generating longitudinal acoustic pressure waves governed by the vacuum's Bulk Modulus.**

With (`:52`) $n_{acoustic}(r)\propto 1/\sqrt{E-eV(r)}$ — the matter wave is a **compression field on the bulk modulus**, riding $c_{bulk}$ (the engine's longitudinal Cosserat $u$ channel, $c_L=\sqrt{10/3}\approx1.826$, **NOT frozen by the V-sector saturation $S$** — `motion_stability_bemf_longitudinal_probe.py` header, verified).

**The channel-ledger LEANS BULK.** `2026-06-10_matter-as-vapor-locked-pump_framing.md` §11.3.4 (Grant-ratified framing, verbatim, re-grepped `origin/main` this session):
> The **pilot wave = the latent store in motion**, with $m_ec^2\cdot\alpha$ the sloshing (reactive) fraction.

and §11.1 L2: "longitudinal = the medium's STATE … BULK-$K$ (the volumetric/density wave)." The framing places the pilot in the **longitudinal/bulk** grade. This is a *lean*, not a closure — the leaf is `CONSISTENCY-CLASS framing` and the fork stays Grant-gated. **This prereg does not inherit the lean; it tests it.**

**Why they predict different slit physics:** the bulk pilot rides $c_{bulk}$ and diffracts as a **compression field** ($\operatorname{div}u\neq0$, longitudinal); the transverse-inductive wake rides the **shear/EM channels** ($w$, $\tau_{zx}$, $\operatorname{curl}\neq0$). The discriminator is **the channel of the field that (a) floods both slits and (b) forms the downstream fringes** — see §5.

## §1 — TARGET, and what the two prior sims did/didn't settle

### §1.1 — The question
When a **self-consistently translating localized defect** transits ONE slit of a two-slit wall, **which field channel** — longitudinal-bulk ($\operatorname{div}u$ / $V_{inc}$ compression, $c_L$) or transverse-shear ($w$ / $\tau_{zx}$, $c_T$) — (a) **floods BOTH slits**, and (b) **forms the downstream interference fringes at the de-Broglie period $\lambda_{dB}=h/p$** computed forward from the defect's *measured* momentum $p$? The **channel** is the fork discriminator; the **period** is the validity gate (real de-Broglie pilot vs. moving-source blur artifact).

### §1.2 — sim-1 (Born-from-clicks, 2026-06-08) — settled the SCREEN, not the PILOT
`research/2026-06-08_ave-double-slit_born-from-clicks_result.md`: Born rule $p\propto|E|^2$ assembled from threshold-crossing detector statistics; **NO defect** — a field (canonical FDTD K4-TLM Maxwell, transverse $E_z$) + an Axiom-4 saturation detector. It settles *how the screen reads* the field; it says nothing about *which channel the pilot rides* because there is no moving defect and only the transverse field is excited. **Not a fork test.**

### §1.3 — sim-2 (stationary-source K4-TLM, 2026-06-04) — the BLUR lesson (the load-bearing design constraint)
`research/2026-06-04_k4tlm-double-slit-darkwake-result.md` realized the **transverse-wake** mechanism on the real K4-TLM engine, but with an **IMPOSED defect trajectory** (depicted overlay; CP5: a $\Gamma=-1$ core has $c_{local}\to0$ and cannot free-propagate). Its §4 integrator-time finding (flag-don't-fix, verbatim):
> A **moving** emission point BLURS the time-averaged fringes (two-slit V ~ 0.62, spacing ~12 cells) because the K4 group velocity is finite, so the wake from successive source positions arrives at the screen with different phase.

Clean-fringe baseline (no observer): **V = 0.920**, fringe spacing 36.0 cells (vs 26.7 forward-predicted; near-field). Single-slit floor V = 0.724. **The lesson: naive emitter-translation is NOT the design.** A defect realized as a *moving source* smears its own fringes. The defect must be a **self-consistent translating wave packet** (the motion-lock under transport): the v6 product translating, or the breather boosted — see §4. This is the single most load-bearing constraint on the build and is encoded as capability-gate C1/C3 (§6).

## §5 — OBSERVABLES (per-channel, frozen before any run)

All maps PML-excluded (`PML+8 ≤ idx < N−PML−8`); shell/structured packets sampled at **density peaks** (top-K $|field|^2$), NOT centroid (a shell's centroid is the empty middle — A-Rule-10). All numbers regenerate from the driver; constants from `ave.core.constants` only.

- **obs-1 — Per-channel field map at the SLIT PLANE (the both-slit flood test).** When the defect transits ONE slit, record at the slit-plane row, separately:
  - longitudinal: $\operatorname{div}u$ and $V_{inc}$ amplitude at each aperture;
  - transverse: $w$/shear and $\tau_{zx}$ (via `DarkWakeObserver`) at each aperture.
  - **Discriminating quantity: the slit-balance ratio per channel** = (amplitude at the *non-transited* slit) / (amplitude at the *transited* slit). A channel that "floods both slits" has ratio → 1; a channel confined to the transited slit has ratio → 0.
- **obs-2 — Downstream fringe pattern, PER CHANNEL.** At the screen row, $|field|^2(y)$ computed *separately* for the longitudinal ($\operatorname{div}u$/$V_{inc}$) and transverse ($w$/$\tau_{zx}$) channels. The channel that shows two-beam fringes is the **fringe-forming** channel; the channel that shows only the single-slit envelope is not.
- **obs-3 — Fringe period vs $\lambda_{dB}=h/p$ (the validity gate).** Measure the defect's momentum $p$ in-channel ($\int\rho\,u\,dV$ for the bulk reading via $c_L$ kinematics; the K4 core Poynting for the transverse reading via $c_T$) and FORWARD-compute $\lambda_{dB}=h/p$ and the Fraunhofer spacing $\Delta y=\lambda_{dB}L/d$. Compare to the *observed* spacing of the fringe-forming channel. Agreement (within the sim-2 near-field correction band) ⇒ real de-Broglie pilot; disagreement / no stable spacing ⇒ blur artifact (NO-GUIDANCE / UNRESOLVED). **Coordinate discipline (§2.5): $p$ read in the same channel's kinematics as the wavelength it is compared to.**
- **obs-4 — Visibility vs the sim-2 blur baseline.** Two-slit visibility $V$ of the fringe-forming channel, compared to sim-2's clean baseline (**V=0.920**) and its moving-emitter blur (**V~0.62**). A self-consistent packet must beat the blur floor decisively ($V$ well above 0.62, toward 0.92) or capability-gate C-localize (§6) has failed. Plus the which-path arm: $V$ vs detector load $Z_{det}$ at the non-transited slit — the channel whose visibility collapses under $Z_{det}$ is the **wake-pays** channel (BOTH-ROLES signature, §9).

## §11 — pre-test-physics-check: the ONE plumber-physical question surfaced to Grant BEFORE design freeze (Rule-16 strengthening)

**The circularity in the transport drive (the load-bearing question, §4).** The only multi-channel host (VacuumEngine3D) can read the fork, but every demonstrated way to *move* the defect taints the answer: a transverse boost **pins** the core (`motion_stability` `c6613c26`, CONTRADICTS-via-PIN), and the longitudinal boost **is** a bulk-compression injection (`native_electron_propagation.py`: "curl-free longitudinal compression bias (de Broglie bulk channel) to drag the defect") — which pre-loads BULK-GUIDES by construction. Plumber-physical: *if you can only get the slug moving by pushing it with the very pressure wave you're trying to detect downstream, the gauge reads your pump, not the flow.*

**The question for Grant (gates Phase-2 build):** is a **one-shot $u_{dot}$ momentum imprint** (an initial condition, then free evolution — not a sustained pump) acceptable as "channel-neutral," on the grounds that it sets momentum and releases (the wake it then sheds is the engine's free response)? OR must the boost be a **full multi-channel Galilean/Lorentz transform of the entire bound configuration** (V, $u$, $\omega$ boosted coherently together) so that no single channel is privileged at $t=0$? The first is buildable on today's engine; the second may itself be an engine-gap. **This is the precondition: until it is answered, Phase-2 (C-neutral, §6.2) is GATED.** Flag-don't-fix: I do not pick; Grant adjudicates the physical question.
