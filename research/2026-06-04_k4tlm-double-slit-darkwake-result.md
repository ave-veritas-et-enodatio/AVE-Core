## 3. Gamma-mismatch which-path decoherence (the measurement effect)

| Configuration | screen visibility V |

| Panel B — two slits, observer at slit 2 (`Z_det = 0.9`) | **0.725** |
| single-slit reference (slit 1 only, no observer) | **0.724** |

The observer collapses the two-slit interference (V = 0.920) onto the single-slit
diffraction envelope (V = 0.724) — i.e. the Ohmic load at slit 2 thermalizes that
slit's throughput, so the screen sees only slit-1 light. This is the canonical

**Continuous, not Copenhagen-binary** (`--visibility-sweep` ->

the single-slit floor. The transition is steepest at low `Z_det` (a finer sweep

the dark-wake/Born prediction, NOT the binary all-or-nothing collapse of the

**The moving-source tension (Rule 10 integrator-time finding — surfaced, not
silently fixed).** The brief specified *"a MOVING localized transverse source
traces the electron-defect trajectory THROUGH ONE SLIT."* Integrator-time runs
showed a genuine tension on this K4-TLM slab:

- A **moving** emission point BLURS the time-averaged fringes (two-slit V ~ 0.62,
  spacing ~12 cells) because the K4 group velocity is finite, so the wake from
  successive source positions arrives at the screen with different phase. The
  pattern averages over many source geometries.
- An emitter **parked AT slit 1** illuminates only slit 1 -> a single screen lobe,
  no two-beam interference (all of two/one/observer collapse to 1 peak).
- A **pure-T_2 forward beam** is too collimated to light two separated slits from
  one emitter at all (wall slit-balance ~ 0.00).
- Only a **stable emission point on the central axis upstream** produces clean,
  symmetric two-slit fringes (V = 0.92, spacing 36 cells, 3 lobes symmetric about
  center) with a strong which-path washout (+0.195).

**Resolution (faithful to the corpus, not a rescue).** The canonical leaf says the
wake is *"generated by its motion"* but *"passes through both slits"*, and the
particle *"navigates the transverse ponderomotive gradients into the troughs."*
The physically clean realization is: the wake field is emitted from a stable
upstream point (so it is broad and phase-stable at the wall -> both slits
symmetrically lit -> clean Young fringes = the real engine measurement), while the
defect trajectory through slit 1 is rendered as an **imposed marker overlay**
(`imposed_trajectory()`) — the depicted deterministic-navigation path. The marker
does NOT drive the engine. This is consistent with the leaf (the trajectory is the
*navigation* of the particle through the ponderomotive gradients, a depicted path
overlaid on the real wake field), and it is labeled "imposed defect trajectory
(depicted)" on the figure. **This is a framing decision at the implementer/Grant
boundary** — surfaced here rather than silently chosen; an alternative faithful
build (literal moving emitter, accepting blurred fringes, or option-(ii)
free-propagation) is a separate decision.
