## §1 — FROZEN BINS (the prove-or-disprove outcome space)

The derivation resolves to exactly one of five bins. **These are frozen before any derivation is run.**

- **B1-FORCED** — a **constraint / gauge-slaving** structure is DERIVED that makes the longitudinal gravitational sector non-dynamical (determined by the instantaneous matter distribution via an *elliptic* constraint, as GR's longitudinal metric parts are pure-gauge / EM's longitudinal photon is Gauss-killed), AND that structure is shown to **govern the gravitational A1 coupling** (not merely be one of two co-carried models).
- **B2-FORCED** — the **source-side** radiative bulk (dilatation) projection **vanishes identically** for the gravitational coupling channel by a DERIVED source-structure argument (not an assumed symmetric coupling), even though a bulk branch exists.
- **B4-FORCED** — the discrete K4/srs lattice demonstrably has **NO independent propagating longitudinal (compression) branch** distinct from the shear/scalar-TL branch — OR the radiative bulk mode is **gapped** so the binary's `2Ω` is sub-gap evanescent. (If the lattice has no gapless propagating compression branch, B4 wins structurally and FLAG-A dissolves.)
- **NONE-DERIVES** — ★ all three traces fail to produce a closing mechanism ⇒ Reading A is forced ⇒ **the pulsar exclusion goes LIVE against the framework**. This is a legitimate, bankable outcome (Rule 11 honest closure). **Anti-seduction fence:** the framework WANTS a mechanism to derive; this bin must be *genuinely reachable* — if the lattice has a propagating compression branch AND the source drives it AND no constraint kills it, NONE-DERIVES wins and is banked plainly.
- **UNDETERMINED** — a candidate mechanism exists but its applicability rests on an **unforced choice** the corpus carries unresolved (e.g. which of two co-carried dynamical models governs the gravitational A1 sector). Per the discipline, an unforced choice ⇒ STOP the trace at UNDETERMINED with the fork stated; do NOT pick a side by fiat.

**Adjudication ordering rule (frozen):** a trace only earns `*-FORCED` if every step is `[derived]` or `[canon-read]` with no `[assumed]` load-bearing step. Any load-bearing `[assumed]`/unforced choice demotes the trace to UNDETERMINED (fork stated) or, if the choice's *other* branch is the radiating one, contributes toward NONE-DERIVES.

## §2 — The three traces (independent routes) + their frozen adjudication criteria

Each trace tags every step `[derived]` / `[canon-read]` / `[assumed]`. An `[assumed]` load-bearing step = STOP at UNDETERMINED with the fork stated.

### Trace B1 — constraint structure (the GR-parallel)
**Question:** does the K4/TKI structure make the longitudinal gravitational sector NON-DYNAMICAL — determined by the instantaneous matter distribution via an elliptic constraint (∇²φ = source) rather than an independently radiating d'Alembertian DOF (□φ = source)? Trace the TKI mechanical↔EM isomorphism (Ax2): EM's longitudinal photon is killed by gauge structure (Gauss); does the isomorphism CARRY that kill to the mechanical A1 sector for the gravitational coupling? **PASSES to B1-FORCED iff:** (i) a DERIVED structure (group-theoretic / gauge / constraint) makes the A1 longitudinal non-propagating, AND (ii) it is shown to govern the *gravitational* A1 coupling, AND (iii) it does not merely restate one side of an unadjudicated model fork.
**Demotes to UNDETERMINED iff:** the structure exists but its ownership of the gravitational A1 coupling is an unforced choice the corpus carries unresolved.

### Trace B4 — band structure (★the sleeper)
**Question:** does the ADJUDICATED discrete band model (`srs-band-structure.md`, `clm-bnd5rq`, arccos TL map) actually CARRY an independent longitudinal acoustic branch, or was the continuum P-wave (`√(10/3)·c`) an idealization the lattice does not realize? If the lattice has no independent gapless propagating P-branch, B4 wins structurally and FLAG-A dissolves.
**PASSES to B4-FORCED iff:** the adjudicated band survey shows NO gapless propagating compression/longitudinal Bloch branch (or a gap above `2Ω`).
**FALSIFIED (B4 does not close the port) iff:** the survey shows a gapless propagating compression branch reaching DC.

### Trace B2 — source-side projection
**Question:** does gravitating matter couple to the lattice ONLY through a shear / divergence-free projection (the bulk source term vanishing identically for the gravitational channel), even though the bulk branch exists? Trace the actual coupling operator in the master equation / trampoline canon.
**PASSES to B2-FORCED iff:** the gravitational source's radiative dilatation projection is DERIVED to vanish identically (a theorem, not an assumed symmetric coupling).
**FALSIFIED iff:** the source drives the bulk branch at quadrupole order with O(1) coupling (Reading A).
