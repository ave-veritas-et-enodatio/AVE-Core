## Sub-read (metric decomposition)

Comoving arm **passes** two of three P13 metrics:

| P13 metric | Full stack | Threshold | |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| `E_frac_interior` | 0.828 | ≥ 0.55 | PASS |
| `width_ratio` | 0.92 | ≤ 2.0 | PASS |
| `peak_retention` | ~0.000 | ≥ 0.40 | **FAIL** |

**Interpretation:** Integer node-roll **does** advance the energy centroid (~1.78 box units over 220 steps) while **width stays bounded** (~0.92×). Failure is **`peak_retention`** — node-local max amplitude collapses under repeated roll + leak clamp, even though total pocket energy fraction remains high.

This is a **measurement / gate-design** tension: `peak_amplitude` = max node RMS is ill-suited when the payload translates and smears across the sorted-axis roll. Not open-srs dispersion (width×≈3.8 on cell C).

**Smoke boost sweep:** gain monotonic in v_boost `{0.5: 0.0, 1.0: 0.14, 2.0: 0.26}` at L=8 — rail directionally consistent.
