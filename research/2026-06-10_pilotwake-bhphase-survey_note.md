## §1 H1 — one mechanism, two sector-distinct impedances (REFINED)

The pilot and the wake share the Lenz/Op14 mechanism but present **different impedances in different sectors**:

- **Pilot = bulk-acoustic reactive store** (longitudinal): [`de-broglie-standing-wave.md:50`](../manuscript/ave-kb/vol2/quantum-orbitals/ch07-quantum-mechanics/de-broglie-standing-wave.md) — the electron "displaces the lattice, generating **longitudinal acoustic pressure waves governed by the vacuum's Bulk Modulus**." The all-reactive endpoint is the quantized reactive shell $Q_{\text{reactive}} = m_e c^2\cdot\alpha$ at [`orbital-friction-paradox.md:35`](../manuscript/ave-kb/vol4/circuit-theory/ch1-vacuum-circuit-analysis/orbital-friction-paradox.md). **CANONICAL.**
- **Wake = shear-sector radiator with its OWN $R + jX$** (reactance-dominated): [`2026-06-08_rrad-l-darkwake_result.md:111-144`](../research/2026-06-08_rrad-l-darkwake_result.md) — $Z_{rad,L} = R_{rad,L} + jX_L$ (line 111), propagating ($R_{rad,L}>0$) but **reactance-dominated (high-Q)** (lines 140–144; radiation $Q \gg 1$, "consistent with the corpus electron picture $Q = 1/\alpha = 137$").
- **The $R/jX$ split lives INSIDE the wake**, mode-level — it does NOT split pilot-vs-wake into real-vs-reactive. This is the anti-identification ratified in **PR #144** (MERGED — "Dark-wake thruster $R_{rad,L}$: Phases 1–5") §6; the substantive precedent is the $\Sigma_{\text{near}} + \tau^{\text{far}}_{zx}$ unification that closed **NEGATIVE** at [`dark-back-reaction-taxonomy.md:54`](../manuscript/ave-kb/common/dark-back-reaction-taxonomy.md) ("Common ancestry in the kernel does not make $\Sigma_{\text{near}}$ and $\tau^{\text{far}}_{zx}$ one field … exactly as two distinct circuits built from the same component law are not one circuit").

## §5 The two-objects proof (phase ≠ regime)

$r = A/A_c$ (the saturation ratio / regime coordinate) is **defined only WITHIN the crystal**. But the **pre-geodesic plasma predates any crystal**: [`cosmic-axes-and-frames-glossary.md:73`](../manuscript/ave-kb/common/cosmic-axes-and-frames-glossary.md) — "the inside region is our universe's pre-crystallization phase (supercooled pre-geodesic plasma)." A phase with **no regime defined**. Therefore phase and regime are two objects, not one — the conflation in §4 is a presentation defect, not a physics identity.
