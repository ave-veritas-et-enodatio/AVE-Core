## §2 — THE STATE-DEPENDENT OPERATOR + the Newton/self-consistent solve + the second-difference `K_tan` observable (the F2 + F3 root repair)

**★The state-dependent operator (frozen — this is what makes the instrument NONLINEAR, the F2 root).** The bond effective stiffness tensor is now a functional of the current displacement state:

Because `Φ_eff` depends on `u`, `K(u)·u = b_source` is NONLINEAR — a bond in tension (`T>0`) stiffens transversely, a bond in compression (`T<0`) softens toward buckling (`k_shear,eff→0`), exactly the #779 anisotropic vessel-state remap and the `axiom-register.md:193` load-response sign rule.

**★The Newton / self-consistent solve (frozen — outer re-evaluation of `T(u)`, inner CG on the tangent operator).**
