## §1 — THE FRAME: one kernel, three unknown arguments

The kernel in question is the Axiom-4 saturation kernel `S(A) = √(1−A²)`; the object under the walk is its **argument** `A`, not its shape. The shape is settled (an L2-norm theorem, count-preserving; see the axiom-register). The open physics is: **what physical variable is `A`, per sector** — and whether it is one principle wearing three sector-dresses or three unrelated keyings.

**The three data points the walk must fit:**

- **μ-side — BANKED (a consistency leg, not a discriminator).** Route C keys the vacuum μ-grade on **circulation** (`∮E·dl` / `∂_tB`), not on flux; a static **B** delivers zero circulation → `S_B = √(1−A_I²)` with `A_I = 0` → transparent. Both live ε-keys re-derive it under one off-line/reactive principle: `research/2026-07-10_x41-radiative-scoping-why_RESULT.md:276-286` (STEP 4′, "**the µ-side consistency leg — both keys re-derive Route C; a check, not a discriminator**"). So the μ-side is *consistent under every candidate* — it constrains none of them. Banked, not load-bearing for the fork.

  - **canon** — charge-keyed on the **full `|E|`, DC included** (`A_V = V/V_yield = |E|/E_yield`, no Helmholtz decomposition): `research/2026-07-10_x41-radiative-scoping-why_RESULT.md:152-154` (the confirmed #547 config-fact; `[DERIVED: CHARGE-KEYED]`);

The walk is a search for that single principle; §2 lays out three candidates, §3 supplies the two physical facts that cut between them.

## §2 — THE THREE CANDIDATE PRINCIPLES (walked as pictures)

Each principle is a one-line answer to "what does the drive have to *do* to the node before the kernel counts it." Walked as physical pictures, not formalism.

- **P1 — STRESS / conjugate-variable keying (the standing canon; the bench null hypothesis).** Each reactive element keys on **its own stored conjugate variable**: an inductor `L` keys on its current `I` (stored in `½LI²`), a capacitor `C` keys on its voltage `V` (stored in `½CV²`) — **DC included**, because a held DC conjugate is genuine stored energy pressing on the element. This is the ε-side canon (full-`|E|`, DC included) generalized. On the bench it is the **null hypothesis**: every biased varactor detunes under held DC, and P1 says the vacuum node does too.

- **P2 — FLOW / propagating-content keying (the K1 ∧ K2 family).** The kernel keys only on **content that is going somewhere** — the on-line traveling/standing wave, the radiative `R_rad`, the transverse projection that survives Helmholtz decomposition off the static longitudinal piece. **Statics off-line buy nothing;** only waves load the kernel. K1 (transverse `|E_T|`) and K2 (on-line/`R_rad`) are two dialects of this family. Its burden is denying the canon DC handle (see §3a).

- **P3 — TEXTURE / gauge-observable keying (the #547 gauge-observability RIDER, promoted to a principle).** The kernel keys on **what a local observer cannot gauge away**: gradients, tides, circulations. *Uniform* anything — uniform potential, uniform field, uniform velocity — is invisible to the kernel because it can be transformed to zero at the node by choice of local frame. This began as a rider inside K2 (the non-uniform-static reduction: `research/2026-07-10_x41-radiative-scoping-why_RESULT.md:37-56`, K2 "reduces to the #547 gauge-observability RIDER"); the walk promotes it to a stand-alone candidate because it is the only one of the three that speaks natively to the gravity sector (§3b: the equivalence principle *is* gauge-unobservability of a uniform `g`).

**The tell to watch for:** P1 and P3 give *opposite* answers on a uniform held field (P1: loads — it is stored stress; P3: transparent — it is gauge-removable). P2 sits with P3 on the ε side (statics off-line) but has no equivalence-principle content, so it says nothing special about gravity. The two cuts in §3 are exactly the experiments that separate P1 from P3.
