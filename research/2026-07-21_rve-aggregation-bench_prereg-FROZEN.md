**★The discriminator (frozen).**
$$\boxed{\ r_Z \;\equiv\; \frac{Z_{\rm bulk,eff}}{Z_0} \;=\; \sqrt{\frac{K_{\rm eff}}{K_0}\cdot\frac{\rho_{\rm eff}}{\rho_0}}\ \ \text{at the space-filling end }\varphi=\varphi_{\rm sf}\ }$$

The **short-vs-open sign competition**: `K_eff` CRASHES (the shells act as pressure-release cavities in series — the bubbly-liquid crash) versus `ρ_eff` RISES (the cages carry the mass). **★The two ρ_eff readings (frozen; the pre-test-physics-check fork).**
- **PRIMARY — engine-native (acoustic inertia):** `ρ_eff/ρ_0 = N_node(RVE)/N_node(RVE) = 1` (the cages are stiffness grades; the lattice node inertia is uniform and unchanged). ⇒ `r_Z = √(K_eff/K_0)`. This is what the lattice's actual compression-wave inertia carries.
- **COMPANION — canon mass-loading:** `mass = trapped acoustic compression` (`master-equation.md:20`) ⇒ each cage carries additional inertia ⇒ `ρ_eff/ρ_0 = 1 + β·φ`, `β` a DISCLOSED loading factor (frozen sweep `β ∈ {0, 1, 3}`; `β=0` reproduces the primary). Reported as a companion; the ontology (is this acoustic inertia?) routed to Grant.

**★The bubbly-liquid reference form (frozen — the FORM the data tests, NOT a fit target).** Textbook underwater-acoustics anchor. For a suspension of pressure-release cavities at volume fraction `φ` in a matrix of bulk modulus `K_0`, the low-frequency effective bulk modulus is the **Reuss / Wood iso-stress average**:
$$\frac{1}{K_{\rm eff}} = \frac{1-\varphi}{K_0} + \frac{\varphi}{K_{\rm cav}} \xrightarrow{K_{\rm cav}\to 0} K_{\rm eff} \approx \frac{K_{\rm cav}}{\varphi} \to 0\quad(\text{catastrophic crash at any }\varphi).$$
The mass density is the arithmetic (Voigt) average `ρ_eff ≈ (1-φ)ρ_0 + φ ρ_cav`. The **contrast form** is the Voigt/parallel bound `K_eff = (1-φ)K_0 + φ K_cav` (matrix carries the load in parallel ⇒ `K` holds ⇒ NOT a crash). The bench measures which bound the srs soft-shell cage array follows — the substrate decides via the shell CONNECTIVITY (Reuss when the soft shells percolate the load path; Voigt when the stiff matrix carries it). ## §3 — EFFECTIVE-MODULI DEFINITIONS + static-homogenization EXTRACTION + bound-character disclosure

**★The extraction (frozen — kinematic uniform boundary condition, KUBC).** On `cce.build_finite_srs(L)`, partition nodes into a BOUNDARY shell `B` (within `bw = 1.5` lattice units of any outer face of the `L³` cell) and INTERIOR `I`. Impose the affine macroscopic strain on the boundary: `u_b = E·(x_b − x_c)`, `x_c` the cell center. Relax the interior to STATIC equilibrium `K_II u_I = −K_IB u_B` by matrix-free conjugate gradient (the operator is `apply_K(w) = −forces(w, Φ, bi, bj, N)`, SPD on the interior with Dirichlet `B`; the RHS is `forces(u₀)_I` for `u₀` = the affine field with `u_I=0`). Compute the total elastic energy `U(E) = ½ Σ_bonds du·Φ·du` at equilibrium, AND the interior-core energy `U_core(E)` (bonds whose midpoint lies in the central `L/2`-cube, to reduce the KUBC boundary-layer over-stiffening bias — the reported verdict uses `U_core`).

**★Effective-moduli ratios (frozen; dimensionless — the ρ_N-style ratio trick).** Because the imposed affine boundary field is IDENTICAL in the caged and uncaged arms, the ratio of core energies at the SAME strain mode `E` and SAME geometry equals the effective-modulus ratio (geometric prefactors cancel):
- **Hydrostatic mode** `E_hydro = ε·diag(1,1,1)` (traceful): `K_eff/K_0 ≡ U_core^caged(E_hydro)/U_core^uncaged(E_hydro)`.
- **Pure-shear mode** `E_shear = ε·[[0,1,0],[1,0,0],[0,0,0]]` (traceless): `G_eff/G_0 ≡ U_core^caged(E_shear)/U_core^uncaged(E_shear)`.
Frozen strain amplitude `ε = 1e-3` (linear regime; amplitude-linearity checked at `ε ∈ {1e-4,1e-3,1e-2}`). `Z_bulk,eff/Z_0 = √((K_eff/K_0)·(ρ_eff/ρ_0))` per §1.

**★Internal validation (frozen — Leg 0).** The cold uncaged `K_0`, `G_0` (absolute, from `U/(½V⟨ε²⟩)`) must be CONSISTENT with the Bloch speeds from `run_c2_speeds(ρ*, k_s)`: `c_P² = (K + 4/3 G)/ρ`, `c_S² = G/ρ` (frozen tol `≤ 0.20` — the finite-KUBC-box tolerance). **★Bound-character disclosure (frozen — the honest scope).** KUBC gives a STIFF (upper-bound-class) effective modulus; the interior-core measure reduces but does not eliminate the boundary-layer bias.
