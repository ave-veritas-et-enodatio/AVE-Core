- **Claim-class tag:** `ClaimClass.CONSISTENCY` or

  `ClaimClass.EMERGENCE` labeled as node genesis.

(no new engine):

  cavity signature **persist** above the documented null floor?

  drive-off localization above floor across the declared battery, (A)-only

  remanence / cavity physics).
