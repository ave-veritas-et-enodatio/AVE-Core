## §1 — Pauli: what canon has, and the shape of the open half

**Two statements get conflated.** The **antisymmetry** (the many-body state
changes sign under exchange) and the **exclusion** (two identical fermions
cannot share a state). The second is a corollary of the first.

**Canon's position, verbatim** (`form-deriving-value-importing.md`:220): the
exchange antisymmetry is *"the antisymmetric **connection** (σ = −I, FR
two-loop braid, #315) — DERIVED at PEER-ahead"*, but *"**the dynamical
selection of the antisymmetric sector (exclusion enforcement) stays OPEN**."*
The π₁ reason: π₁ of the Cosserat configuration space is ℤ₂ and
Hom(ℤ₂, U(1)) has TWO elements — boson and fermion — so π₁ **admits both and
forces neither**; canon imports the selection.

**Canon also already carries the repulsion side in operator form:** Op9
*"Pauli-level overlap → impedance divergence, Γ → −1"* and Op4's pairwise
potential `Z(r) = Z₀/(1−(d_sat/r)²)^{1/4}` with a Pauli regime. So Grant's
capacity reading is **not a competitor to the FR-braid treatment — it is a
candidate for the open ENFORCEMENT half.**

**★ The reframe that makes energetic exclusion legitimate here [WALK].** In
QM, exclusion is kinematic (the wavefunction is identically zero), not
energetic — the usual objection to any "Pauli as repulsion" story. But AVE is
a deterministic field substrate, not a wavefunction theory, and **for a
classical field "that configuration does not exist" and "that configuration
costs infinite energy" are the same statement.** The kinematic/energetic
distinction is an artifact of the formalism where states are wavefunctions; it
does not survive translation to a medium. So exclusion-by-impedance-divergence
is not a weaker kind of exclusion here — it is the only kind available.

**The spin-dependence problem, and a canon-supported repair [WALK].**
Exclusion is spin-dependent: two opposite-spin electrons share an orbital;
two same-spin cannot. A spin-blind capacity mechanism forbids both. But
PR#260 (ratified) makes the μ-vs-ε branch the **spin sign-selector**, and DP-3
(ratified) gives **separate `S_μ`, `S_ε` kernels — L2-sum WITHIN a grade,
L∞ ACROSS grades.** Same-spin windings load the same grade → amplitudes sum →
that grade saturates → divergence → forbidden. Opposite-spin load different
grades → do not sum → allowed. **Two per orbital, spin-antiparallel, falls out
of the ratified combine rule.** And #260's "degenerate on equilibrium
observables" is the FEATURE, not the obstacle: it is why the two spin states
are energetically identical while still not competing for capacity. *(The
orchestrator had this backwards on first pass and corrected it in-walk.)*

**The objection that survives.** Exclusion is **exactly two** — a
capacity/proportion argument gives a SOFT cutoff, because a real electron
loads both grades and the branch only says which saturates first. **You get
repulsion; you do not get an integer.** The honest shape of a complete
mechanism is therefore **topology supplies the integer (charge = Link, #416),
capacity supplies the enforcement** — a division of labour that matches
exactly what canon derives vs imports.

## §2 — "2 defects, or effectively 2?" — Grant's correction to the orchestrator's framing

**Grant was right and the orchestrator's framing was ill-posed.** In a field
theory there is no "two objects at the same place" — there is one field
configuration, and topological charge **adds**. Two co-located same-orientation
windings ARE a Link = 2 configuration: not two electrons, one object of twice
the charge.

**The test must therefore be posed about ONE configuration's internal
structure:** *can a single self-consistent configuration carry two units of
winding in the SAME grade, versus one unit in each of two grades?* That is
well-posed, it is what harmonic balance can answer, and it is the version
where DP-3's combine rule actually bites. **Recorded consequence:** exclusion
in a field theory is not "you cannot put two things in one box" — it is *"the
configuration you would call two-in-one-box is a different single object, and
the question is whether that object exists."* This framing is now the P2
Pauli arm's specification.

## §3 — The window shade, and its missing catch

and the catch is the rim.

## §6 — The Cosserat DOF, physically; and what sets a bound core's extent

the surrounding swirl — and the difference between the node's microrotation
and the macroscopic swirl is what generates **couple-stress**.

**Plumber picture: the vacuum is a lattice of gyroscopes** — each site a
flywheel, neighbours coupled by torsional springs. Canon's EE reading:
translational DOF = **capacitor** (E-field storage); microrotational =
**inductive flywheel** (B-field storage); the neighbour torsion = transformer
mutual-inductance gradient = γ_c. Axiom 1: *"Cosserat rotational DOF IS the
substrate-native origin of intrinsic spin."*

**The consequence that matters:** without the Cosserat DOF the vacuum could
store energy in compression and shear but **could not store angular momentum

scalar-only solver cannot see any of them (R58 §3, blocker S1).
