\noindent\textbf{Applied Vacuum Engineering: Volume IV} \\
This document transitions AVE from a theoretical framework into an applied, falsifiable engineering discipline.

\begin{abstract}
    A physical theory is judged by its engineering utility and its falsifiability.
    
    \textbf{Volume IV: Applied Impedance Engineering \& Experimental Program} sets out the framework's bench-falsification program. The sharpest near-term tests probe the tree-level vacuum nonlinearity, where AVE's saturation kernel
    \begin{equation}
        S(A) = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{V}{V_{yield}}\right)^2}, \qquad V_{yield} \approx 43.65\ \text{kV}
    \end{equation}
    departs from the polynomial Euler--Heisenberg expansion of QED --- a difference measurable in cross-polarized vacuum birefringence and asymmetric-electrode vacuum-mirror geometries (Ch.~11). The volume also applies the substrate's impedance principles to engineering questions, including the topological reasons thermomagnetic tokamak fusion is difficult and the limits on antimatter--vacuum annihilation.
\end{abstract}
