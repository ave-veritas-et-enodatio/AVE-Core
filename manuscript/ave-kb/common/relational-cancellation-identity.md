## §0 — The EE-native lead: infinite CMRR by identity, not by engineering

A bench instrumentation amplifier rejects common-mode signal at a **finite** CMRR —
80–120 dB — because its rejection is **built**: a matched resistor network, trimmed,
drifting, finite. The substrate is a different kind of differential instrument. Its
reference channel is not a second, separately-matched path: **the reference is made of
the same medium as the signal** — rulers and clocks are wave-made of the thing being
biased — so the "matching" is not a manufacturing tolerance, it is an **identity**.
An influence that rescales signal and reference *together* never appears in any
reading formed *between* them. The rejection is **algebraic, not engineered:
the common-mode term cancels exactly, at every amplitude, to all orders, with zero
trimmed parameters. The substrate's CMRR is infinite BY IDENTITY.**

Canon already states this at the coupling level, verbatim on the EP row — *"**At the
WEP / composition level** CMRR is **infinite BY IDENTITY**: gravitational charge ≡
inertial mass, nothing to mismatch under a uniform (common-mode) drive"*, and
*"the **tide is the DIFFERENTIAL mode**"* ([`translation-tables/translation-circuit.md:156`](translation-tables/translation-circuit.md)).
That row's own axis discipline carries over unchanged: **coupling-level** CMRR (EP —
nothing to mismatch) is a different mechanism from **readout-level** CMRR (the
ε-sector gauge rider — a uniform bias loads the Q-point but reflects nothing,
∇A = 0); this leaf's identity is the common algebra both wear, and every instance
must say which level it instantiates.

**Two boundary markers, stated before the theorem is.**

**(i) The measured per-instance boundary.** The same EP row supplies it: at the
**SEP** level, CMRR is **FINITE** — the Nordtvedt gravitational-self-energy term is a
nonzero mismatch. That is not a counterexample to the identity; it is the identity's
**premise failing on that instance** (the "reference" does not co-transform *exactly*
once self-energy differs), which is exactly where the physics lives. The theorem
cancels what co-transforms; the boundary of what co-transforms is measurable content.

**(ii) The standing sector-by-sector debt (this leaf does NOT discharge it).** The
premise this identity runs on — that physical rulers and clocks *actually* co-transform
with the influence, exactly — is an **open corpus debt**, not a settled theorem, in the
one sector that matters most for relativity. The lineage map states it as the standing
Lorentzian debt: *"The standing debt: derive exact self-cancellation sector-by-sector,
or state where it breaks and at what magnitude"* ([`physics-lineage-map.md:473`](physics-lineage-map.md)),
and it grades the exactness explicitly: *"If exactness fails at any accessible order,
the Lorentzian branch dies for AVE exactly as it would have died in 1905"* — because
*"weak-C exactness is a prediction, not a theorem"* ([`physics-lineage-map.md:587`](physics-lineage-map.md)).
The algebra below is exact **given** exact co-transformation; **it supplies no evidence
that any real sector co-transforms exactly**, and it must never be cited as retiring
that debt. Per-sector premise-membership is the physics; the identity is the
bookkeeping over it.

## §1 — The theorem, with its premise exposed

**Premise (the relational-observable / phase-only epistemology).** Call an observable
$O$ **relational** when it is expressed as a **ratio** (or, in offset/log form, a
**difference**) of a signal against a reference,

$$O = \frac{x}{r} \quad\text{or}\quad O = x - r,$$

and call an influence $T$ **common-mode for $O$** when it acts on signal and
reference **identically and exactly**:
$T: (x, r) \mapsto (g\,x,\; g\,r)$ (ratio form) or $(x + g,\; r + g)$ (offset form),
for one common $g$. The premise has **two legs**, and both can fail.

**Premise leg 1 — class membership (which observables are relational).** The
framework's answer is *all of them*, and it is not a free assumption: in a phase-only
substrate there is no sub-graph anchor to read an absolute against. This is the
measurement principle `clm-acdc07` (i) — *"**all measurement is AC.** A uniform DC bias
is gauge-relative — it self-cancels, because rulers and clocks are wave-made"*
([`form-deriving-value-importing.md:292-293`](form-deriving-value-importing.md)) —
whose excitation-side twin is Corollary B, *"a uniform offset is shared by the rulers
and clocks — which are themselves wave-made of the same medium — so it self-cancels
and cannot be read out"* ([`relative-offset-principle.md:35`](relative-offset-principle.md), `clm-hu1jjw`).
⚑ **The integer carve (stated, because the quantifier would otherwise over-reach).**
Canon's other observable family is topological — windings and `Link(∂Ω,F)`, summarized
in canon's own words: *"The substrate observes integer/half-integer counts of
relational observables; everything else is interior plumbing"* ([`boundary-observables-m-q-j.md:54`](boundary-observables-m-q-j.md)). Those **are**
members of the relational class *for the purpose of this identity*: a winding is a
closed-loop sum of phase **differences**, so each summand is already offset-form and a
common-mode offset cancels term-by-term. But their robustness under a **differential**
perturbation is a **different mechanism entirely** — quantization, not ratio-algebra:
*"integer protection is topological, so it does NOT lapse at the yield boundary"*
([`axiom-register.md:555`](axiom-register.md)). This identity claims **only** the
common-mode blindness of the integer, never its topological protection, and a lane
that cites `clm-relcnc` for the latter has mis-cited it.

**Premise leg 2 — exact co-transformation (which influences are common-mode for a
given observable).** This is a **physical fact per instance**, not an algebraic one:
it must be established for each claimed instance and it *can fail* (§3 case 2b; the
SEP boundary of §0(i); and, sector-wide, the open debt of §0(ii)). Substrate-scope for
the carve at source-law level: Axiom 5's clause **Q** is the reference-fixing clause —
*"the **quiescent reference (Q-point) that makes the potentials defined and clause G's
elliptic solve well-posed**"* ([`manuscript/common_equations/eq_axiom_5.tex:82-87`](../../common_equations/eq_axiom_5.tex)),
ruled a gauge choice at R55: *"A ground reference is a gauge choice, not a material
primitive"* ([`_orchestration/docket-entries/2026-08-24-ruling-r55-axiom5-source-law.md:54`](../../../_orchestration/docket-entries/2026-08-24-ruling-r55-axiom5-source-law.md)).
Clause **G** is the register's *"**G (bias coupling / bridge).**"* ([`axiom-register.md:359`](axiom-register.md)),
and R55's *"**Clause G is the one LAW-grade clause.**"*
([`_orchestration/docket-entries/2026-08-24-ruling-r55-axiom5-source-law.md:49`](../../../_orchestration/docket-entries/2026-08-24-ruling-r55-axiom5-source-law.md))
— the κ-stiffened elliptic bias law. ⚑ The reading that pairs them
as *gauge-vs-physical* — *"the source law makes the *uniform* bias pure gauge (clause
Q) and the *gradient* physical (clause G, $\mathbf{u}_0 = -\mathcal{A}_g\nabla\varepsilon_{11}$)"* —
is the walk's, at WALK grade ([`research/2026-08-24_frame-invariance-observer-walk_RECORD.md:83-84`](../../../research/2026-08-24_frame-invariance-observer-walk_RECORD.md)),
not a canon line; it is carried here attributed, not asserted.

**Theorem (the cancellation identity).** *For any relational observable $O$ and any
influence $T$ that is common-mode for $O$, the common-mode component cancels
IDENTICALLY:*

$$O(g\,x,\; g\,r) = \frac{g\,x}{g\,r} = \frac{x}{r} = O(x, r)
\qquad\text{(and } (x+g)-(r+g) = x-r \text{ in offset form).}$$

*The cancellation is **algebraic**: exact at every scale and amplitude of $g$, to all
orders (there is no expansion — $g$ never enters the observable at all), with zero
parameters (nothing is matched, tuned, or renormalized away). Where the premise
holds, the identity is EXACT; where the premise fails, the identity asserts
nothing.*

The proof is the one line above. That is the point: the theorem's entire mathematical
content is a cancellation any algebra permits — **and one the incumbent frameworks
already state in their own vocabulary** (§4) — while its entire **physical** content is
the premise: *which* observables are relational (leg 1) and *which* influences actually
co-transform the reference **exactly** (leg 2).

⚑ **Writing this in identity form does not upgrade the parent claim's booking.** Canon books the
AC/DC carve as *"an organizing PRINCIPLE not a theorem (solidity 0.55)"*
([`form-deriving-value-importing.md:372`](form-deriving-value-importing.md)). Writing
that principle's measurement statement in identity form **changes none of that**: the
word "Theorem" here labels one line of algebra whose premises are the contested
content, `clm-relcnc` books in the same organizing-principle class at the same 0.50–0.55
band (§2, §6), and no lane may cite this leaf's heading to re-grade `clm-acdc07`.

**The differential complement (the physical content).** Everything that does **not**
enter as common mode survives in full — a differential coupling $g_1 \neq g_2$
across signal and reference is precisely what a relational observable reads, and it
is the **only** thing one can read. The canonical row pair states it: *"**Uniform
external field** | **Common-mode bias**"* / *"**External field gradient** | **Differential bias**"*
([`translation-tables/translation-circuit.md:115-116`](translation-tables/translation-circuit.md)).
The theorem and its complement are one statement: **a relational observable rejects
the common mode exactly and transduces the differential mode entirely.**

**What the cancellation does NOT make unreal.** The uniform component is
*unobservable from inside via relational observables*, not *absent*. AVE's
distinctive bet (`clm-acdc07`) is that the demoted DC sector is a **physical medium
state** — this identity is the *mechanism of the demotion* (why perfected-AC
incumbents could discard the DC sector and lose nothing observational), not a denial
of the DC sector. **The walk reads the self-cancellation of the uniform part as the
framework's reading of relativity** — *"relativity = the physics of
only-ratios-observable"* — and that reading is carried here at **WALK grade,
un-promoted** (§3 row 11); this leaf does not assert it. What it *does* assert about
relativity is only the §4 positioning: the same statement is the incumbents' own, and
the constructive/mechanism half has its own prior art (§4.2).
