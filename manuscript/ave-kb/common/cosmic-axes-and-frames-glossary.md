## §1 — Rest frames

### K4 lattice rest frame

The chiral Laves K4 Cosserat substrate $\mathcal{M}_A$ (Ax 1) has a preferred rest frame. This frame is identified empirically with the CMB rest frame to high precision per AVE-QED Q-G24.

Per [`preferred-frame-and-emergent-lorentz.md:29`](../vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/preferred-frame-and-emergent-lorentz.md):

> "The identification of K4 lattice rest frame with the CMB rest frame is empirical (vacuum crystallization at recombination defines the CMB as the universe's rest frame; the K4 lattice was cast at that boundary). Earth moves at $\sim 370$ km/s relative to this frame, measurable as the CMB dipole anisotropy."

Strict Lorentz invariance at observable wavelengths is EMERGENT from the K4 cubic symmetry $(Fd\bar{3}m)$ — anisotropic corrections are suppressed by $(q\ell_{node})^4 \approx 10^{-22}$ at optical scales, two to three orders of magnitude below current cavity bounds. The rest frame exists; the cubic symmetry hides it at observable scales.

### Sun's velocity vector through $\mathcal{M}_A$ rest frame

The Sun moves at $\sim 370$ km/s through the K4 lattice rest frame toward the CMB dipole direction $(l \approx 264°, b \approx 48°)$. This is the Sun's local kinematic state — its instantaneous velocity vector through the lattice — and is NOT a fundamental cosmological axis. The CMB dipole anisotropy IS the detection of this velocity.

Per [`flyby-anomaly-sagnac-operator.md:34`](../vol3/cosmology/ch14-orbital-mechanics/flyby-anomaly-sagnac-operator.md):

> "Bulk scale: K4 lattice at rest in CMB; Earth's center-of-mass moves at ~370 km/s through it."

### Substrate equilibrium velocity floor

A separate kinematic prediction, scoped DIFFERENTLY from the 370 km/s instantaneous Sun velocity, is the substrate equilibrium velocity for LSR-class systems:

$$v_{\text{substrate}} = \frac{\alpha c}{2\pi} \approx 348.18 \text{ km/s}$$

This is a zero-parameter Hoop-Stress-2π projection from the Schwinger anomalous-moment substrate-rate (canonical at [`mond-hoop-stress.md:43-56`](../vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/mond-hoop-stress.md)). It applies to LSR-class local-region kinematics (Sun + nearby thin-disk stars) per Gaia DR3 test 2026-05-17; it does NOT extend to globular-cluster-class populations (GC test 2026-05-17 night Outcome III — median $|v_{CMB}| \approx 564$ km/s, far from $\alpha c / (2\pi)$ and near Local Group cosmic-flow scale).

The two velocities (370 km/s, 348 km/s) reflect different scoping: 370 km/s is the instantaneous Sun velocity; $\alpha c / (2\pi)$ is the equilibrium floor for LSR-class systems. They are NOT the same prediction.

## §5 — What is NOT $\hat{\Omega}_{\text{freeze}}$ (anti-confusion clarifications)

The plumber-physical adjudication of E1b-prime (Pantheon+ pipeline circularity) depends on distinguishing the following concepts cleanly:

### CMB dipole direction $\neq$ $\hat{\Omega}_{\text{freeze}}$

The CMB dipole at $(l \approx 264°, b \approx 48°)$ is the Sun's velocity vector through the K4 lattice rest frame. The CMB axis-of-evil at $(l = 60.28°, b = 50.48°)$ is the $\hat{\Omega}_{\text{freeze}}$ projection into low-$\ell$ multipoles. These two directions are separated by $\approx 79°$. They are distinct directions corresponding to distinct concepts (instantaneous solar kinematics vs cosmological initial condition).

Pantheon+ bulk-flow analyses subtract solar motion at the CMB dipole direction (concept 1); the AVE prediction that Hubble bulk-flow aligns with $\hat{\Omega}_{\text{freeze}}$ (concept 2) is therefore structurally non-circular — the two directions are different by 79°.

### Sun's local kinematics $\neq$ cosmological initial condition

The 370 km/s LSR-class motion and the $\sim 348$ km/s substrate-equilibrium prediction both describe local-scale kinematics relative to the lattice rest frame. Neither is the cosmological chirality direction. The cosmological chirality direction $\hat{\Omega}_{\text{freeze}}$ is a vector locked in at lattice genesis and inherited by every region inside the crystallization front; it does NOT depend on local stellar motions.

### Hubble flow magnitude is NOT predicted by AVE

AVE predicts the DIRECTION of bulk Hubble flow (aligned with $\hat{\Omega}_{\text{freeze}}$ per Observable 2), not its magnitude. The Hubble constant $H_\infty = 28\pi m_e^3 cG/(\hbar^2 \alpha^2)$ is a separate prediction (lattice creep rate); Hubble bulk-flow magnitude (residual matter drift after subtracting $H_0$-scaled expansion) is set by initial-condition specifics not derivable from substrate constants alone.

### Lattice rest frame velocity (370 km/s) ≠ substrate equilibrium velocity ($\alpha c / 2\pi$)

These two velocity predictions have DIFFERENT scoping and are not interchangeable:

- 370 km/s: instantaneous Sun-through-lattice velocity; the CMB dipole IS its measurement; system-specific.
- $\alpha c / (2\pi) \approx 348$ km/s: equilibrium velocity floor for LSR-class kinematics; zero-parameter Hoop-Stress-2π projection; LSR-population-statistical prediction.

The 9% gap between the two (LSR cluster median 375 km/s observed vs $\alpha c / 2\pi$ prediction) reflects LSR participation in CMB-dipole-aligned cosmic flow (Local Group → Great Attractor), not a contradiction between the two predictions.
