## §0 — The contract

**What this leaf is.** An **index of established, external theorems** — one canonical framework-neutral statement each, the set of names that theorem wears in the different disciplines the corpus reads from, an **exactness class**, the **ontological-silence ceiling** it carries, its **verified corpus use sites**, and its **regime fence**. It exists because the same theorem arrives in this program under four or five different names, and an agent who meets it under one name does not recognise it under another — so the corpus either re-derives it, or (worse) treats the two names as two independent confirmations.

**What this leaf is NOT, stated once and binding on every consumer:**

1. **It is not a chord list, and cannot be read as one.** Every row here is *established mathematics that predates AVE and belongs to nobody*. A theorem that holds in two frameworks is a statement about the **algebra**, not about the world. This is why the ceiling column is **mandatory on every EXACT row** and why it is not optional prose: the canonical statement of the ceiling is Kron's, verbatim from [`physics-lineage-map.md`](physics-lineage-map.md):359 — ***"an exact isomorphism is ontologically silent."*** T8 records its status as **STANDING**, *"not evaded — conceded and relocated"* (:366). Nothing in this leaf relaxes that.
2. **It mints no physics claim and no id.** No `clm-` / `def-` / `exp-` / `sup-` / `ilk-`; no `confidence`, no `solidity`. Per **INVARIANT-S11** (extend, don't reinvent) and the `clm-eemap1` precedent — a means-tested consolidation of exact-but-silent algebra lands **under** existing structure, not as a new node scheme.
3. **It derives nothing and strengthens nothing.** A row's presence is not evidence for the corpus statement that cites it. If a corpus use site is weak, wrong, or asserted, the row **says so** (see TH-7's asserted-step flags) rather than laundering it.
4. **It is not the AVE-machinery register.** Theorems the AXIOMS force are `clm-`-backed and live in [`foundational-machinery-register.md`](foundational-machinery-register.md) (Theorem 3.1′, the Mass-Closure Theorem, the No-Hair rule, …). This leaf carries **only** theorems the program **imports**. The two sets are disjoint by construction; where they touch, the row cross-refs rather than restates.

**Admission criterion (why the register is short and stays short).** A row lands only if **all four** hold: (a) the object is a **theorem or a named form**, not a phenomenon or an analogy; (b) it wears **≥ 2 genuinely different discipline names** — a thesaurus row for a term with one name has no thesaurus content; (c) it has **≥ 1 verified corpus use site on `main`** (file:line, two-method), and unmerged-branch sites are cited **only** as supplements, always flagged; (d) its **ceiling and regime fence are stateable**. Candidates failing any of the four are recorded in **§5** with the reason, not quietly dropped.

## §1 — Structure adjudication (the load-bearing organisational call)

**Decision: a NEW LEAF at `manuscript/ave-kb/common/theorem-thesaurus.md`** — cross-indexed from the lineage map (T8 + §8) and from [`index.md`](index.md), citing (never duplicating) the lineage-map T-entries and the translation-table rows. Two alternatives were considered and rejected, and one deviation from the brief's suggested path is surfaced rather than taken silently.

**Rejected (b) — a §9 extension of [`physics-lineage-map.md`](physics-lineage-map.md).** That leaf's own §1 contract declares it *"the actual history of physics … organized around the **forking decision points** where physics chose a branch. Exactly one of the fourteen (T8) is theorem-shaped. A theorem-identity index has no fork to record, no WHY-class, no killer, and the §7 Romance Guard does not apply to it (a theorem is not a re-openable branch). Grafting the register on would dilute an explicit contract, and the leaf is already ~600 lines at the top of the practical read window.

**Rejected (a-as-briefed) — `common/translation-tables/theorem-thesaurus.md`.** That subtree is governed by the **Grant-ratified HUB-AND-SPOKE RULE** ([`translation-tables/README-architecture.md`](translation-tables/README-architecture.md):31, ratified 2026-07-20): *"A discipline maps **only** to the substrate-native representation (the hub). A cross-discipline correspondence (discipline-X concept ↔ discipline-Y concept) is expressed as **two spoke rows that share a hub cell**, never as a direct X↔Y row"* — and §2 defines a spoke as one file per discipline (`translation-<discipline>.md`). This register's core column **is** a direct cross-discipline name-set, and it is admissible **only because its join is a mathematical identity rather than a substrate identification** — i.e. it falls *outside* the rule's scope (the rule's stated rationale is that a direct X↔Y row *"smuggles in an unstated substrate identification and is unauditable"*; a theorem-identity smuggles nothing, and its audit is the proof). Filing it inside the subtree would either need an edit to the ratified architecture leaf, or leave a rule-violating-looking file in the family. **Deviation surfaced, not taken silently:** if the auditor lane prefers the briefed path, the move is a pure `git mv` plus a carve-out paragraph in `README-architecture.md` §3 — routed, not done here.

**Why this is an EXTENSION and not a third parallel structure** (the named failure mode):

1. **It is the second member of an existing `common/` register family, not a new family.** [`foundational-machinery-register.md`](foundational-machinery-register.md) is the status/provenance VIEW over AVE's **own** derived machinery (10 `clm-` members; rung 2 of the provenance ladder). This leaf is the same shape for the **imported** half — no `clm-`, no solidity, **exactness class + ceiling** in their place. Together they answer one question in two halves: *what theorems does this program stand on, and which of them did it derive?*
2. **Cite-don't-duplicate is structural, not aspirational.** Every row's `use sites` column is file:line into the lineage map, the translation tables, the KB leaves, the research docs, or the driver docstrings. No row restates a T-entry, a §4.7 row, or a claim rationale; each quotes at most the one verbatim clause that fixes the ceiling.
3. **The drift-check is mechanical.** §7 names three tripwires (a lineage-map T-entry rewrite, a translation-table row landing/merging, a use-site walk-back) and every row's citations are greppable, so a stale row fails a link/anchor check rather than rotting silently.

## §3 — Row schema + the exactness classes

Each row in §4 carries seven fields.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **id** | `TH-n`. A **local table label only** — deliberately NOT a spine id (no `\b[a-z]{3}-[a-z0-9]{6}\b` form, nothing materialised into `.index/`), so it cannot be mistaken for, or accrete into, a shadow knowledge-id scheme (INVARIANT-S11). It is a row number with a prefix |
| **canonical statement** | One sentence, framework-neutral mathematics. No substrate nouns, no EE nouns, no AVE symbols |
| **names across disciplines** | Only names that genuinely denote **this** theorem. A name that denotes a *cousin* goes in §6, not here |
| **exactness class** | One of the four below |
| **ceiling** | The ontological-silence ceiling — **mandatory on every EXACT row**, and stated in the row, not deferred |
| **corpus use sites** | file:line, two-method verified at `c8ceacc3`. **Unmerged-branch sites are flagged inline** (`★UNMERGED`) and never carry a row alone |
| **regime fence** | Where the identity stops. A row with no fence says "none known", explicitly |

**The four exactness classes:**

- **EXACT-same-theorem** — the names denote the *identical* mathematical statement; only the interpretation of the symbols differs. Proof-level identity, not resemblance. **Always carries a ceiling.**
- **structural-analogy** — the same *shape* under a mapping that is not an identity (some load-bearing object fails to map). Not used in §4 this pass; reserved so a future row cannot be silently upgraded.
- **regime-scoped** — an identity that holds *inside* a stated fence and fails outside it. The fence is the content of the row.
- **IMPORTED-FORM** — ★**not a theorem at all**: a constitutive/model form taken from an external literature, carried here *only* so it cannot inherit exactness by sitting next to exact rows. One row (TH-9) is in this class and says so in every cell.

### TH-1 — Dirichlet's principle (minimum potential energy)

- **Canonical statement.** For a positive-semidefinite quadratic energy form on a finite domain, the equilibrium field is the minimiser over all fields matching the prescribed *boundary values*; prescribing more boundary degrees of freedom shrinks the admissible set, so the constrained minimum can only rise.
- **Names across disciplines.** *Potential theory / PDE:* Dirichlet's principle. *Graph theory / numerics:* the Dirichlet energy of a weighted Laplacian, minimised under a Dirichlet partition. *Elasticity:* the principle of minimum potential energy. *Homogenization:* the **KUBC** (kinematic uniform boundary condition) arm — $u = E\cdot x$ prescribed on $\partial V$. *EE-network:* the **voltage-clamped / prescribed-potential** drive — hold the boundary nodes with ideal voltage sources, let the currents float, read the multiport $[Y]$.
- **Exactness class.** **EXACT-same-theorem.** All five are the Dirichlet partition of one PSD quadratic form; the elasticity and network statements differ only in whether the per-edge block is $3\times3$ or $1\times1$.
- **Ceiling.** Kron-class: this is an exact algebraic isomorphism, and *"an exact isomorphism is ontologically silent"* ([`physics-lineage-map.md`](physics-lineage-map.md):359). Recovering it on a lattice is **consistency-class**; it confers no chord and cannot.
- **Corpus use sites.** [`the-sourced-charge-no-go-cascade.md`](the-sourced-charge-no-go-cascade.md):94, :98 — the Dirichlet energy `φᵀ L_w φ = Σ_edge ε_eff·(Δφ)²` on the weighted srs graph Laplacian `L_w = Bᵀ diag(ε_eff) B`, **proof class THEOREM** ("discrete maximum principle / Dirichlet-energy argument"), established three independent ways; mirrored at [`claim-quality.md`](claim-quality.md):1345 (Lock 3). · [`relative-offset-principle.md`](relative-offset-principle.md):57 + [`claim-quality.md`](claim-quality.md):1541 — the `#782` K-side is *"a **KUBC upper bound** … an upper bound cannot support a floor claim"*. · ★UNMERGED: `translation-circuit.md` §4.7.2 **row D** (PR #804 OPEN) and `research/2026-07-28_subc-kubc-bracket_prereg-FROZEN.md` §1.3 (PR #802 OPEN).
- **Regime fence.** Needs a genuinely PSD form. On `main` the use is the **source-free** problem `L_w φ = 0` with **strictly positive** edge weights — the corpus's own caveat, at [`claim-quality.md`](claim-quality.md):1345, is explicit: *"the hypothesis is `S > 0` **strictly**; the `S = 0` (rupture-point, `ε → 0`) puncture is out-of-scope and is lane Z's doorway."* ★**Do not conflate the two on-main readings:** `:94` uses the Dirichlet **energy** to prove a *no-flux* result (a maximum-principle argument); the *bound/minimisation* reading — Dirichlet's **principle** proper — appears only in the unmerged bracket lane. Citing `:94` as a variational bound is a mis-cite.

### TH-2 — Thomson's (Kelvin's) principle

- **Canonical statement.** Dual to TH-1: among all flux fields satisfying the balance law and the prescribed sources/injections, the equilibrium flux minimises the dual quadratic form; prescribing the injection pattern shrinks *that* admissible set, so its minimum can only rise.
- **Names across disciplines.** *EE-network:* Thomson's (Kelvin's) principle of **minimum dissipation** — the steady current distribution minimises $\sum_e r_e I_e^2$ over KCL-admissible flows. *Potential theory:* Thomson's principle (the dual of Dirichlet's). *Elasticity:* the principle of **minimum complementary energy**. *Homogenization:* the **SUBC** (static uniform boundary condition) arm — $t = \Sigma\cdot n$ prescribed, no displacement prescribed anywhere. *EE-drive:* the **current-fed** boundary — ideal current sources, potentials float, read $[Z] = [Y]^{+}$.
- **Exactness class.** **EXACT-same-theorem** (the Neumann partition of the same PSD form as TH-1).
- **Ceiling.** Kron-class: the dual partition is an exact algebraic isomorphism no less than the primal one, and *"an exact isomorphism is ontologically silent"* ([`physics-lineage-map.md`](physics-lineage-map.md):359). Duality does not weaken the ceiling; it inherits it unchanged from TH-1.
- **Corpus use sites.** [`electron-plumbing-primer.md`](electron-plumbing-primer.md):101, verbatim — *"the field arranges itself (surface charges) so the **global** steady flow is the one that **minimizes total dissipation** (Thomson's principle)"*. · ★UNMERGED: `translation-circuit.md` §4.7.2 **row E** (#804) and the #802 frozen prereg §1.3, verbatim: *"Under `t = Σ·n` on `∂V` the equilibrium stress field minimizes the complementary energy over all STATICALLY ADMISSIBLE stress fields … i.e. `SUBC is a rigorous lower (soft) bound on the stiffness`"*.
- **Regime fence — ★load-bearing, do not drop at cite time.** The on-`main` citation is the **dissipative (resistive)** reading, used as a plumber-physical illustration in a wire, and the primer immediately analogises it to Axiom 3 (:103, *"This is the substrate's own law — Axiom 3"*). The **analogy is structural** — local greedy rule ⇒ global variational answer — and is **not** a licence to read the substrate as dissipative: the sub-yield interior is **Ax3 lossless-reactive**, so the substrate-side object is **stored** reactive energy, not dissipated power. The algebra is identical only for a **single-sign** form ($g \to b$, one sign of susceptance). For a general AC mixed-reactance network it **fails outright** — see **TH-8**.

### TH-3 — The two-sided effective-property bracket

- **Canonical statement.** TH-1 and TH-2 applied to the same specimen bracket its exact effective property from above and below; any intermediate boundary condition on that specimen lands between them.
- **Names across disciplines.** *Homogenization / micromechanics:* the **Hill (1963) / Huet (1990) / Hazanov–Huet (1994)** apparent-modulus ordering $C^{SUBC} \le C^{*} \le C^{KUBC}$. *Polycrystal averaging:* the **Voigt** (upper, uniform-strain trial field) and **Reuss** (lower, uniform-stress trial field) bounds. *EE-network:* the **Dirichlet–Thomson two-sided bound** on effective conductance, $G^{\text{current-fed}} \le G^{*} \le G^{\text{voltage-clamped}}$, with **Rayleigh's short-cut and cut laws** as the two special cases. *Composite theory:* the elementary / first-order bounds.
- **Exactness class.** **EXACT-same-theorem.** One constraint-monotonicity argument, applied twice in dual directions.
- **★AUDIT-FLAG (2026-07-28, #805 compact audit — Grant ruling pending; flagged, NOT fixed).** The **Voigt/Reuss** inclusion in this row's `EXACT-same-theorem` class is **under adjudication**. Voigt/Reuss are **scale-free uniform-trial-field evaluations** — no BVP, no specimen, no boundary layer; **Hill 1963 / Huet 1990 / Hazanov–Huet 1994** are **size-dependent boundary-condition BVP bounds** converging to $C^{*}$. The regime-fence sentence below (*"the finite-cluster boundary layer is carried by **both** bounds"*) therefore applies to the **Hill/Huet pair ONLY**. The class cell and the row structure are **left unchanged** until that ruling lands.
- **Ceiling.** Kron-class: one constraint-monotonicity argument is the same algebra in every discipline that names it, and *"an exact isomorphism is ontologically silent"* ([`physics-lineage-map.md`](physics-lineage-map.md):359) — plus a second, sharper one: **the bounds are on the ABSOLUTE effective property. A RATIO of two boundary-conditioned readings is NOT bound-ordered.** (Demonstrated, not asserted: the unmerged #804 §4.7.3 carried-exposure paragraph records `absolute_theorem_grade_ordering_holds_everywhere = True` across all 48 walked rows while the *ratio* inverts wherever the reference's own boundary-layer gap exceeds the arm's.)
- **Corpus use sites.** [`relative-offset-principle.md`](relative-offset-principle.md):57 + [`claim-quality.md`](claim-quality.md):1541 (the KUBC upper-bound reading, and its consequence that *"an upper bound cannot support a floor claim"*). · `research/2026-07-04_saturated-elastic-tensor_prereg_FROZEN.md`:204 — `G_Voigt, G_Reuss, G_Hill` computed as *"the anisotropy spread"*. · `research/2026-06-15_k2g-crystalline-provenance_prereg_FROZEN.md`:51 — *"either elastic isotropy (Zener A=1) or a Voigt/Reuss/Hill average — itself a **choice***"; result doc :31, :47, :62. · `research/2026-07-04_prestress-tensor_prereg_FROZEN.md`:230. · ★UNMERGED: `translation-circuit.md` §4.7.2 **row F** + §6 **#32** (#804); #802 prereg §1.3, which tags the ordering *"a `[derived]` textbook theorem — the FORM the extraction is built on, not a fit"*.

### TH-4 — Kron's exact network representability (+ boundary Schur reduction)

- **Canonical statement.** Any **linear** physical system is exactly representable as an electrical network with the same solution set; eliminating interior degrees of freedom to expose a boundary/port response is the Schur complement of the system's (block) Laplacian.
- **Names across disciplines.** *EE:* Kron's equivalent circuit; **Kron reduction**; **diakoptics** (tearing + exact reassembly through the intersection network). *Numerical linear algebra:* the **Schur complement**; **domain decomposition**; substructuring / static condensation. *CEM:* **TLM** (Johns–Beurle 1971) as the direct-descent instance. *Graph theory:* boundary reduction of a weighted (block) Laplacian; the effective-conductance/impedance matrix.
