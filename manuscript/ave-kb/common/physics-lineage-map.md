> **Companions:** [`historical-precedents.md`](historical-precedents.md) (the two 19th-century roots, orientation) and [`the-abandoned-interior.md`](the-abandoned-interior.md) (the two-deletions-one-restoration synthesis). It cross-links; it does not re-tell.

---

## §1 — The contract

**What this leaf is.** The actual history of physics — Kelvin's vortex atoms, Maxwell's quaternions, MacCullagh's rotational aether, Born–Infeld, the Cosserats, Kron, the superconductivity arc, pilot-wave theory, analog gravity — treated as **navigational infrastructure** for the AVE program: named-object archaeology at century scale, organized around the **forking decision points** where physics chose a branch. The heart is the **Fork Registry** (§2); the fourteen thread capsules (§4) are supporting depth, not the spine.

**The five uses:**

1. **Every fork records WHY the mainline branch won**, classified on a four-value axis:
   - **EXPERIMENT** — data forced it. Re-opening the fork requires the killer be addressed *by name* (not around, not below).
   - **PARSIMONY** — Occam with no discriminating data. The road not taken remained empirically live; re-opening requires giving the deleted structure observable work to do.
   - **FASHION** — sociology: attention, careers, institutional gravity, taboo. Never technical; legitimately re-openable, but the *underlying technical constraints* (listed separately) still bind.
   - **NOTATION** — a usability war between formalisms. No experiment adjudicated; structure was excised for throughput, not falsified.

   A fork taken on FASHION or NOTATION may be legitimately re-opened. A fork taken on EXPERIMENT may only be re-opened by meeting its killer head-on — and §5 lists every killer still standing.

2. **AVE's position is marked at every node:** **FITS** (same branch as mainline — shared ground, the AC side of the corpus AC/DC carve, [`form-deriving-value-importing.md`](form-deriving-value-importing.md) §"AC/DC") or **CLASHES** (the road not taken — the DC side). Every CLASH carries its **LIVE CONFRONTATION**: the named data/test that adjudicates the fork *now*, with corpus receipt and current status.

3. **Before minting a structure, check the lineage.** A dead program may have already built it — the Cosserats built MacCullagh's missing balance law; Kron built the network Maxwell; London built the fluxoid. Prior art first (§4 capsules), mint second.

4. **The killers-still-standing register (§5) is the open-threats list.** Anything that killed an ancestor program and has not been removed by modern tools constrains AVE *today*. A killer does not care that the program wearing it is new.

5. **Prior-art honesty.** Ancestors are cited properly — the SVE Letter already does this for Born–Infeld (`papers/2026_birefringence_letter/main.tex:336-343`); the bibliography already declares Cosserat 1909 as "the prior art for the K4 node's microrotational DOF" (`manuscript/bibliography.bib:239-247`). This leaf extends that discipline to the whole tree.

**Register.** NO-CLAIM. The history is fact; every AVE-bridge statement is a pointer to a canonical leaf at that leaf's own class ceiling (mostly consistency-class). Nothing here upgrades any claim.
