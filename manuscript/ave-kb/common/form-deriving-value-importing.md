## The principle (one line)

> **The geometry and topology of the chiral K4 Cosserat substrate FORCE the
> dimensionless FORMS (the structure — the "chords"). The dimensionful VALUES of
> the handful of calibration constants the substrate is *fed* are calibration
> INPUTS it does not independently select (the "echoes").**

This is the framework's `determinism → emergent` north-star (real chord or echo?)
**resolved one level up**: the question is not answered globally "chord" or
"echo" — it splits cleanly by *type*. **Structure is chord; the magnitude of the
calibration *inputs* is echo.** A theory can force the SHAPE of a result from its
geometry while still taking the SCALE of that result as a measured input — and
that is what the accumulated AVE record now shows.

This is **not** a deflation. Forcing the form — the exact dimensionless skeleton —
from topology alone is a strong, falsifiable claim and is **untouched** by this
finding. What it closes is the *stronger* aspiration that the substrate also
**selects the numerical values** of its own calibration constants.

## The calibration inputs — 3 dimensionful constants + 4 axioms

The substrate is specified by the **four foundational axioms** (substrate

kernel — see [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) INVARIANT-S2) plus a marked
**calibration set of 3 dimensionful constants** `{m_e, α, G}` (the

as a geometric ratio of `ℓ_node`. The meta-finding is the **statement of which
side each calibration constant lands on**, and how each downstream magnitude
inherits its provenance.

## The testing consequence

*(from the divergence-prediction sweep `wqdp2zxcb`, 2026-06-20)*

Because **every dimensionful magnitude is an echo by construction** — its
numerical value traces back to one of the imported calibration constants — an
AVE-distinct *testable* prediction cannot live in any single dimensionful number
matching experiment (that match is built in, or is the SM's match re-derived).
The AVE-distinct chords that experiment can actually discriminate live only in
two places:

1. **FORM-EXISTENCE divergences** — a structural feature AVE's medium *has* that
   the SM vacuum *lacks*. The substrate **saturates** (a finite yield field),
   admits a **longitudinal scalar** grade, and is **natively birefringent** —
   structures with no SM-vacuum counterpart. A measurement that probes the
   *existence* of such a structure (not its imported magnitude) is testing a
   chord. Example: vacuum nonlinearity departing from QED's Euler–Heisenberg
   expansion at tree level — AVE has a yield, QED does not.

   Mach-Zehnder split, `g_* = 7³/4`. A measurement of one of these tests a chord
   directly.
