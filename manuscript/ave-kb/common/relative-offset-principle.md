## The principle

<!-- claim-quality: clm-hu1jjw — the relative-offset principle + the Fork-ρ direction (walk-ratified direction, pending adversarial review) -->

> **In a phase-only substrate there is no spatial frame beneath the node graph. Position is defined only ON the graph — as a pattern of relative phase offsets across nodes. A localized excitation IS such a pattern, and its energy goes where its host nodes go. It therefore cannot hold station against the material displacement of its nodes: to do so would require hopping node-to-node in exact anti-advective compensation, which presupposes the very sub-graph spatial anchor a phase-only substrate denies. An excitation's rest frame is thus not the crystal's frame (≈ the CMB-isotropy frame, in which `Ω_freeze` is defined) but its own state of motion, set by history. Only the RELATIVE offset between an excitation and the medium it rides is defined, and a uniform such offset is unobservable.**

The crystal has a frame; the **excitation does not inherit it**. The lattice / crystal rest frame **exists** — cosmologically it is the CMB-isotropy frame ([`cosmic-axes-and-frames-glossary.md`](cosmic-axes-and-frames-glossary.md); [`preferred-frame-and-emergent-lorentz.md`](../vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/preferred-frame-and-emergent-lorentz.md)). But a **boosted breather is an equally valid lossless solution** below the band edge: a lossless soliton translating uniformly through the cold lattice radiates nothing — **there is no aether drag**. So the soliton's rest frame is set by its **history**, not pinned to the crystal's frame.

## The anti-advection impossibility argument

The load-bearing step is a **reductio**. Suppose a localized excitation could **hold station** — remain at a fixed spatial location while the material of its host nodes displaces past it (e.g. under a passing carrier's material motion). On a phase-only graph "a fixed spatial location" is not a primitive: the only positions that exist are node indices and the relative phase pattern across them. To stay at a fixed *spatial* location while the *material* moves, the pattern would have to **re-host itself node-to-node in exact compensation** for the material displacement — advecting *backward* relative to the medium at exactly the medium's forward material velocity (**anti-advection**). But "the medium's material velocity" is only defined against **some frame**, and the only frame the pattern could anti-advect against is a **spatial anchor beneath the graph** — which is exactly what phase-only forbids (there is no sub-graph space to be still in). The premise is therefore self-contradictory: **station-keeping presupposes the anchor whose absence defines the substrate.** Hence a trapped pattern **cannot hold station against material displacement; it participates in it.**

## The three corollaries

**Corollary A — discrete pinning delivers material TRACKING, not station-keeping.**
Discrete (Peierls-Nabarro-class) pinning pins an excitation to **node INDICES**. Node indices **displace with the carrier** (they are the medium's own material points). So pinning delivers **FULL material tracking**, not station-keeping — **there is no pinned escape from advection.** The common reading "a pinned defect is stuck in place" silently means "stuck at these node indices," and those indices move with the medium; pinning strength sets *how tightly* the pattern tracks its host indices, never *whether* those indices move.

**Corollary B — this is the mechanism-side of no-aether-drag.**
The same fact seen from the **excitation's** side is the mechanism-level statement of **why there is no aether-drag observable** (emergent-relativity self-cancellation). Only the *relative* offset between an excitation and the medium is defined; a *uniform* offset is shared by the rulers and clocks — which are themselves wave-made of the same medium — so it self-cancels and cannot be read out. This is the excitation-side twin of the crystal-side statement that the preferred frame exists yet is optically undetectable at observable wavelengths ([`preferred-frame-and-emergent-lorentz.md`](../vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/preferred-frame-and-emergent-lorentz.md)), and of the AC/DC carve's "all measurement is AC; a uniform DC offset self-cancels" ([`form-deriving-value-importing.md`](form-deriving-value-importing.md), `clm-acdc07`).

**Corollary C — the Fork-ρ direction is Reading A (MATCHED).**
Because trapped-energy patterns **participate in any carrier's oscillatory material motion** (they sway with the node displacements the carrier is made of — *material* velocity, not *phase/group* velocity; the "surf" reading is retracted, transport was never required), a caged soliton's trapped-energy mass **loads** the effective inertia `ρ_eff` a compression carrier feels. So the **direction** of Fork ρ (*is trapped-energy mass acoustic inertia, or gravitating-only?*, routed from the `#782` RVE bench, [`research/2026-07-21_rve-aggregation-bench_result.md`](../../../research/2026-07-21_rve-aggregation-bench_result.md) §7.4) is **Reading A — toward MATCHED** (`ρ_eff/ρ_0 = 1 + β·φ > 1`), not the engine-native inertially-inert Reading B (`ρ_eff/ρ_0 = 1`).
