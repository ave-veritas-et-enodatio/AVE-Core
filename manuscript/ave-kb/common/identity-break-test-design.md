## 1. TWO FIXED POINTS, NO LADDER

The framework's boundary mathematics has exactly **two** behaviors, not a graded ladder of
reflection coefficients:

- **Perfect mismatch — `Γ = −1`.** Topologically gated, intrinsic, **`Q → ∞`**. The
  `Γ = −1` wall is a phase-transition / topological boundary (the ruptured `Γ = −1` surface,
  the electron's terminal pole).
- **Perfect match — `Γ → 0`.** Absorbing / radiating, **`Q ~ few`** (finite, coupling-set).

**Apparent intermediate structure resolves to these two, projected through sector ports.** The
sharpest receipt is the **T1 atom-Q adjudication** (merged **#668**,
[`../../../research/2026-07-13_t1-atom-q-cascade-gate_RESULT.md`](../../../research/2026-07-13_t1-atom-q-cascade-gate_RESULT.md)):
the atom's observed `~10⁷` "excited-state Q" *looked* like a distinct intermediate cascade-filter
cutoff sitting between the electron's loaded `α⁻¹` and the black hole's `Q = ℓ`. It is not. The
result splits it across the two fixed points seen through **different sector ports**:

- the **wall channel** (longitudinal `Z_bulk` trapping walls of the graded Coulomb dress) returns
  the **intrinsic endpoint** `Q_wall → ∞`, **α-free** (H(1s), H(2s), He⁺, reduced-mass leg all
  diverge; Z-/coupling-/mass-invariant to `rel < 1e-9`) — the `Γ = −1` fixed point;
- the number `~10⁷` lives only in the **transverse-EM (`Z_EM`) radiative port** — a *different*
  sector the longitudinal Hermitian eigencavity does not express — and it is the **α-echo**
  `Q_rad = 4 α⁻³` exactly (the QM value `≈ 9.6 α⁻³ ≈ 2.5×10⁷` is the measured linewidth rung).

The black-hole endpoint stays canon and **distinct** (`Q = ℓ`, Op21 at the at/above-rupture
`Γ = −1` boundary — a genuine structural cutoff, the contrast that sharpens the atom's collapse).
So the "cascade filter" is the homogeneous vacuum line **relabeled** on this rung — a vocabulary
echo, not a third fixed point. **There is no ladder; there are two fixed points read through
ports.**

## 3. EVEN NONLINEARITY ⇒ SECOND-ORDER-ONLY SIGNATURES

The saturation kernel is **even in the amplitude**: `S(A) = √(1 − A²)`, `S(−A) = S(A)`. An even
nonlinearity **cannot rectify** — there is no first-order (odd) observable, no DC-offset from a
symmetric drive, no directed ratchet. Every signature is **A²-class** (second order). It follows
that a discriminator must be **FORM-class** — a *sign*, a *distance-power*, a *frequency
flatness*, a *parameter-free zero*, an *exponent* — and **never magnitude-class**, because the
magnitude is `(A/A_yield)²`-suppressed to unreachability at any bench field.

**Receipts:**

- **CVR requirements** (merged **#667**,
  [`../../../research/2026-07-13_cvr-requirements_DERIVED.md`](../../../research/2026-07-13_cvr-requirements_DERIVED.md)).
  `CVR-REQ-FRAME` makes the response `E²`-even and the magnitude route **dead three ways**
  (unreachable by `~20` OOM; the bench bound is `~10` OOM looser than the lattice coefficient it
  would need to detect). `CVR-REQ-BIAS` is therefore **classifier-driven, not magnitude-driven**.
  Because the response is even, the CLEAVE-01 polarity-reversal (odd) trick is **unavailable** —
  the even sag is polarity-degenerate with every even fixture systematic. The discriminator is the
  **triple form-conjunction** `{negative-sign ∧ d⁻² ∧ flat-in-f}` (chord slope `−1/2`, tangent
  slope `−3/2`, `1/√2` NDC point), no one of which any single classical mechanism survives — a
  parameter-free FORM object, not a magnitude.
- **The diode / rectifier "four deaths"** (F6 charter §(iv),
  [`../../../research/2026-07-13_f6-tier1-two-reservoir-ledger_CHARTER.md`](../../../research/2026-07-13_f6-tier1-two-reservoir-ledger_CHARTER.md)):
  a one-way valve is dead four ways because the even kernel cannot rectify — (1) *"`S(A)=√(1−A²)`
  is instantaneous, even, memoryless — identical 2nd-order momentum for symmetric and asymmetric
  drive"*; (2) any true rectifying loop is Level-2 memristive `= dissipative` (contradicts
  losslessness); (3) the diode threshold `V_f` is **free, not forced** (no substrate scale gives a
  forward dead-zone); (4) chirality-ratchet-as-arrow is **refuted** ("chirality is a parity
  selector, not the arrow"). The arrow, where it exists, must come from **where the energy goes**
  (mode-count), never from a valve.

**Design consequence:** headlining an even-kernel result on a *magnitude* is a category error — it
is `A²`-suppressed and unreachable. Headline the **form**.

## 4. THREE VALUE-GENERATORS, CLOSED

Every number the corpus produces traces to exactly **three** generators. No fourth has ever
appeared.

| generator | owns | worked instances (receipts) |
|---|---|---|
| **Topology** | the integers | `z = 3 ⇒ Γ = (2−z)/z = −1/3`, `|Γ|² = 1/9` (#669; `translation-circuit.md:180`); the confinement counts `c = 3, 5, 7`; `Q = ℓ` (BH endpoint, Op21, #668) |
| **The kernel's algebra** | the parameter-free forms | `1/√2` tangent/NDC zero; `−3/2` vs `−1/2` slopes (tangent vs chord, #667); `√S` vs `S` (small- vs large-signal); `√(1−A²)` even shape |
| **The import basis `{m_e, α, G}`** | all the scales | with **`α`-POWERS as the sector-port ladder**: `α⁻¹` = loaded electron tank; `α⁻³` = radiative atom (#668). The powers are set by *which port* (loaded reactive vs transverse radiative), not by a new constant |

The generators are **closed**: topology hands integers, the kernel hands forms, the import basis
hands scales, and `α`-powers index the port. A result that appears to need a *fourth* generator —
a new scale, a new form, a magic number outside `{integers} × {kernel forms} × {m_e, α, G}^n` — is
the tell that either an import went unlabeled or an identity is being read as a derivation. This is
the numerical face of FORM-derives / VALUE-imports (see
[`form-deriving-value-importing.md`](form-deriving-value-importing.md)).

## 5. THE DESIGN RULE (and its reviewer corollary)

The four observations above have one operative consequence.

> **A discriminating AVE test never measures a magnitude — it BREAKS AN IDENTITY.**

The five identity-break levers, each with a live receipt:

1. **Decorrelate imported histories** — break a co-monotone lock so the response reveals *which*
   input it tracks (F6 §4.7 decorrelated arm: the only tier-1 chord-carrier;
   [`../../../research/2026-07-13_f6-tier1-two-reservoir-ledger_CHARTER.md`](../../../research/2026-07-13_f6-tier1-two-reservoir-ledger_CHARTER.md)).
   **Update (F6 tier-1 executed, PR #674, ruled 2026-07-13 — KEEP-BOTH).** The lever's
   identity-break example **stands** — the decorrelation arms isolated *which* input `Γ` responds to
   (matter-occupancy `n_B`), i.e. decorrelated histories **did break the degeneracy** the lever is
   built to break. What is **superseded** is "*the only* tier-1 chord-carrier": the driver found the
   chord separable from the frontier default on **physical** histories too (different powers of `a`),
   and Grant's §5.4 ruling (2026-07-13, BOTH) closed the homogeneous ledger as the **wrong instrument**
   at late epochs (chord → Λ past `τ₀≈300`). The F6 CHORD therefore **relocates to the spatial
   cross-correlation channel** (DESI/Euclid); the decorrelation *design principle* stays a live
   identity-break lever regardless (`research/2026-07-13_f6-tier1-ledger-driver_result.md`).
2. **Sweep a geometric power** — fit an exponent, not a level (CVR `d⁻²` vs electrode `d⁻³`,
   `Δs = 1` at `σ_s ≤ 0.1`; #667).
3. **Cross a band edge** — move from the homogenized interior identity to the resolving edge
   (#669).
4. **Flip a boundary condition** — compare `Γ = −1` reflecting vs `Γ → 0` absorbing on the *same*
   seed (the #670 PML-vs-closed-box axis, which is *how* the E-channel vacuity was exposed).
5. **Close/open a port with a fireability control** — a positive control that *can* fire the
   distinct bin, so the null is a verdict and not an unfireable checklist (#668 Leg B returned an
   in-window `Q_control = 1.02×10⁶`, proving the `Q_wall → ∞` was physics, not an instrument that
   cannot fire).

### Reviewer corollary — the VACUOUS-DETECTOR KNIFE

> **For every PASS criterion in a PR under review, audit whether it is an identity in disguise —
> a conservation law, a calibration return, or a co-monotone form. A detector that cannot fail on
> physical inputs is a checklist, not a gate.**

Three kinds of disguised identity, each caught in the record:

- **Conservation-law-in-disguise:** the closed-box `E`-channel discriminator caught vacuous in
  **#670**'s own review — *"the boundary-axis E-channel discriminator is vacuous"* (finding #1/#3,
  `E ≡ 1.0000` mode-independent, including the structure-dead `photon_lock` seed). A retention

- **Reach-limited equality-audit:** the F6 **IMPOSED-LEAK** conservation/destination audit owns
  its own limit in the charter — it catches only a **non-conserving, energy-destroying** transfer;
  *"an honestly-booked conserving dissipative term (`dρ=−γρ`, `dE_T2=+γρ`) is NOT caught"* because
  entropic-vs-dissipative is inexpressible in two scalars. The gate is real but bounded, and the
  bound is disclosed, not hidden.
- **Calibration-return-in-disguise:** the X36 **install-tautology** — a ceiling that equals the
  installed anchor is *calibration-in, calibration-out (identity-class), NOT emergence*
  ([`../../../research/2026-07-09_x36-node-bottleneck_result.md`](../../../research/2026-07-09_x36-node-bottleneck_result.md)).

The knife is the same one the standing review instrument already carries as

## Honest coda — corpus-shape or vacuum-shape?

Everything above is the shape of the mathematics **as the corpus has it**: it **derives forms and
imports values**. The two fixed points, the identity interior, the even kernel, and the three
closed generators are audited facts about the artifact we have built. They do **not** by themselves
establish that the *vacuum* is so shaped — a value-importing echo would exhibit exactly this
signature (a self-consistent form-machine fed calibrated scales). **Which it is** — the shape of
the vacuum, or the shape of the corpus's echo — is precisely what **identity-breaking forward
tests** adjudicate: a test that breaks an identity on *decorrelated / swept / edge-crossing /
port-toggled* inputs and returns a **parameter-free FORM the import basis cannot supply** is the
only thing that distinguishes a chord from an echo. Until such a forward break lands, this leaf is
a **review instrument**, not a claim: it tells you where the discriminating content *can* live and
which PASS criteria are identities wearing a gate's coat.
