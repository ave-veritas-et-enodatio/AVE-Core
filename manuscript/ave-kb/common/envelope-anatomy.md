# The Envelope Three-Surface Anatomy (wall · balance shell · knee/dress edge)

> **Sector / regime declaration (substrate-native-first).**
> **MODE:** static rest-configuration of a bound soliton (the electron is the pilot); no dynamics.
> **SECTOR:** the boundary the substrate sees carries the three boundary observables $\mathcal{M}$ (A1 dilatation mass) $\perp$ $\mathcal{Q}$ (Cosserat $(2,3)$ winding charge) $\perp$ $\mathcal{J}$ (spin) — see [`boundary-observables-m-q-j.md`](boundary-observables-m-q-j.md); the three surfaces below are **radial loci on the real-space saturation profile** $S(A(r))$, NOT phase-space contours (A46 phase-space-vs-real-space discipline: these are `spatial-Brillouin`-axis lengths).
> **PHASE-STATE:** the innermost surface (the wall) is the **fully-yielded** locus $S(A)\to 0$ (the one dissipative-adjacent / TIR boundary); the outer two surfaces (balance shell, knee) sit in the **sub-yield, lossless-reactive** interior of the profile. Declaring the phase-state matters: a "reflection" at the knee (sub-yield, near-matched) is a different object from the $|\Gamma|=1$ mirror at the wall (fully-yielded).

**Status: FORM minted per Grant Ruling 8 (option C, in-chat 2026-07-14; ruling record: PR #695 docket continuation).** What is canonical here is the **three-surface FORM** — that a bound soliton's boundary region is not one radius but three physically-distinct radial surfaces with distinct roles. **Every NUMERICAL value below stays measured / gate-registered — nothing numerical is promoted to SOLID by this mint** (the radii ride CODATA-derived $\ell_{node} = \hbar/m_e c$; see the per-surface status tags).

### (i) The wall — the fully-yielded $\lvert\Gamma\rvert = 1$ mirror

The wall is the substrate's observability boundary: the locus where Axiom 4's kernel reaches $S(A)\to 0$ locally, giving total internal reflection ($\lvert\Gamma\rvert = 1$) and hiding the interior. It carries exactly the three integrated boundary observables $\mathcal{M},\mathcal{Q},\mathcal{J}$ ([`boundary-observables-m-q-j.md`](boundary-observables-m-q-j.md), clm-ze4clw / clm-lv3uw1). The **sign** of the $\Gamma = -1$ wall (magnetic-branch $\mu_{eff}\to 0$ short vs the electric-branch $\varepsilon_{eff}\to 0$ open) is the **ruled-degenerate SIGN / spin selector, NOT an independent branch** (the wall-branch fork H3 = B3 DEGENERATE, #260; mass = A1 settled independently). **Ground-state floor identity (ROLE-1 $\equiv$ ROLE-2):** for the ground state the observability wall coincides with the **ropelength floor** $\ell_{node}/2\pi \approx 61$ fm (the minimum closed-loop / horn-torus tube-radius scale; [`trampoline-framework.md`](trampoline-framework.md):687 §4.4 — this is the tube-radius / ropelength-floor scale, **not** the classical electron radius $r_e = \alpha\,\ell_{node} \approx 2.82$ fm). Excited states lift the wall off the floor (the floor is the ground-state minimum, not a universal wall radius).

### (ii) The balance shell — the $\sigma$-opposite-equal crossing

The electron admits a self-consistent **hollow-vortex** description ([`../vol2/particle-physics/ch01-topological-matter/hollow-vortex-binding.md`](../vol2/particle-physics/ch01-topological-matter/hollow-vortex-binding.md):49, clm-hvb7q3): a cavitated core drilled open by the $(2,3)$ Cosserat circulation (outward $\propto \Gamma^2/R^3$) and closed by the void$\leftrightarrow$vacuum surface tension (inward $\sigma/R$), with the crossing at $R^\ast = \Gamma/\sqrt\sigma \approx 1.6\,\ell_{node}$. **Softness rider (do not over-read the number):** $R^\ast/\ell_{node} = n\cdot M_{edge}/\sqrt\sigma$ is a product of three $O(1)$ numbers and $\ell_{node}$ is the theory's ONLY length, so the $O(1)$ landing is **DIMENSIONALLY FORCED — a consistency check, not a discriminating test** (`hollow-vortex-binding.md`:133; clm-hvb7q3 caveats). **Status — CONJECTURED $\equiv$ the wall (Grant Ruling 6):** the knee is the proportional limit ($\Delta S = \alpha$); the **wall is the fully-yielded $S\to 0$ mirror**; the balance shell is conjectured to BE the wall (the $\sigma$-balance crossing and the fully-yielded mirror the same surface). Whether the balance shell is a distinct supra-node surface or coincides with the wall is **gate-(b)** — the value-side adjudication is gated.

### (iii) The knee / dress edge — the $\Delta S = \alpha$ proportional limit

The knee is the substrate's **proportional limit**: the amplitude $A^2 = A^2_{yield} = 2\alpha$ at which the saturation kernel departs linear response and corrections / particle-to-particle dressing / external load-transfer begin. Engine authority: [`chiral_lattice_v10.py`](../../../src/ave/core/chiral_lattice_v10.py):29-30 (`A_YIELD_SQ = 2·α`, "three-regime knee") + :56 (the saturation gain $s = \sqrt{1 - A^2}$). At the knee, $S = \sqrt{1 - 2\alpha} \approx 0.9927$, so the strain-cost of reaching the knee is $\Delta S = 1 - \sqrt{1 - 2\alpha} \approx \alpha$ (leading order: $\sqrt{1-2\alpha} \approx 1-\alpha$; numerically $0.007329$ vs $\alpha = 0.007297$, agreement to $\sim\alpha/2$). The reflection off the knee is **small** ($\lvert\Gamma\rvert \ll 1$, the near-matched sub-yield regime — distinct from the wall's $\lvert\Gamma\rvert = 1$). **Radius — REGISTERED CHECK:** the hypothesis that the particle-dressing **collar edge $\equiv$ the knee** is under test by a sibling lane; the knee's radius is not yet pinned.

## The §45 A-vs-B fork dissolves into named surfaces

The long-open **§45 "size" A-vs-B fork** — is the soliton's canonical size a *sub-node charge-core feature* (**Resolution A**) or a *supra-node body envelope* (**Resolution B**)? — was a **false binary**, because there is **not one envelope but three surfaces**. The FORM mint dissolves it (this is the brief's "Sense A ↔ wall/floor, Sense B ↔ balance-or-knee", named precisely against the §45 resolutions):

- **Resolution A (sub-node charge-core)** $\leftrightarrow$ the **wall / ropelength floor** (surface i) — the ground-state observability boundary at $\ell_{node}/2\pi \approx 61$ fm, which is a **sub-node** scale ($< \ell_{node}$).
- **Resolution B (supra-node body envelope)** $\leftrightarrow$ the **balance shell or the knee** (surfaces ii/iii) — genuinely distinct **supra-node** surfaces ($\gtrsim \ell_{node}$) sitting outside the wall.

So Resolution A and Resolution B are **different surfaces of one anatomy**, not competing answers to "the size." *(This §45 Resolution-A/B fork is distinct from the `def-envl0p` **Sense-A/B** overload — real-space shell vs phasor-gain — which the anatomy does not touch; the wall is a real-space shell, i.e. `def-envl0p` Sense A, and separately the §45 Resolution A.)* **This is a FORM-level dissolution only: the VALUES (which radius each excited state's wall sits at, whether the balance shell is distinct from the wall) stay gate-(b)-dependent and are NOT promoted to SOLID.** The register `def-` nodes ([`vocabulary-register.md`](vocabulary-register.md): r_env def-088f0d, node-Nyquist-size-boundary def-e0cd83, envelope def-envl0p, and the anatomy def-anat3s) now reference this anatomy; the length-canonical mint (r_env / node-Nyquist proposed → SOLID) remains Grant-gated.
