# Lattice Extreme-Map — One Kernel, Two Extremes (the BH Rationality Test)

The single Axiom-4 saturation kernel $S(A)=\sqrt{1-A^2}$ has **two** extremes, and the macroscopic objects of the framework are what the lattice does at each. Pushed to **compression** ($A^2\to1$) it builds the $\Gamma=-1$ confinement wall — the electron and the black hole. Pushed to **rarefaction** ($\bar\rho\to-1/\varphi$) it tears — cavitation, the fourth object. The **rationality test**: does the lattice, taken to its compression extreme, reproduce General Relativity — the established macroscopic theory it must recover if it is a faithful substrate? It does, to $<2\%$ with zero free parameters. That is the test passing; the discriminators are where it departs.
