## §2 — SYM pins the ratio, not the product

A bond TL has two combinations:

\[
Z=\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}},\qquad c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}.
\]

Gravity-class loading is **SYM**: \(\mu\) and \(\varepsilon\) take the **same** factor so \(Z'=Z_0\) and \(\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}=0\) ([`achromatic-impedance-matching.md`](achromatic-impedance-matching.md):20–28; [`clm-07kd5v`](../../claim-quality.md); Axiom 3). Independent TL \(L\) vs \(C\) (different scale factors) would move \(Z\) and make the well a reflector. That is the **ASYM** channel (static-\(E\), Meissner-asymmetric), not gravity.

So bond TL \(L\) and \(C\) **may both grade**; they **may not grade apart**. The leftover candidate knob is the gap \(\Omega^2=S_\gamma/C\), which no impedance theorem constrains. \(Z=\sqrt{L/C}\) pinned is definitional for that TL pair. **Which** pair \(Z\) pins is a channel question, and the answer differs per channel — §2.1.

### §2.1 — Channel scope of the bond-TL identification (repair, 2026-09-06)

A **bond** is not one transmission line. Canon draws the medium as **three wired reactance channels** — \(Z_{\mathrm{EM}}\) (T2 transverse), \(Z_{\mathrm{shear}}\) (deviatoric \(G\)), \(Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}\) (dilatation \(K\)) — [`def-gv1net`](../../../common/vocabulary-register.md):715–726, tabulated at [`three-channel-impedances.md`](../../../vol9/ch4-dc-electrical-characteristics/three-channel-impedances.md):20–22. Only \(Z_{\mathrm{EM}}\equiv Z_0\) is an **electrical** impedance (\(\Omega\), V/A); \(Z_{\mathrm{shear}}\) and \(Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}\) are **mechanical/acoustic** (Pa·s/m) and are **not in \(Z_0\) units** ([`resonant-lc-solitons.md`](../../../vol4/circuit-theory/ch1-vacuum-circuit-analysis/resonant-lc-solitons.md):133). So there is no single \((L,C)\) pair whose ratio is "the bond's" impedance, and no single bond-TL \(L,C\) that \(\mu,\varepsilon\) could be mapped onto wholesale.

**On the channel this lane's prereg names, the identification is canon's own.** The frozen prereg §0 row 1 fixes the observable as "hop delay / `c_eff` of the **gapless T2/EM channel**" ([`research/2026-08-28_qpoint-lc-constitutive_prereg-FROZEN.md`](../../../../../research/2026-08-28_qpoint-lc-constitutive_prereg-FROZEN.md):19). For that channel, [`translation-circuit.md`](../../../common/translation-tables/translation-circuit.md) §10.1 tabulates \(L=\mu_0\) ("series line inductance / inertia") and \(C=\varepsilon_0\) ("shunt line capacitance / compliance") as **Class A identities** at `:811` and `:812`; the rendered mirror is [`glossary.md`](../../../../../docs/glossary.md):96 ("Line-property constant … \(L=\mu_0\), \(C=\varepsilon_0\) … Class A identities").

**Therefore, scope the refusal:**

| Object | Is bond TL \((L,C)\leftrightarrow(\mu,\varepsilon)\) licensed? | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| The **whole three-channel bond** | **No.** Three channels, three impedances, two unit domains — no one \((L,C)\) exists to identify. Phase-1, open. | `def-gv1net`; `three-channel-impedances.md`:20–22; `resonant-lc-solitons.md`:133 |
| The **gapless T2/EM channel** (prereg §0) | **Yes.** \(L=\mu_0\), \(C=\varepsilon_0\) is a **Class A identity** already in canon. | `translation-circuit.md`:811–812; `glossary.md`:96 |

The bare fence *"identifying bond TL \(L,C\) with \(\mu,\varepsilon\) is an identification, not licensed"* — as it landed on this branch at `f943a034` — is **right for the whole bond and wrong by omission for the channel the prereg names**. Every fence site on this branch now carries the channel qualifier. This is a **scoping repair, not a physics ruling**: nothing here decides whether the substrate assigns \(S(\varepsilon_{11})\) independently (Forks F1–F3 stay Grant-routed), and no solidity moves.

**Provenance of the fence, recorded.** The pre-flip wording at `d1b55e87` read *"so hop \(L\) and \(C\) **co-scale with \(n\)**"* — unconditional, and unqualified in the other direction. `f943a034` inverted it the same day under an "option-1 audit." ## §3 — Under what strain do \(L\) and \(C\) scale linearly?

**They do not, from Axiom 4.** The kernel is even. "Linear" here is a homonym ([`def-ln3str`](../../../common/vocabulary-register.md)). Three senses:

### Sense K — kernel (Ax4): no linear term; Regime I \(\approx\) constant

\[
S(r)=\sqrt{1-r^2}=1-\frac{r^2}{2}-\frac{r^4}{8}-\cdots
\]

([`four-regimes.md`](../../../vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch7-regime-map/four-regimes.md):110–114.) First correction is \(O(r^2)\). The A1 varactor is the same even expansion: \(C_{\mathrm{eff}}=C_0/S=C_0\bigl(1+\tfrac12(V/V_{\mathrm{snap}})^2+\cdots\bigr)\) ([`nonlinear-vacuum-capacitance.md`](../../../vol4/circuit-theory/ch1-vacuum-circuit-analysis/nonlinear-vacuum-capacitance.md):33–34).

**Regime I** is \(r<\sqrt{2\alpha}\approx0.1208\), where \(\Delta S=r^2/2\) is sub-\(\alpha\) and is dropped ([`four-regimes.md`](../../../vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch7-regime-map/four-regimes.md):26, :35–41). The SYM constitutive table then reads \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{eff}}=\varepsilon_0\), \(\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}=\mu_0\), \(C_{\mathrm{eff}}=C_0\) ([`regime-equation-sets.md`](../../../vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch7-regime-map/regime-equation-sets.md):19–25). Solar-surface gravitational strain is \(\varepsilon_{11}=1.486\times10^{-5}\) — **deep Regime I** ([`domain-catalog.md`](../../../vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch7-regime-map/domain-catalog.md):50). At the limb, kernel \(\Delta S\approx\varepsilon_{11}^2/2\sim10^{-10}\) (control parameter \(r=\varepsilon_{11}\), [`domain-catalog.md`](../../../vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch7-regime-map/domain-catalog.md):47). That is the **kernel correction**, not a derived bond \(\delta L/L\) or \(\delta C/C\).

Solar-system gravity is therefore **kernel-frozen in Regime I**: the kernel is dropped; SYM-table column I (\(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{eff}}\), \(\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}\), \(C_{\mathrm{eff}}\)) is constant to working precision. That is not "\(L,C\propto\varepsilon_{11}\)." Bond TL \(L,C\) in §2–§3 means the TL pair (\(L',C'\)) unless named as A1 \(C_{\mathrm{eff}}\) or T2 \(\varepsilon\).

### Sense P — photoelastic / Op19: linear in \(\varepsilon_{11}\), still Regime I, different object

Op19 is \(n=1+\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}\cdot\varepsilon_{11}\) ([`operators.md`](../../../common/operators.md):59). Under SYM, \(\mu'=n\mu_0\), \(\varepsilon'=n\varepsilon_0\) ([`achromatic-impedance-matching.md`](achromatic-impedance-matching.md):20–28). Identifying bond TL \(L,C\) with those \(\mu,\varepsilon\) is **channel-scoped** (§2.1). On the **gapless T2/EM channel** the prereg names, \(L=\mu_0\) and \(C=\varepsilon_0\) is canon's own **Class A identity** ([`translation-circuit.md`](../../../common/translation-tables/translation-circuit.md):811–812; [`glossary.md`](../../../../../docs/glossary.md):96), so on that channel bond TL \(L\) and \(C\) **do** co-scale with \(n\) and the leading index is **linear in \(\varepsilon_{11}\)**. For the **whole three-channel bond** the identification is not available at all (Phase-1; bond-as-TL at [`translation-circuit.md`](../../../common/translation-tables/translation-circuit.md):98). At the limb, \((2/7)\varepsilon_{11}=4.25\times10^{-6}\) (\(\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}\) from Op19 \(\times\) solar \(\varepsilon_{11}\) at [`domain-catalog.md`](../../../vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch7-regime-map/domain-catalog.md):50).

This is **not** the kernel's Taylor series. Photoelasticity is linear in strain; the kernel's index shift is quadratic in amplitude ([`research/2026-07-31_anisotropy-observable_scoping.md`](../../../../../research/2026-07-31_anisotropy-observable_scoping.md):893). The gravity-linearity audit: GR-exact observables are licensed by a **linear projection** of strain; **the Ax4 kernel appears nowhere in that chain** ([`research/2026-08-11_gravity-linearity-audit_result.md`](../../../../../research/2026-08-11_gravity-linearity-audit_result.md):24). \(\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}=2/7\) is **GR-imported** (#261). The rank-4 map \(p_{ijkl}\) that would convert strain to index in a real medium is **unnamed** (F-B4).

**Regime of Sense P:** the same weak-field / cold / sub-yield band as Sense K (solar-system \(\varepsilon_{11}\ll\sqrt{2\alpha}\)). The coupling is linear **because it is Op19**, not because Regime I linearized the kernel.

### Sense Z — SYM co-scale: \(L\propto C\) on the T2/EM channel, at every gravity-class strain

**Sense Z carries the same channel condition as Sense P (§2.1), and is stated conditionally for that reason.** On the **T2/EM channel** — where \(Z=\sqrt{L/C}=Z_0=\sqrt{\mu_0/\varepsilon_0}\) is canon’s Class-A identity ([`translation-circuit.md`](../../../common/translation-tables/translation-circuit.md):810–812) — bond TL \(L\) and \(C\) remain **proportional** (ratio \(=Z_0^2\)) from Regime I through yield, until the loading class leaves SYM. That is "linear in each other," not linear in strain. Near \(A\to1\), both still co-scale; \(c_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and \(\Omega\) grade strongly via the **kernel** \(S(A)\). Independent TL \(L\) vs \(C\) on that channel stays forbidden.

For the **whole three-channel bond**, Sense Z is **not** an unconditional definitional truth: \(Z_{\mathrm{shear}}\) and \(Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}\) are mechanical/acoustic and not in \(Z_0\) units ([`resonant-lc-solitons.md`](../../../vol4/circuit-theory/ch1-vacuum-circuit-analysis/resonant-lc-solitons.md):133), and achromatic matching’s \(\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}=0\) is a statement about the EM channel only ([`achromatic-impedance-matching.md`](achromatic-impedance-matching.md):20–28). There is no one \(L/C\) for "the bond" to pin. The earlier unconditional phrasing of Sense Z is superseded by this one; it lives in git.
