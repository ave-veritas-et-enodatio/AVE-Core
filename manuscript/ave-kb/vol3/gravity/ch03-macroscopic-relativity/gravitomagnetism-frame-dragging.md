## Gravitomagnetism: Frame Dragging as Mutual Inductance

In standard General Relativity, a rotating massive body drags the geometric fabric of spacetime along with it---a phenomenon known as the Lense-Thirring effect (Gravitomagnetism). In the AVE framework, because the physical vacuum operates dynamically as an LC network (the substrate), this effect is the **macroscopic mutual-inductance** form of the *same* native process described microscopically in the companion leaf [`../ch02-general-relativity/frame-dragging-impedance-convolution.md`](../ch02-general-relativity/frame-dragging-impedance-convolution.md): the rotating mass convolutes the static gravitational refractive index $n(r)$ (the $Z_{local}$ impedance gradient) into an angle-dependent, *non-reciprocal* bias of the surrounding LC network. **"Impedance convolution" (Ch.2) and "mutual inductance" ($M_{12}$, this leaf) are two names for one native-EE mechanism — not two competing pictures.** This leaf carries the macroscopic $M_{12}$ statement; the microscopic mechanism, the Kerr result box, and the D-shadow derivation are owned by the Ch.2 leaf.

As a massive macroscopic boundary (a spinning planet) rotates, its circulating current ($I_{mass}$) couples to the adjacent spatial metric layer via **macroscopic mutual inductance** ($M_{12}$). Macroscopic inductive momentum transport continues across the lattice, inducing a localised, steady-state, non-reciprocal impedance bias in the surrounding vacuum. Two scalings must be kept distinct: the gravitomagnetic *vector potential* (the induced rotational vector potential of the LC network) decays as an inverse square ($A_g \propto 1/r^2$), while the frame-dragging *angular velocity* — the physically observed Lense-Thirring precession $\Omega_{LT}$ — is its curl and therefore decays as $\Omega_{LT} \propto r^{-3}$ in the far field, consistent with the Kerr $\omega(r) = 2Mar/(r^2+a^2)^2 \to r^{-3}$ result box in the Ch.2 leaf.

Per the language `clm-rd9cjm` already uses (claim-quality.md:46), the framework here **"reproduces the standard result via metric refraction, not an independent novel prediction."** This is a **category-(iii) consistency check / recovery-of-known**, peer-with-standard (every optical-metric / post-Newtonian treatment recovers weak-field GR the same way) — reported honestly as a feature, *not* an AVE-distinct prediction. The AVE-distinct signature is deferred (see below).
