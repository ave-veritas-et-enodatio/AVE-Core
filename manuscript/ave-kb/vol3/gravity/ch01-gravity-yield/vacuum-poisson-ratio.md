> **[Resultbox]** *Vacuum Poisson Ratio*
>
> $$
> \nu_{vac} = \frac{3K_{vac} - 2G_{vac}}{2(3K_{vac} + G_{vac})} = \frac{6G_{vac} - 2G_{vac}}{2(6G_{vac} + G_{vac})} = \frac{4}{14} = \frac{2}{7}
> $$

---

**Anisotropy tag (PR #506; value unchanged).** $\nu_{\mathrm{Hill}} = 2/7$ is the
**isotropic (Voigt–Reuss–Hill) average** scalar at $K = 2G$; the real srs/K4 lattice is
materially **anisotropic** (Zener $A \approx 1.23$ at $\rho^\* \approx 9.77$, #506), so $2/7$
is an **averaging choice, not a per-direction lattice output** — the isotropic-identity and
direct-anisotropic-mode readings pick different bond ratios. **KEEP-BOTH:** the
diamond-z4-instrument / direct-reading numbers ($A \approx 1.755$, #586) are a *different
lattice + convention* and do **not** supersede this canonical srs-Hill value.

See `research/2026-07-04_srs-elastic-tensor_result.md` (srs Cauchy elastic tensor,
one-parameter family in $\rho = k_a/k_s$; $\nu_{\mathrm{Hill}} = 2/7$ only at externally-supplied
$\rho^\* \approx 9.7734$).
