### Derivation

$$
\boxed{\alpha_s = \alpha^{d/n} = \alpha^{3/7} \approx 0.1214}
$$

where $d = 3$ (spatial dimensions) and $n = 7$ (compliance modes per node, from $\nu_{vac} = 2/7$).
