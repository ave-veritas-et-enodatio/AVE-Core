## The Macroscopic Halo Offset

Because the $4\alpha$ core acts as a monolithic, closed nucleus, the external Tritium nodes bind dynamically to the gravitational gradient projecting outward from one of the underlying Alpha vertices.

By executing the semiconductor junction model targeting the empirical CODATA nuclear mass of Fluorine-19 ($17692.302$ MeV), the solver fits the physical separation distance between the core and the halo. It converges to a $0.0000\%$ fitting tolerance on the per-nucleus halo scalar when the Tritium triangle is driven radially outward to $R_{halo} = 398.5d$ from the target alpha's barycenter (the topology is $(Z,A)$-forced; $R$ is the one fitted degree of freedom).

This sheer distance---spanning hundreds of femtometers---creates a highly asymmetric, reactive gravitational whip. This extended topological moment arm directly dictates Fluorine's profound electronegativity and aggressive chemical binding profile.
