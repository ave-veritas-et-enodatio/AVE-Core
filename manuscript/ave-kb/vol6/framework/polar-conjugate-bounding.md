# Polar Conjugate Topo-Kinematic Bounding Limit (Heavy Elements)

Heavy elements ($Z \geq 31$, beginning at Gallium and Germanium) introduce the $d^{10}$ internal sub-shells, forming nested complete torus-knot geometric arrays within the core. This forces these elements mathematically out of the small-signal approximations used for Period-3 elements and directly into the **Large Signal Regime ($M > 100$)**. When the tracking transversal valence wave encounters the identical closely-packed boundaries of these internal continuous sub-shells, the effective impedance bound drops abruptly inward ($Z_{out} < Z_{in}$), mapping mathematically to a **Polar Conjugate Mirror** (a perfect half-wave $\pi$-phase flip shift where $\Gamma < 0$).

## Total Internal Reflection Compartmentalization

In the idealized limit the full inner shell acts as a Total Internal Reflection (TIR) boundary: the valence string reflects outward with $\Gamma < 0$ and the Phase-C crossover sets scattering penetration to zero (`Y_loss = 0.0`) for fully-mirrored geometries.

Under TIR compartmentalization the continuous LC string is partitioned into isolated topological segments. The macroscopic cavity integrates to a base scale ($E_{base}$) for unbroken continuous space, but the bounded valence string fits only in the outermost geometric void:

$$L_{val} = \frac{L_{total}}{\text{core\_d\_knots} + 1}$$

And since bounding resonance frequency and energy scale strictly as $E \propto 1/L$, the structurally truncated wave resonates optimally by the exact integer metric:

> **[Resultbox]** *Topo-Kinematic Valence Energy Scaling*
>
> $$E_{val} = E_{base} \times (\text{core\_d\_knots} + 1)$$

Compressing the integration baseline by this integer metric converts the $\sim 70\%$ baseline underestimate into sub-$5\%$ residuals across the Period-4 $d^{10}$-enclosed elements (Ga--Kr); the heavy-element catalog extends this to $Z = 86$.
