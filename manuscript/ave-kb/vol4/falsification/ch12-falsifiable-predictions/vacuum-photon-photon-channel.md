Both magnitudes are stated for $\omega\ll\omega_0=m_ec^2/\hbar$ — the band containing every registered prediction of the Letter (optical pump $\sim1.55$ eV, X-ray probe $9$–$13$ keV).

## Domain of validity — a low-frequency EFT, and why collider LbL does not bound it

The kernel is a **low-frequency effective description** of the vacuum's dielectric response, with response scale $\omega_0=m_ec^2/\hbar$. Its saturable enhancement is defined **only for fields and probes well below** $\omega_0$. It is **not** a Lorentz-invariant *contact* nonlinear electrodynamics defined at arbitrary energy.

- **The collider four-photon measurement is out of domain.** ATLAS/CMS elastic $\gamma\gamma\to\gamma\gamma$ in ultraperipheral Pb–Pb ($\sqrt s\gtrsim6$ GeV $\simeq10^4\,\hbar\omega_0$, SM-consistent — arXiv 1904.03536) probes the four-photon amplitude at the electron scale, where every low-frequency four-photon description (Euler–Heisenberg included) is compared to the **full electron-box amplitude**, not to a low-frequency contact vertex. Above $\omega_0$ the four-photon physics is carried by the **shared electron-pair box** — the same physics QED and AVE share — so the SM-consistent collider result IS that shared physics.
- **A contact-NED promotion would be excluded, and is DISCLAIMED.** Were the kernel wrongly promoted to a Lorentz-invariant contact NED defined at collider energies, the light-by-light bound on the Born–Infeld scale ($\Lambda\gtrsim100$ GeV, Ellis–Mavromatos–You 2017, PRL 118 261802) would exclude it by many orders of magnitude ($\sim11$ orders via the $\chi^{(3)}$-ratio method; $\sim22$ orders via the contact-BI method, $\Lambda_{\mathrm{SVE}}\approx0.27$ MeV vs $\Lambda\gtrsim100$ GeV). The model's collider consistency rests on its **effective-theory domain**, stated plainly — not on a derivation that "survives ATLAS."
