## Chirality and Antimatter Disintegration

Because the substrate LC network supports polarised transverse EM waves, it breaks geometric symmetry between left and right. Electric charge polarity is defined as the **Topological Twist Direction** of the closed magnetic standing wave. Canonically — because the corpus defines charge **as** Beltrami helicity ([`master-equation.md`](../../../vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/master-equation.md):20, $H_{\mathrm{bel}}=\int\boldsymbol{\omega}\cdot(\nabla\times\boldsymbol{\omega})$; the charge-sign convention follows the charge-defining quantity) — the electron ($e^-$) carries **left-handed** Beltrami helicity (the LH content of the confined flux, per [`pair-production-axiom-derivation.md`](pair-production-axiom-derivation.md):27,79); the positron ($e^+$) is physically identical but wound with **right-handed** helicity. The conjugate reading "$e^-$ = a **right-handed unknot**" is the real-space **writhe** face of the *same* conserved twist budget: by the **Călugăreanu relation** $\mathrm{Lk} = \mathrm{Tw} + \mathrm{Wr}$, the internal twist $\mathrm{Tw}$ (the charge-defining LH Beltrami helicity) and the real-space loop **writhe** $\mathrm{Wr}$ are two lawful readings of the one conserved linking number $\mathrm{Lk}$ (= charge). Both readings are correct; the **helicity sign is canonical** (Grant 2026-07-09, R1).

> **Tag (D4, sector + provenance).** The twist-direction ⇄ charge-sign identification is **FORM-derived** (charge sign = winding / dislocation handedness, Axiom 2; the winding is the integer $\mathrm{Link}(\partial\Omega,F)\in\mathbb{Z}$, `def-3638f2`), and it lives in the **T2 Cosserat micro-rotation** grade — orthogonal to the A1 dilatation-mass ([`master-equation.md`](../../../vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/master-equation.md):20, A1⊥T2; `def-5d2b8a`). The *sign labelling* is now **settled** (Grant 2026-07-09, R1): the canonical charge-sign is the **Beltrami/helicity** sign, so $e^-$ = **left-handed** (matching [`pair-production-axiom-derivation.md`](pair-production-axiom-derivation.md):27,79, Beltrami-flux chirality). This leaf's earlier "$e^-$ = right-handed unknot" is the conjugate **writhe** reading of the *same* conserved twist budget ($\mathrm{Lk} = \mathrm{Tw} + \mathrm{Wr}$; both readings lawful, helicity sign canonical). See the **Handedness Register (D4)** below.

By Mazur's Theorem, the connected sum of a left-handed knot and a right-handed knot produces a composite "Square Knot." In a purely continuous mathematical manifold, matter-antimatter annihilation is topologically impossible because geometrical lines cannot mechanically pass through each other.

The AVE framework resolves this mathematical paradox via **Optical Phase Cancellation**. When an electron ($+\boldsymbol{\omega}$) and positron ($-\boldsymbol{\omega}$) collide, their localised inductive scale and rotational phase frequencies are identical, but their polarisation states are inverted.

At the moment of overlap, the opposed internal electromagnetic standing waves destructively interfere to zero ($\boldsymbol{\omega} + (-\boldsymbol{\omega}) = 0$). The topological optical boundary condition confining the resonant loop snaps. The localised inductive energy, previously trapped within the closed LC resonance of the $0_1$ unknot, unwinds. Unbound from the loop, the stored electromagnetic energy unspools into linear transverse vector waves (gamma-ray photons). The energy balance is:

> **[Resultbox]** *Antimatter Disintegration*
>
> $$
> e^{-}(+\boldsymbol{\omega}) + e^{+}(-\boldsymbol{\omega}) \longrightarrow 2\gamma \qquad E_{total} = 2 m_e c^2 = 1.022 \text{ MeV}
> $$

"Mass" is not converted into "energy"; the geometric phase of the standing optical rotation is severed by its antipode, freeing the confined light.

> **Tag (peer, not chord).** The observable $2\gamma$ at $2 m_e c^2 = 1.022$ MeV is the **standard-QED result** — AVE claims **no** distinct cross-section, branching ratio, or kinematic distribution (peer, not an AVE-distinct chord; `clm-hb2xmj` confidence 0.30, "asserted mechanism"). The energy balance is a **trivial identity** given $m_e$ (`consistency-vs-emergence`: identity-class). What AVE offers here is a re-interpretation of the *mechanism* (optical phase cancellation severing the topological boundary), which is **asserted**, not derived — see the claim-quality caveat at [`vol2/claim-quality.md`](../../claim-quality.md) (clm-hb2xmj).

### Adjudication — this leaf's "$e^-$ right-handed" (:10) vs `pair-production` "$e^-$ LH" (:27/:79)

Both labels attach to the electron's **single charge-sign discriminator** and assign it **opposite** handedness:

- **This leaf** (:10, *original pre-R1 reading*): $e^-$ = **right-handed**, on attributes **(2)+(3) welded** (real-space writhe of the $0_1$ unknot, *identified with* the twist direction of the closed magnetic standing wave) — R1 **un-welds** these into conjugate faces (see below).
- **`pair-production`** (:27, :79): $e^-$ = **left-handed**, on attribute **(3)/(4)** (Beltrami helical flux / contra-rotating vortex sense).

The labels **overlap at attribute (3)** — the magnetic-flux twist / Beltrami helicity — which is the *same physical attribute*, and the two leaves *appeared* to give it the *opposite* sign for $e^-$. **R1 now fixes exactly this writhe↔helicity relation**: by the **Călugăreanu** relation $\mathrm{Lk} = \mathrm{Tw} + \mathrm{Wr}$, "RH **writhe**" (attribute (2), $\mathrm{Wr}$) and "LH **Beltrami**" (attribute (3), $\mathrm{Tw}$) are **both correct** for the one $e^-$ — they are conjugate faces of the single conserved $\mathrm{Lk}$ (= charge), so the leaf's "RH" was the writhe face, not the Beltrami face. The def-node (`def-7c3f9e`) remains `status: ambiguous` **only** for its *other* open seam (whether the engine realizes the chiral space group, attribute (1); the dynamical $\kappa_{chiral}$ loading), which R1 does **not** touch.

**Resolution (Grant 2026-07-09, R1 — the previously-open SAME-ATTRIBUTE clash is now adjudicated).** The canonical charge-sign is the **Beltrami/helicity** sign, *because the corpus defines charge AS Beltrami helicity* ([`master-equation.md`](../../../vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/master-equation.md):20 — the charge-sign convention follows the charge-defining quantity). Canonically therefore **$e^-$ = left-handed** (the [`pair-production-axiom-derivation.md`](pair-production-axiom-derivation.md):27,79 label). This leaf's "$e^-$ = right-handed unknot" (:10) is **not wrong** — it is the conjugate **writhe** ($\mathrm{Wr}$, real-space loop geometry) reading of the *same* conserved twist budget whose flux face is the LH Beltrami twist ($\mathrm{Tw}$), reconciled by the **Călugăreanu** relation $\mathrm{Lk} = \mathrm{Tw} + \mathrm{Wr}$ (both readings lawful; helicity sign canonical). The leaf opening (:10) and the D4 Tag have been relabelled accordingly, and a one-line register crossref recording the LH label as canonical-sign is added to [`pair-production-axiom-derivation.md`](pair-production-axiom-derivation.md) (R1(4); no other edit to that leaf).

> **Physical note (Grant-walked, register-grade; adjudicated framing).** The $\Gamma = -1$ TIR confining boundary's reflection **magnitude** is **handedness-blind** — both enantiomers ($e^-$ LH, $e^+$ RH) confine identically, which is the register-level statement of **CPT mass equality** ($m_{e^-} = m_{e^+}$). What carries a handed choice is the wall-**branch** (short- vs open-circuit termination), the **$\mu$-sign selector** (#260, degenerate B3 — a sign/spin selector, **not** a sector branch) that pairs with the sign DOF. Handedness itself is **internal content**: the twist $\mathrm{Tw}$ *is* the charge-defining Beltrami helicity; the writhe $\mathrm{Wr}$ is the **real-space geometric expression** of that internal content; and the conserved far-field quantity the electron **projects/broadcasts** (a static electron does **not** radiate) is the linking number $\mathrm{Lk}$ (= charge).
