% --- Line-breaking discipline ---
% \sloppy permits TeX to stretch interword glue to avoid Overfull \hbox
% on narrative paragraphs with long inline \texttt{paths} (research/file.md
% references, src/scripts/ paths, KB cross-refs). Without this, the
% post-build margin gate at src/scripts/check_latex_margins.py:6
% trips on cosmetic narrative-only overruns even though the PDF renders.
% Affects line spacing slightly; preferred over inserting \allowbreak /
% \path{} at every long-texttt site.
\sloppy
% \emergencystretch gives TeX additional flexibility when \sloppy alone is
% insufficient: pdfTeX can stretch by this amount per line if needed to
% avoid overfull boxes, before resorting to overrun. 3em is reasonable for
% narrative paragraphs with many \texttt{} run-ins; quality-gate threshold
% in check_latex_margins.py governs final cosmetic acceptability.
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{5em}
