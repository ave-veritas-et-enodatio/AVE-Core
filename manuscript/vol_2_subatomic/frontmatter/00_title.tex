This document maps the empirical particle zoo to specific, deterministic topological fluid knots within the LC Condensate.

\begin{abstract}
    Where the Standard Model uses point particles and gauge fields, AVE models matter as continuous topological structures in the substrate.
    
    \textbf{Volume II: The Subatomic Lattice} develops the Standard-Model mass hierarchy from discrete knot topology and dielectric saturation, modelling leptons as $0_1$ unknots, baryons as $6^3_2$ Borromean linkages, and neutrinos as dispersive twisted $0_1$ unknots, bound by the Faddeev-Skyrme energy functional:
    \begin{equation}
        E = \int \left( \frac{1}{2}\partial_\mu \vec{n} \cdot \partial^\mu \vec{n} + \frac{1}{4e^2}(\partial_\mu \vec{n} \times \partial_\nu \vec{n})^2 \right) d^3x
    \end{equation}
    On this basis the proton/electron mass ratio ($\approx 1836.14$), the strong nuclear force, and the Higgs mechanism are developed as structural properties of the bounded network, with the fine-structure constant $\alpha$ taken as a calibration input (see the honest-scope notes in the common foreword).
\end{abstract}
