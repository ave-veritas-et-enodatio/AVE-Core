\section{GW Propagation as Impedance Modulation}
\label{sec:gw_propagation}

Gravitational waves are transverse inductive shear waves in the LC
lattice --- the same medium governed by the same operators. In accordance with the \textbf{Symmetric Scaling} axiom required to preserve a uniform optical vacuum, gravity scales both the local permittivity and permeability symmetrically via the refractive metric $n(r) = (1 - r_s/r)^{-1}$:
\begin{align}
    \varepsilon_{eff}(r) &= \varepsilon_0 \cdot n(r) \\
    \mu_{eff}(r) &= \mu_0 \cdot n(r)
\end{align}
where $r_s = 2GM/c^2$. Because both components scale proportionally, the macroscopic gravitational impedance remains invariant everywhere:
\begin{resultbox}{Invariant Gravitational Impedance}
\begin{equation}
    Z(r) = \sqrt{\frac{\mu_{eff}(r)}{\varepsilon_{eff}(r)}} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu_0 \cdot n}{\varepsilon_0 \cdot n}} \equiv Z_0
    \label{eq:Z_grav}
\end{equation}
\end{resultbox}
Therefore, the EM-transverse reflection coefficient $\Gamma_{EM}$ across any gravitational gradient evaluates identically to zero ($\Gamma_{EM} = 0$). While the local speed of light slows down near mass ($c_l = c/n$), the EM impedance matching remains perfect. In the weak-field regions traversed by detected signals, gravitational waves propagate without reflection or scattering. At the saturation boundary ($r_{sat} = 7GM/c^2$) the shear channel reflects totally ($\Gamma_{shear} = -1$, where $G_{shear} \to 0$), distinct from the EM channel which remains matched ($\Gamma_{EM} = 0$).

\section{GW Detection: The Impedance Antenna}
\label{sec:gw_detection}

A gravitational wave detector is an impedance antenna.  LIGO's 4~km
Fabry--P\'erot arms form resonant cavities in the LC vacuum, where
each light bounce amplifies the GW-induced impedance modulation.
The passing GW strain $h$ perturbs the local vacuum impedance:
\begin{resultbox}{GW-Induced Shear-Impedance Perturbation}
\begin{equation}
    \delta Z_{shear} = Z_{shear} \cdot h
\end{equation}
\end{resultbox}
where $Z_{shear} = \rho\,c_{shear}$ is the shear (transverse-mechanical) impedance. The EM characteristic impedance $Z_0 \equiv Z_{EM}$ is the matched radiative port and is invariant under symmetric gravity; it is a distinct channel.
The accumulated phase shift after $N$ bounces is:
\begin{resultbox}{Fabry-P\'erot Accumulated Phase Shift}
\begin{equation}
    \Delta\phi = \frac{2\pi f_{GW}}{c} \cdot L \cdot N \cdot h
\end{equation}
\end{resultbox}

$\Delta\phi \approx 2.3 \times 10^{-21}$~rad --- resolved via
homodyne readout against 750~kW circulating laser power.

shot noise (phase) and radiation pressure (amplitude), whose
