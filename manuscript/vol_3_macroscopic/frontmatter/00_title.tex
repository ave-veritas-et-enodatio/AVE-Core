This document unites microscopic knot theory with macroscopic thermodynamics, gravity, and cosmology.

\begin{abstract}
    \textbf{Volume III: The Macroscopic Continuum} applies the substrate model across scales, treating gravitation as the macroscopic scalar impedance gradient ($Z$) of the LC network rather than as an independent geometric manifold. Writing the stress-energy content as an acoustic fluid state:
    \begin{equation}
        T_{\mu\nu} = (\rho + P)u_\mu u_\nu + P \eta_{\mu\nu}

    the volume develops gravity, thermodynamic entropy, superconductivity, and the universe's expansion limits (dark energy) from the substrate's tension and latent-heat dissipation. The gravitational \emph{form} is substrate-derived; $G$'s value is treated as a calibration input (see the common foreword's honest-scope notes).
