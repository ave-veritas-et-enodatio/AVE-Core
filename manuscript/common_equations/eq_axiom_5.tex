\begin{resultbox}{Axiom 5 — Substrate DC Bias (deposit $\cdot$ grade $\cdot$ quiescence)}
The four founding axioms write a \emph{floating} network: they fix the substrate's topology (Ax 1), its charge dictionary (Ax 2), its lossless extremal dynamics (Ax 3), and its saturation kernel (Ax 4) --- but they never write the source coupling that pins the bound (A1 dilatation) sector's absolute state. Axiom 5 is that missing specification: the substrate's \textbf{DC operating point}. Three clauses, ratified together:

\medskip\noindent\textbf{S (deposit).} A matter defect deposits a nonzero net A1 dilatation flux:
\begin{equation}
    \oint_S \mathbf{u}\cdot\hat{\mathbf{n}} = 4\pi B(M)
    \label{eq:axiom5_deposit}
\end{equation}
over any enclosing exterior surface, with $B(M)$ = the defect's A1 mass accounting (dimensional VALUE via the imported $G/\xi$ chain). \textbf{Mass is an enclosed compression charge BY LAW}: the write event is a genuine new law (the four-lock no-go bars the receipted dynamics from creating it), and Kirchhoff's current law / the derived conservation leg $\partial_t(\nabla\cdot\pi) = -\nabla\cdot j_m$ preserves it thereafter. \textbf{Clause S is MECHANISM-AGNOSTIC:} it is \emph{genesis-deposited boundary data on the crystalline phase}, and it states \emph{that} the flux is written, not \emph{how}. The genesis mechanism is a named-open entry of the genesis phase (phase map below) --- this clause neither supplies one nor licenses one.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{G (grade coupling / bridge).} The operating-point grade is the bound sector's potential:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{u}_0 = -\mathcal{A}_g\nabla\varepsilon_{11},\qquad
    -\nabla\cdot\!\left[\kappa\, D(A)\,\nabla\varepsilon_{11}\right] = T_{00},\qquad
    \kappa = \frac{c^4}{7G}
    \label{eq:axiom5_bridge}
\end{equation}
(the $\kappa$-stiffened elliptic grade law; $\kappa$'s VALUE stays imported). Equivalently: the canon backreaction solve becomes the bound-sector constitutive law in potential form. \textbf{One coupling constant, $\mathcal{A}_g$} (the grade-coupling area, $[\mathrm{m}^2]$), enters the framework here and nowhere else.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Q (quiescence --- the DC operating point).} The sourceless substrate sits at the cold operating point:
\begin{equation}
    \nabla\cdot\pi = 0,\qquad \theta = 0,\qquad \varepsilon_{11} = 0
    \label{eq:axiom5_quiescence}
\end{equation}
away from defects. This is the \textbf{quiescent reference (Q-point) that makes the potentials defined and clause G's elliptic solve well-posed} --- without it the conserved data is unpinned far from sources and the potential problem has no boundary condition. Clause Q is a statement about the substrate's \emph{state}; it is not a claim that small-signal linearisation is valid, which is a separate, separately-declared regime condition.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{The internal falsifier ships with the axiom.} Clauses S and G jointly force the consistency relation
\begin{equation}
    B = \frac{7\,\mathcal{A}_g\,G M}{c^2}
    \label{eq:axiom5_falsifier}
\end{equation}
(from $\mathbf{u}_0 = -\mathcal{A}_g\nabla\varepsilon_{11}$ and the canon grade profile $\varepsilon_{11} = 7GM/c^2 r$). \textbf{One $\mathcal{A}_g$ across every consumer}: measuring $\mathcal{A}_g$ from independent consumers over-determines it, and the over-determination is the axiom's own test port --- built in, not appended.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{$\mathcal{A}_g$ is DERIVE-FIRST and enters NO register (R46).} The frozen hypothesis is $\mathcal{A}_g = c\,\ell_{node}^2$ with $c$ a pure number ($\ell_{node} = \hbar/m_ec$, core-derived). Until the derivation lane adjudicates it, $\mathcal{A}_g$ is deliberately absent from the interlock register and from the calibration-parameter set --- so the framework's independent-parameter count is \emph{not} moved by this axiom, in either direction, and no count claim rides on it. If DERIVED, $\mathcal{A}_g$ is core-derived and the import question dissolves; if not, its register entry and the count adjudication are a Grant ruling.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Phase structure (R45 --- the axiom carries its phases inside).} \emph{(a) Crystalline phase (cold, sub-yield, lossless-reactive):} clauses \textbf{Q} and \textbf{G} are the law here, as written above. Clause \textbf{S} is genesis-deposited boundary \emph{data} on this phase --- present, conserved, mechanism-agnostic. \emph{(b) At the saturation boundary:} $D(A)$ carries clause G's response into the graded regime; the $D(A)\to\infty$ wall behaviour is past-wall-adjacent and \textbf{not written here}. \emph{(c) NAMED-OPEN holes (first-class, not omissions):} the \textbf{genesis-phase} law that writes clause S's flux (mechanism unknown --- the deposit is data, not a derivation); the \textbf{de-bonded phase} form of all three clauses (unwritten); the \textbf{pre-freeze} phase (unwritten). Comprehensiveness is the FRAME: this axiom is a law of the material across its phases, and the phases whose law is not yet written are named rather than silently assumed.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{What Axiom 5 forbids.} A sourceless dress (no net A1 flux without matter); any new longitudinal stiffness or wave (it adds no kinetic or potential term on the flat direction, so the pole-absence results survive it untouched); any EM/winding-sector deposit (the four locks are untouched --- the A1 sector's transportability, not a new source, is what breaks the A1/EM symmetry).
