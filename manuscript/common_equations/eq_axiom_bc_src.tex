\begin{resultbox}{BC-SRC — the Bound-Sector Source Coupling (the substrate's DC specification)}
The four founding axioms write a \emph{floating} network: they fix the substrate's topology (Ax 1), its charge dictionary (Ax 2), its lossless extremal dynamics (Ax 3), and its saturation kernel (Ax 4) --- but they never write the source coupling that pins the bound (A1 dilatation) sector's absolute state. BC-SRC is that missing specification: the substrate's \textbf{DC operating point}. Three clauses, all ratified together:

\medskip\noindent\textbf{S (deposit).} A matter defect deposits a nonzero net A1 dilatation flux:
\begin{equation}
    \oint_S \mathbf{u}\cdot\hat{\mathbf{n}} = 4\pi B(M)
    \label{eq:bcsrc_deposit}
\end{equation}
over any enclosing exterior surface, with $B(M)$ = the defect's A1 mass accounting (dimensional VALUE via the imported $G/\xi$ chain). Every defect deposits this flux \emph{at genesis} --- particles are factory-charged; \textbf{mass is an enclosed compression charge BY LAW}. The write event is a genuine new law: the four-lock no-go bars the receipted dynamics from creating it, and Kirchhoff's current law / the derived conservation leg $\partial_t(\nabla\cdot\pi) = -\nabla\cdot j_m$ preserves it thereafter.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{G (grade coupling / bridge).} The operating-point grade is the bound sector's potential:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{u}_0 = -\lambda\nabla\varepsilon_{11},\qquad
    -\nabla\cdot\!\left[\kappa\, D(A)\,\nabla\varepsilon_{11}\right] = T_{00},\qquad
    \kappa = \frac{c^4}{7G}
    \label{eq:bcsrc_bridge}
\end{equation}
(the $\kappa$-stiffened elliptic grade law; $\kappa$'s VALUE stays imported). Equivalently: the canon backreaction solve becomes the bound-sector constitutive law in potential form. Read as a circuit, this is the \textbf{DC-bias-network law}: mass-energy injects current, the saturation-graded conductance distributes the potential $\varepsilon_{11}$, the dress is its field, and $\kappa$ is the bias sector's permittivity. \textbf{One new constant, $\lambda$}, enters the framework here and nowhere else.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Q (quiescence --- the DC operating point).} The sourceless substrate sits at the cold operating point:
\begin{equation}
    \nabla\cdot\pi = 0,\qquad \theta = 0,\qquad \varepsilon_{11} = 0
    \label{eq:bcsrc_quiescence}
\end{equation}
away from defects. This is the \textbf{quiescent reference (Q-point) that makes the potentials defined and clause G's elliptic solve well-posed} --- without it the conserved data is unpinned far from sources and the potential problem has no boundary condition. In EE-analogy terms only: \emph{``BC-SRC is the ground reference of the floating network the bare axioms built''} (Grant's physical framing of record). The canonical noun is the \textbf{DC operating point / quiescent point (Q-point)}; ``ground'' is the analogy, never the canonical term.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{The internal falsifier ships with the axiom.} Clauses S and G jointly force the consistency relation
\begin{equation}
    B = \frac{7\lambda G M}{c^2}
    \label{eq:bcsrc_falsifier}
\end{equation}
(from $\mathbf{u}_0 = -\lambda\nabla\varepsilon_{11}$ and the canon grade profile $\varepsilon_{11} = 7GM/c^2 r$). \textbf{One $\lambda$ across every consumer}: measuring $\lambda$ from two independent consumers over-determines it, and the over-determination is the axiom's own test port. This is the axiom's built-in falsifier, not an add-on.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Minimality (each clause load-bearing; ablation).} Without \textbf{S}, the derived conservation legs leave the dress value unpinned. Without \textbf{G}, there is no receipted home for the finite static stiffness, no grade-reading causality, no energy functional, and no derived connection to the static $/7$ chain. Without \textbf{Q}, the conserved data is unpinned far from sources. No clause is derivable from the receipted action.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{What BC-SRC forbids.} A sourceless dress (no net A1 flux without matter); any new longitudinal stiffness or wave (BC-SRC adds no kinetic or potential term on the flat direction, so the pole-absence results survive it untouched); any EM/winding-sector deposit (the four locks are untouched --- the A1 sector's transportability, not a new source, is what breaks the A1/EM symmetry).
\end{resultbox}
