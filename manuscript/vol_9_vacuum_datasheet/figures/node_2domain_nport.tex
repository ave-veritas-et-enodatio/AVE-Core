\caption{Two-domain N-port netlist of the vacuum node (rendering the structure of \texttt{src/scripts/vol\_9\_device/node\_2domain\_nport.py}). The EM (electrical, $\Omega$) sub-network is the substrate's \emph{sole external} radiative port ($\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}=0$, $Z_{\mathrm{EM}}\equiv Z_0\approx376.73\,\Omega$). The two mechanical ports --- shear (charge-``3'', Cosserat $(2,3)$ micro-rotation) and bulk (mass-``3'', A1 dilatation, $Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}=\sqrt2\,\rho_b c_0$ at $K=2G$) --- are in the \emph{mechanical} (Rayl $=$ Pa$\cdot$s/m) domain, both confined ($\Gamma\to-1$, the saturated-cage short), and form ONE mechanical 2-port coupled through the conserved $H_{\mathrm{couple}}$. The EM ($\Omega$) and mechanical (Rayl) domains carry different units, so joining them requires a TRANSDUCER, not a direct wire: an ideal transformer (\emph{not} a gyrator). $\alpha$ localizes to exactly the transducer turns-ratio --- the $p_c=8\pi\alpha$ packing-fraction residual --- isolated and visible, not smeared across the network (driver header lines 84--89; $p_c=8\pi\alpha$ canonical at \kbleaf{topological-packing-fraction.md}). The transformer node is itself \texttt{def-tk1xfm}, status \emph{proposed} (gated --- flagged, not asserted).}
