\begin{warningbox}[Scope and residual caveats --- a [RENDERING]-scale topology model]
The kit is \textbf{rebuilt and validated} (2026-06-21). The legacy preview-only limitations are \textbf{resolved}: every gated part now exports \textbf{watertight} and \texttt{is\_volume=True} (reloaded slicer-equivalent: \texttt{process=True}, \texttt{merge\_vertices=True}), because parts are exported \emph{directly} from the welded \texttt{trimesh} object rather than round-tripping through \texttt{numpy-stl} (the source of the old non-manifold reload). \textbf{Honest residual caveats (unchanged by the rebuild):}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{It is a topology model, not a physically scaled replica.} Corpus $\ell_{node} \approx 386\,\mathrm{fm}$; at the $100\,\mathrm{mm}/\ell_{node}$ print default the linear magnification versus physical pitch is $\sim\!2.6\times10^{11}$. Sub-node bodies ($D_p \ll \ell_{node}$) remain unresolvable in any print tier.
    \item \textbf{The assembled L4 chunk is large.} The default $L=4$ finite crystal spans $\sim\!300\,\mathrm{mm}$ ($\sim\!0.3\,\mathrm{m}$) per axis once seated on the jig --- larger than the print bed; it is assembled off-bed from individually-printed parts.
    \item \textbf{Phase space is never a printed length.} The LC-tank saturation state is rendered only by the impedance disc / phasor dial, explicitly tagged (Sec.~\ref{sec:vol9_exp_prints_representation}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{warningbox}

\section{Framing: Bench-Adjacent Topology Lab}
\label{sec:vol9_exp_prints_framing}

The preceding chapters characterize the vacuum cell in datasheet and simulator-spec form. This chapter records a complementary \textbf{pedagogical track}: 3D-printable exports that let a reader \emph{assemble} a K4 lattice from repeatable parts and verify that the printed graph matches its engine net. \emph{(D1 carrier note: the current kit prints the degree-4 \texttt{build\_diamond\_net}, which is the non-canonical topology-demo instrument, not the ratified \texttt{srs} $z=3$ production carrier; see the Production-vs-instrument note below.)}

\textbf{What this is.} A \textbf{laboratory exercise} in discrete-topology consistency --- the same epistemic class as verifying a circuit board against its netlist. The exercise checks bipartite A/B placement, four tetrahedral \textbf{bond-ports} per active node (Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_pin_port_configuration}~\S Per-port wave content; a K4 connectivity element, exactly four per node --- \emph{not} Op5, which is the Multiport Y-to-S conversion operator, per Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_pin_port_configuration} footnote), and transmission-line bonds along tetrahedral nearest-neighbour vectors (Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_dc_electrical_characteristics}).

\textbf{What this is not.} Not a Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_falsification_tests} falsifier (no new substrate-physics prediction is tested). Not a substitute for Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_engine_requirements} (numerical dynamics are not exercised). Not a physically scaled replica: millimetre-scale prints carry magnification $\sim\!2.6\times10^{11}$ versus physical pitch --- unavoidable for any visible model. Per INVARIANT-S9, a printed mesh is not registered as an \texttt{exp-} experiment; it is a \textbf{topology demonstration} supporting derivation navigation.

\textbf{Production carrier vs topology-demo instrument (D1, Grant 2026-07-03).} The ratified \textbf{production substrate} is the chiral degree-3 \texttt{srs} $z=3$ net (the Sunada-K4 / Laves / $I4_132$ net Axiom~1 names) --- shipped in the kit as the \texttt{build\_srs\_net} piece (both enantiomorphs, handedness glyph). The kit's assembled crystal currently prints \texttt{build\_diamond\_net} --- the degree-4 achiral Fd-3m diamond net --- which is a \textbf{non-canonical topology-demo instrument} (statics-pathological; re-tagged per the 2026-07-03 D1 ratification, \kbleaf{\_orchestration/index.md}). It is a legitimate discrete-topology teaching object (bipartite A/B, tetrahedral connectivity), but it is \emph{not} the production carrier; the historical manifest label ``diamond production K4'' is a \textbf{naming defect} pending the deferred srs-name-walkback arc (\kbleaf{\_orchestration/2026-07-03\_srs-migration-policy.md} §d). The two nets must not be conflated. (``K4'' remains a documented three-way overload: the axiom-named chiral Laves K4 = degree-3 srs (the carrier); the engine ``K4'' = degree-4 diamond (this instrument); the rotation group $K_4$ = a distinct finite-group label. The kit labels each piece by its actual degree.)

\section{The Representation Principle}
\label{sec:vol9_exp_prints_representation}

A 3D print is a \textbf{real-space object}. The faithful rendering of the vacuum cell therefore depends on \emph{which coordinate system} a quantity lives in. The kit follows one rule, summarized in Table~\ref{tab:vol9_exp_print_representation}:

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Representation principle --- what may be rendered as a printed length, and what may not.}
\label{tab:vol9_exp_print_representation}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.30\linewidth} p{0.18\linewidth} p{0.42\linewidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Quantity} & \textbf{Coordinate system} & \textbf{Rendering in the print} \\
\midrule
Node positions, bonds, 4 tetrahedral ports & real space & \textbf{isomorphic} --- the printed skeleton (true $\sqrt{3}\,\ell_{node}$ pitch) \\
Translational vs micro-rotational vs breathing DOF basis & real-space \emph{directions} at each node & \textbf{labeled accents} --- snap-on triad / rings / breathing core, color per store \\
LC-tank state, $(V_{inc}, V_{ref})$ phasor / impedance plane & phase space & \textbf{labeled proxy only} --- impedance disc + phasor dial, never a spatial length \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Real space is isomorphic.} The printed skeleton is graph-isomorphic to \texttt{build\_diamond\_net}: a degree-4 diamond net (the topology-demo instrument, not the \texttt{srs} production carrier --- D1), an \emph{identical solid node body} for both A and B sublattices, four tetrahedral \textbf{round} bond sockets per node (round so the rigid bond press-fits both end sockets at any relative rotation --- a hex tip cannot face-flush two independently-clocked sockets at once), and bonds at the true $\sqrt{3}\,\ell_{node}$ pitch. (Verified: kit port directions equal the engine \texttt{\_DIAMOND\_PORTS}; all bonds bipartite A--B; port-pair angle $109.47^\circ = \arccos(-1/3)$.)

\textbf{The DOF basis is shown by accents.} The seven kinematic modes are rendered as snap-on color accents, one filament color per store:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{triad\_E} --- the 3 \emph{translational} DOF $\to$ $\mathbf{E}$ store $\to$ $\varepsilon^2$;
    \item \texttt{rings\_B} --- the 3 \emph{micro-rotational} DOF $\to$ $\mathbf{B}$ store $\to$ $\kappa^2$;
    \item \texttt{breathing\_V} --- the single A1 \emph{volumetric breathing} mode $\to$ $V^2$ \emph{store} $=$ \textbf{mass}.
\end{itemize}
This is the seven-mode store split
\begin{equation}
A^2 \;=\; \varepsilon^2 \;+\; \kappa^2 \;+\; V^2,
\label{eq:vol9_seven_mode_split}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ 6 spatial DOF (3 translational $+$ 3 micro-rotational) \emph{plus} 1 A1 breathing mode. The breathing axis is kept \textbf{independent of the rotation rings}: \textbf{A1 $\perp$ T2} (Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_mechanical_characteristics}) --- mass (A1 dilatation) and charge/spin (Cosserat micro-rotation) are orthogonal grades and never share one $(V_{inc}, V_{ref})$ phasor.

\textbf{Phase space is a labeled proxy only.} The saturation amplitude $A$ in Eq.~\ref{eq:vol9_seven_mode_split} is the LC-tank \emph{state}, not a spatial DOF. It is rendered \emph{only} by the impedance disc and phasor dial, each physically separate from the lattice and stamped \texttt{[STATE-SPACE --- NOT A COORDINATE]} --- the print-medium analog of the \texttt{[RENDERING]} scale tag. By A1 $\perp$ T2, any phase-state indicator is \emph{two independent orthogonal} dials (mass vs charge-spin), never one merged pointer. \textbf{No real-space length in the print may stand for a phase-space quantity.}

\textbf{Hero first print.} The recommended starter is the \textbf{tetrahedral unit cell} (degree-4 coordination shell): \texttt{1$\times$node\_A $+$ 4$\times$node\_B $+$ 4$\times$bond $+$ accents $+$ jig\_unit\_cell} ($\sim\!9$ parts). It shows $z=4$ coordination, A/B bipartiteness, and tetrahedral ports without committing to the full $L=4$ part count.

\textbf{Keyed base jig.} The jig is a printed baseplate with embossed \texttt{node-id} $+$ A/B pockets and per-port pips, so node placement \emph{and} which-port$\to$which-neighbor are forced by geometry; the manifest \texttt{nodes[].ports} field carries the port$\to$neighbor map. For the full $L=4$ chunk the jig is tiled to the Prusa $250\times210$ bed (four \texttt{jig\_tile} pieces).

\textbf{A/B is the bipartite sublattice, not a DOF split (the shape lie is gone).} In the rebuilt kit, Type-A and Type-B are the \textbf{same solid node body}; the sublattice is distinguished only by \textbf{color} and an embossed \textbf{A/B key} (\texttt{key\_A.stl} / \texttt{key\_B.stl}). The legacy cube(A)-vs-sphere(B) ``shape key'' --- which read as a denied capacitive-only/inductive-only \emph{storage} split --- is \textbf{removed}. Per Axiom~1 (Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_mechanical_characteristics}), every node is a full LC oscillator carrying all six spatial DOF (3 translational $\to$ $\mathbf{E}$ \emph{and} 3 micro-rotational $\to$ $\mathbf{B}$) plus the A1 breathing mode ($\to$ $V$/mass); the A/B label is a bipartite-sublattice tag, never a partition of the substrate's degrees of freedom.

\section{Laboratory Exercise L-EP1: Assemble the Production Chunk}
\label{sec:vol9_lab_LEP1}

\begin{exercisebox}
\textbf{Laboratory Exercise L-EP1 --- Assemble the diamond topology-demo chunk on the keyed jig}

\textbf{Objective.} Verify by physical assembly that the exported kit is graph-isomorphic to \texttt{build\_diamond\_net(L)} on the finite-crystal bond list (no periodic wrap edges).

\textbf{Materials.} Kit parts (Sec.~\ref{sec:vol9_exp_prints_kit}); manifest \texttt{vacuum\_assembly\_L\{L\}.json}; the printed keyed jig (\texttt{jig\_unit\_cell.stl} for the hero unit cell, or the tiled \texttt{jig\_tile\_\{0..3\}.stl} baseplate for $L=4$).

\textbf{Procedure.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Assemble the jig: snap/glue the \texttt{jig\_tile} edges into the baseplate (or use \texttt{jig\_unit\_cell} for the hero build).
    \item Print \texttt{node\_A} $\times$\,\texttt{counts.node\_A} and \texttt{node\_B} $\times$\,\texttt{counts.node\_B} in two colors; snap on the matching A/B key.
    \item Drop each node into its keyed jig pocket --- the pocket emboss (\texttt{node-id} $+$ A/B) forces placement; the manifest \texttt{nodes[]} entry carries the same id and \texttt{sublattice}.
    \item Press a bond into each socket; the pocket port-pips show which port $\to$ which neighbor (manifest \texttt{nodes[].ports}). The press-fit is real: $0.10\,\mathrm{mm}$ diametral interference ($0.05\,\mathrm{mm}$/side). Tune via \texttt{KIT\_FRICTION\_INTERFERENCE\_MM} ($0.03$--$0.08$) if loose or tight.
    \item Optional: snap on the DOF accents (\texttt{triad\_E} / \texttt{rings\_B} / \texttt{breathing\_V}), color per store; set the phasor dials / read the impedance disc as the LC-tank \emph{state} (not a position).
    \item Checklist: degree $4$ at every interior node; every bond connects A$\leftrightarrow$B; bond lengths match manifest \texttt{center\_pitch\_mm} ($173.2\,\mathrm{mm}$ at default scale) within tolerance; no anomalously long ``wrap'' struts.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Pass.} Assembled graph matches engine finite-crystal topology; the press-fit holds. \textbf{Fail.} Wrong degree, same-sublattice bond, wrap-scale bond length outlier, or joints that do not seat (re-tune interference).

\textbf{Discipline class.} Identity-class consistency on K4 graph (Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_topological_characteristics} K4 valence; Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_pin_port_configuration} tetrahedral bond-ports).
\end{exercisebox}

\section{Laboratory Exercise L-EP2: Monolith vs Kit (Optional)}

\textbf{Laboratory Exercise L-EP2 --- Monolith vs kit comparison}

\textbf{Objective.} At fixed $L$ and \texttt{KIT\_PRINT\_MM\_PER\_L\_NODE}, confirm the monolithic STL span and bond pitch agree with the kit manifest \texttt{assembled\_bbox\_mm} ($\sim\!300\times300\times300\,\mathrm{mm}$ at $L=4$) and \texttt{bonds[].center\_pitch\_mm}.
