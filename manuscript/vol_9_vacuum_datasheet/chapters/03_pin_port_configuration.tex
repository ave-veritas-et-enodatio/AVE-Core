\section{Substrate Boundary Semantics}
\label{sec:vol9_substrate_boundary_semantics}

Every internal interface between two regions of the substrate $\mathcal{M}_A$ that present distinct local characteristic impedances $Z_1$ and $Z_2$ is, by the canonical Op3 definition, a substrate \emph{port}. The reflection coefficient at every such port:

\begin{equation}
\Gamma = \frac{Z_2 - Z_1}{Z_2 + Z_1}
\label{eq:vol9_universal_gamma}
\end{equation}

is the substrate's native single-port figure-of-merit. The formula is Op3 (Vol~1 Ch.~6 \S 6.3 canonical; KB canonical at \texttt{ave-kb/vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch6-universal-operators/reflection-coefficient.md}, claim \texttt{clm-gdd70j}) and is scale-invariant across all 14 orders of magnitude documented in the universal-operator catalogue: sub-nuclear ($Z_{knot} \to 0 \Rightarrow \Gamma = -1$ Pauli exclusion), lab-scale antenna $S_{11}$, planetary seismic Moho discontinuity, gravitational substrate boundary, and beyond. The substrate ports are not engineered; they are natural impedance discontinuities arising wherever the substrate's local $(\varepsilon, \mu)$ state changes across an interface.

Axiom~3 (\textbf{Minimum Reflection Principle}, CLAUDE.md INVARIANT-S2; canonical equation file \texttt{common\_equations/eq\_axiom\_3.tex}) is the substrate-native action principle:

\begin{quote}
\emph{$\mathcal{M}_A$ minimizes $|\Gamma|^2$ at every internal impedance boundary.}
\end{quote}

\section{Port Class Taxonomy}
\label{sec:vol9_port_class_taxonomy}

The substrate's $(\varepsilon, \mu)$ state can be modulated in distinct ways across a boundary. Two canonical scaling regimes (CLAUDE.md INVARIANT-S2 + canonical leaf \texttt{clm-8nkvwy} at \texttt{ave-kb/vol2/particle-physics/ch01-topological-matter/pair-production-axiom-derivation.md}) classify the ports of $\mathcal{M}_A$:

\subsection{SYM-class ports}

Under SYM-class scaling, $\mu(r)$ and $\varepsilon(r)$ scale together by the same factor across the boundary, so the characteristic impedance $Z_0 = \sqrt{\mu/\varepsilon}$ is invariant: $\Gamma = 0$ at every interior point. The substrate's $c_{shear}(A_0) = c_0\sqrt{S(A_0)}$ tracks $\sqrt{S}$ while $c_{EM}(A_0) = c_0/S(A_0)$ tracks $1/S$, producing the gradient-index transmission-line profile characteristic of gravitational time-dilation in the weak-field limit. The fine-structure constant is \emph{exactly invariant} under SYM-class scaling (canonical proof at \texttt{ave-kb/vol3/gravity/ch01-gravity-yield/alpha-invariance-symmetric-gravity.md}, claim \texttt{clm-3zz0f6}).

SYM-class is the substrate's \emph{gravity-class realization}: every macroscopic gravitational boundary in $\mathcal{M}_A$ (Schwarzschild reduction, weak-field deflection, gravitational redshift) is a SYM port at which $|\Gamma|^2$ is exactly minimized. Multi-species clock comparisons at different gravitational potentials predict a null $\Delta\alpha/\alpha$, consistent with all current experimental bounds.

\subsection{ASYM-class ports}

Under ASYM-class scaling, $\mu$ and $\varepsilon$ scale by different factors across the boundary; $Z_0$ is not preserved and $\alpha$ is not invariant. ASYM-class is the canonical substrate mechanism for $\alpha$-modulating predictions. Three documented ASYM subclasses, distinguished by which constitutive parameter carries the asymmetry:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{ASYM-N($\varepsilon$)}: $\varepsilon$-only modulation; $\mu$ at cold-lattice. Strong-field large-amplitude saturation regime canonical at \texttt{clm-8nkvwy}; appears in the cosmic dark-energy $\varepsilon$-only entry of the universal-saturation-kernel catalogue (A-034 expanded catalogue, classified ASYM-N at \texttt{ave-kb/common/operators.md} Op2 row).
    \item \textbf{ASYM-N($\mu$)}: $\mu$-only modulation; $\varepsilon$ at cold-lattice. Canonical BCS instance ($B_c(T)$ at $0.00\%$ error per the same A-034 catalogue); plasma instances also occupy this subclass.
    \item \textbf{Cosserat-Curie thermal-mode-population ASYM}: low-amplitude thermal-bath regime where the substrate's bipartite mode structure produces asymmetric thermal occupation. The Cosserat rotation-sector mass-gap $\omega_m \sim \qty{1}{\mega\electronvolt}$ thermally freezes B-modes ($\mu$ side at cold-lattice) while E-modes ($\varepsilon$ side) are thermally populated. Canonical at \texttt{ave-kb/vol3/cosmology/ch05-dark-sector/delta-strain-cosmic-tcc.md} (\texttt{clm-hp7nlm}, landed 2026-05-28). The substrate-mechanism class for $\delta_{strain} \approx \num{2.225e-6}$ at $T_{CMB}$.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
The three ASYM subclasses are substrate-mechanism distinct (different physical primitives drive the asymmetry) but share the structural consequence: $|\Gamma|^2 > 0$ at the boundary even when $|\Gamma|^2$ is locally minimized over the available substrate state. The Cosserat-Curie subclass is the load-bearing mechanism for cosmic-temperature $\alpha$-drift forward predictions.

\section{Operator-Level Port Behavior}
\label{sec:vol9_operator_port_behavior}

Two universal scale-invariant operators (out of the 22-operator catalogue at \texttt{ave-kb/common/operators.md}, claim \texttt{clm-sysqaf}) act directly on substrate boundaries, occupying the two endpoints of substrate $\Gamma$-space:

\subsection{Op17 --- Power Transmission}

Canonical leaf at \texttt{ave-kb/vol4/circuit-theory/ch1-vacuum-circuit-analysis/op21-multi-mode-mode-counting.md} (no-claim leaf; strengthens \texttt{clm-rtdmsn} and \texttt{clm-0ktpcn}, landed Phase 3-A4 2026-05-27). \begin{itemize}
