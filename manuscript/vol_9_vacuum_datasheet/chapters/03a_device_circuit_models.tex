\subsection{Graded Vacuum Impedance Network}
\label{sec:vol9_graded_vacuum_network}

\noindent The graded vacuum impedance network is the equivalent-circuit model of the medium drawn as three wired reactance channels --- $Z_{\mathrm{EM}} \equiv Z_0$ (the matched radiative port, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}=0$), $Z_{\mathrm{shear}}=\rho_{\mathrm{bulk}}\,c_{\mathrm{shear}}$ (deviatoric $G$, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{shear}}\to-1$, the charge-``3'' Cosserat winding), and $Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}=\rho_{\mathrm{bulk}}\,c_{\mathrm{bulk}}=\sqrt{2}\,\rho_{\mathrm{bulk}}\,c_0$ at $K=2G$ ($\Gamma_{\mathrm{bulk}}\to-1$, the mass-``3'' A1 dilatation) --- coupled through a chiral circulator and terminated at confinement surfaces. \textbf{Mixed impedance domains:} only $Z_{\mathrm{EM}}\equiv Z_0$ is an \emph{electrical} impedance ($\Omega$); $Z_{\mathrm{shear}}$ and $Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ are \emph{mechanical/acoustic} ($\rho\times$speed) and are \emph{not} in $Z_0$ units (writing ``$Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}=\sqrt2\,Z_0$'' is the electrical-vs-mechanical mis-scope the three-impedance law warns against). The channels are co-equal in role but in different domains, so EM$\leftrightarrow$mechanical coupling requires a transducer, not a direct wire (the TKI-transformer; provisional, pending experimental confirmation), while the bulk$\leftrightarrow$shear inter-grade coupling is the conserved $H_{\mathrm{couple}}$. This is a \textbf{consistency re-expression} of the three-impedance law, not a new substrate primitive (per \textsc{invariant-n1} the network is the circuit model, not a substrate-object noun). Full content, the $\mathcal{M},\mathcal{J},\mathcal{Q}$ map (the one-to-one correspondence does not hold), the forks, and the open-gate registry live in \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol9/ch3-pin-port-configuration/device-circuit-models.md} \S6. The per-DOF node constitutive layer \emph{beneath} the scalar cell --- each translation DOF an $(L_i,C_i)$ reactance, from which one circuit yields isotropic-co-scale achromaticity, deviatoric birefringence, and the $(q\,\ell_{\mathrm{node}})$ dispersion tell --- is documented in \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol9/ch3-pin-port-configuration/per-dof-vacuum-node-circuit.md}.

\paragraph{Isolation eigensolver: the intrinsic mode is lossless ($Q\to\infty$); $\alpha^{-1}$ is the loaded $Q$.}
The substrate-native graded-vacuum-network isolation eigensolver yields a lossless-confined isolation-leg eigen-$Q$ ($Q\gg45$, growing toward $\infty$ with resolution) --- not in the validate band, not $\sim137$, not $\sim3$. This result admits three readings (full content in \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol2/particle-physics/ch01-topological-matter/electron-bound-resonator-coverage.md} and \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol4/circuit-theory/ch1-vacuum-circuit-analysis/theorem-3-1-q-factor.md}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Electron persistence (Class-C \emph{consistency}, not a chord).} With the EM radiative port closed the operator is Hermitian, $\mathrm{Im}(\omega)\to0$, so the \emph{intrinsic} $Q\to\infty$ ($1.4\times10^{16}$) $\Rightarrow$ infinite lifetime $\Rightarrow$ the electron persists in an ideal vacuum, matching the empirically most-stable particle ($\tau>10^{28}\,$yr). AVE obtains stability from an \emph{impedance} argument (lossless medium $+$ $\Gamma=-1$ confinement) where standard physics uses a \emph{symmetry} argument (charge conservation). Recorded as a consistency check --- it partly follows from the lossless axiom (Axiom~3) --- never headlined as emergence.
  \item \textbf{$\boldsymbol{\alpha^{-1}=137}$ is the \emph{loaded/radiative} $Q$, not the intrinsic resonator $Q$.} Since the intrinsic mode is lossless, $137$ is attributed to how the lossless mode couples \emph{out} through the EM port ($|\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}|^2=1-\alpha$, a fraction $\alpha$ leaking per bounce). Clean separation: \emph{stability/persistence} $=$ intrinsic lossless confinement ($Q\to\infty$); \emph{interaction} $=$ radiative loading (loaded $Q=1/\alpha$ $=$ the intrinsic vacuum$\leftrightarrow$EM coupling coefficient). Even loaded, the electron does \emph{not} decay --- $1/\alpha$ is a coupling (self-dressing), not a lifetime. $\alpha$ remains an echo at the value level; the loaded-$Q$ value is calibrated, not derived.
  \item \textbf{$\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ stays echo.} Golden-Torus $R\cdot r=\tfrac14$, the $z_0$-route, and $4\pi^3+\pi^2+\pi$ as a calibration identity all point the same way: $\alpha$ remains an echo at the value level. The cold-cage $Q\approx30.8$ and the eigenframe $Q\to\infty$ are both consistent with this. A direct derivation of the loaded $Q=1/\alpha$ is circular (the engine leak is literally $1-\alpha$; the $\alpha$-free edge-radiation answer is already the cold-cage $30.8$); the open item is the coupled-network $Q$ targeting the \emph{observed} electron $Q$ (not $137$).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Two-Domain N-Port (EM $\Omega$ $+$ mechanical Rayl, joined by a transformer)}
\label{sec:vol9_node_2domain_nport}

\noindent The graded-network schematic above draws the three reactance channels in one picture, but $Z_{\mathrm{EM}}$ is an \emph{electrical} impedance ($\Omega$) while $Z_{\mathrm{shear}}$ and $Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ are \emph{mechanical/acoustic} (Rayl $=$ Pa$\cdot$s/m). A faithful netlist must keep the two impedance domains separate. The substrate node is therefore a \textbf{two-domain N-port}: an EM sub-network (the \emph{sole external} radiative port, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}=0$, $Z_{\mathrm{EM}}\equiv Z_0$) and a mechanical 2-port (shear $=$ charge and bulk $=$ mass, both confined $\Gamma\to-1$, coupled through the conserved $H_{\mathrm{couple}}$), bridged by an electro-mechanical \textbf{transducer}. Because the two ports carry different units, the bridge is an \textbf{ideal transformer} (a turns-ratio change-of-reference), \emph{not} a gyrator and \emph{not} a direct wire. The load-bearing consequence: $\alpha$ does not smear across the network --- it \textbf{localizes to exactly the transducer turns-ratio}, the $p_c = 8\pi\alpha$ packing-fraction residual ($p_c = 8\pi\alpha$ canonical at \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol3/gravity/ch01-gravity-yield/topological-packing-fraction.md}; the $\alpha$-leak isolated and visible at the transformer, driver \texttt{\seqsplit{src/scripts/vol\_9\_device/node\_2domain\_nport.py}}). \subsection{Bulk-Shear Coupling (reciprocal skew vs the 3-port ring)}
\label{sec:vol9_node_circulator_ring}

\noindent The question is whether the mass channel (bulk) couples to the charge channel (shear). The circulator-coupling driver (\texttt{\seqsplit{src/scripts/vol\_9\_device/node\_circulator\_coupling.py}}; KB \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol9/ch3-pin-port-configuration/device-circuit-models.md} \S6.5) gives a partial answer: A conservative skew-Hermitian shear$\leftrightarrow$bulk coupling \textbf{exists}: it does \emph{not} pump (norm conserved to $1.1\times10^{-12}$ over 40k steps), is \emph{not} isolation (bulk-only load transfers 100\% into the empty shear winding), and is \emph{not} inert (acts on the poloidal winding, ON $\ne$ OFF). What it does \emph{not} yet close: the \textbf{2-port skew is RECIPROCAL} (forward $=$ reverse, RH $=$ LH) --- it \emph{is} the reciprocal optical-activity \textbf{gyrator}, not a one-way router. Genuine chiral \textbf{non-reciprocity} (a one-way circulator) requires the \textbf{3-port ring} with the EM/photon port as the third leg, whose gauge-invariant loop phase $3\chi\theta_\chi$ ($\theta_\chi = 2\pi\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}$, $\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}=2/7$) flips sign with the lattice chirality $\chi$. But the asymmetry is small ($1.75\times10^{-3}$) and the non-reciprocity \textbf{magnitude is IMPOSED} ($\theta_\chi, \tilde\kappa$ plugged) because the cubic-FDTD engine averages chirality out --- so the non-reciprocity magnitude is an echo (calibrated, not derived), consistent with the FORM-deriving / VALUE-importing classification of the rest of the node (Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_general_description}~$\S$\ref{subsec:vol9_form_vs_value}). The coupling exists, is bounded, lossless, winding-acting, and helicity-transferring (form forced trivially), is $\alpha$-free throughout ($\tilde\kappa = 6/5$, $\theta_\chi = 2\pi\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}$), and \textbf{directional-motion$\to$mass is NEGATIVE}. Distinguish the chiral \emph{circulator} (non-reciprocal inter-tank) from the optical-activity \emph{gyrator} (reciprocal); they are different elements (\S\ref{sec:vol9_graded_vacuum_network}, device-circuit-models \S6.3).

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Dispersion (Q1).} The K4 lattice is a graded LC transmission line: per-node $L_{\mathrm{cell}}=\mu_0\ell_{\mathrm{node}}$, $C_{\mathrm{cell}}=\varepsilon_0\ell_{\mathrm{node}}$ wired by the $z=3$ mutual inductive struts. This is the photon's first anisotropy and is the AVE-distinct FORM-level CHORD; the magnitude ($\sim2.2\times10^{-22}$ at $633$ nm optical, $3.5\times10^{-39}$ at $30$ GHz microwave) is \textbf{imported, not derived} (\kbleaf{ave-kb/vol1/dynamics/ch4-continuum-electrodynamics/preferred-frame-and-emergent-lorentz.md}, clm-yr6tu4, weak-C scoped).
