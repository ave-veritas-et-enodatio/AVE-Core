\subsection{Graded Vacuum Impedance Network (Foundation Repair)}
\label{sec:vol9_graded_vacuum_network}

\noindent The graded vacuum impedance network is the equivalent-circuit model of the medium drawn as three wired reactance channels --- $Z_{\mathrm{EM}} \equiv Z_0$ (the matched radiative port, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}=0$), $Z_{\mathrm{shear}}=\rho_{\mathrm{bulk}}\,c_{\mathrm{shear}}$ (deviatoric $G$, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{shear}}\to-1$, the charge-``3'' Cosserat winding), and $Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}=\rho_{\mathrm{bulk}}\,c_{\mathrm{bulk}}=\sqrt{2}\,\rho_{\mathrm{bulk}}\,c_0$ at $K=2G$ ($\Gamma_{\mathrm{bulk}}\to-1$, the mass-``3'' A1 dilatation) --- coupled through a chiral circulator and terminated at confinement surfaces. \textbf{Mixed impedance domains:} only $Z_{\mathrm{EM}}\equiv Z_0$ is an \emph{electrical} impedance ($\Omega$); $Z_{\mathrm{shear}}$ and $Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ are \emph{mechanical/acoustic} ($\rho\times$speed) and are \emph{not} in $Z_0$ units (writing ``$Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}=\sqrt2\,Z_0$'' is the electrical-vs-mechanical mis-scope the three-impedance law warns against). The channels are co-equal in role but in different domains, so EM$\leftrightarrow$mechanical coupling requires a transducer, not a direct wire (candidate: the TKI-transformer, \texttt{def-tk1xfm}, proposed/gated --- flagged not asserted), while the bulk$\leftrightarrow$shear inter-grade coupling is the conserved $H_{\mathrm{couple}}$. This is a \textbf{consistency re-expression} of the three-impedance law, not a new substrate primitive (per \textsc{invariant-n1} the network is the circuit model, not a substrate-object noun). It is documented as \textbf{foundation repair carrying open gates}, not a solved framework. Full content, the $\mathcal{M},\mathcal{J},\mathcal{Q}$ honest map (refuted bijection), the forks, and the open-gate registry live in \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol9/ch3-pin-port-configuration/device-circuit-models.md} \S6. The per-DOF node constitutive layer \emph{beneath} the scalar cell --- each translation DOF an $(L_i,C_i)$ reactance, from which one circuit yields isotropic-co-scale achromaticity, deviatoric birefringence, and the $(q\,\ell_{\mathrm{node}})$ dispersion tell --- is documented in \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol9/ch3-pin-port-configuration/per-dof-vacuum-node-circuit.md}.

\paragraph{Build-A isolation eigensolver --- the intrinsic mode is lossless ($Q\to\infty$); $\alpha^{-1}$ is the \emph{loaded} $Q$.}
The substrate-native graded-vacuum-network isolation eigensolver (Build-A, Stages~0--3) was run and \textbf{halted at its mandatory validate-on-known gate}: the native isolation-leg eigen-$Q$ is \emph{lossless-confined} ($Q\gg45$, growing toward $\infty$ with resolution) --- not in the validate band, not $\sim137$, not $\sim3$. This is the honest discriminating-test outcome, and it carries three canonical readings (full content in \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol2/particle-physics/ch01-topological-matter/electron-bound-resonator-coverage.md} and \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol4/circuit-theory/ch1-vacuum-circuit-analysis/theorem-3-1-q-factor.md}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Electron persistence (Class-C \emph{consistency}, not a chord).} With the EM radiative port closed the operator is Hermitian, $\mathrm{Im}(\omega)\to0$, so the \emph{intrinsic} $Q\to\infty$ ($1.4\times10^{16}$) $\Rightarrow$ infinite lifetime $\Rightarrow$ the electron persists in an ideal vacuum, matching the empirically most-stable particle ($\tau>10^{28}\,$yr). AVE obtains stability from an \emph{impedance} argument (lossless medium $+$ $\Gamma=-1$ confinement) where standard physics uses a \emph{symmetry} argument (charge conservation). Recorded as a consistency check --- it partly follows from the lossless axiom (Axiom~3) --- never headlined as emergence.
  \item \textbf{$\boldsymbol{\alpha^{-1}=137}$ is the \emph{loaded/radiative} $Q$, not the intrinsic resonator $Q$ (a reframe).} Since the intrinsic mode is lossless, $137$ is re-attributed to how the lossless mode couples \emph{out} through the EM port ($|\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}|^2=1-\alpha$, a fraction $\alpha$ leaking per bounce). Clean separation: \emph{stability/persistence} $=$ intrinsic lossless confinement ($Q\to\infty$); \emph{interaction} $=$ radiative loading (loaded $Q=1/\alpha$ $=$ the intrinsic vacuum$\leftrightarrow$EM coupling coefficient). Even loaded, the electron does \emph{not} decay --- $1/\alpha$ is a coupling (self-dressing), not a lifetime. This retracts no prior: $\alpha$ stays an \emph{echo} at the value level; derivability of the loaded-$Q$ value remains open.
  \item \textbf{$\boldsymbol{\alpha}$ stays echo --- five ways; the $Q$ slot stays empty.} Golden-Torus $R\cdot r=\tfrac14$ closed, the $z_0$-route closed, $4\pi^3+\pi^2+\pi$ a calibration identity, the cold-cage $Q\approx30.8\ne137$, and the eigenframe $Q\to\infty$. Both the $30.8$ negative and the eigenframe-$\infty$ stand as reported rows; no $\alpha$ echo$\to$chord promotion. The re-posed ``derive the loaded $Q$'' test is adjudicated \emph{circular} (the engine leak is literally \texttt{1.0-alpha}; the $\alpha$-free edge-radiation answer is already the cold-cage $30.8$) --- no new slot is minted; the load-bearing next step stays the substrate-native coupled-network $Q$ targeting the \emph{observed} electron $Q$ (not $137$), fusing the rank-2 base-crack repair.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Two-Domain N-Port (EM $\Omega$ $+$ mechanical Rayl, joined by a transformer)}
\label{sec:vol9_node_2domain_nport}

\noindent The graded-network schematic above draws the three reactance channels in one picture, but $Z_{\mathrm{EM}}$ is an \emph{electrical} impedance ($\Omega$) while $Z_{\mathrm{shear}}$ and $Z_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ are \emph{mechanical/acoustic} (Rayl $=$ Pa$\cdot$s/m). A faithful netlist must keep the two impedance domains separate. The substrate node is therefore a \textbf{two-domain N-port}: an EM sub-network (the \emph{sole external} radiative port, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{EM}}=0$, $Z_{\mathrm{EM}}\equiv Z_0$) and a mechanical 2-port (shear $=$ charge and bulk $=$ mass, both confined $\Gamma\to-1$, coupled through the conserved $H_{\mathrm{couple}}$), bridged by an electro-mechanical \textbf{transducer}. Because the two ports carry different units, the bridge is an \textbf{ideal transformer} (a turns-ratio change-of-reference), \emph{not} a gyrator and \emph{not} a direct wire. The load-bearing consequence: $\alpha$ does not smear across the network --- it \textbf{localizes to exactly the transducer turns-ratio}, the $p_c = 8\pi\alpha$ packing-fraction residual ($p_c = 8\pi\alpha$ canonical at \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol3/gravity/ch01-gravity-yield/topological-packing-fraction.md}; the $\alpha$-leak isolated and visible at the transformer, driver \texttt{src/scripts/vol\_9\_device/node\_2domain\_nport.py} header lines 84--89). The transformer node is itself \texttt{def-tk1xfm}, status \emph{proposed} --- gated behind ratification, flagged not asserted (KB \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol9/ch3-pin-port-configuration/device-circuit-models.md} \S6.5). \subsection{Fork-A Coupling Resolution (reciprocal skew vs the 3-port ring)}
\label{sec:vol9_node_circulator_ring}

\noindent Fork-A asks whether the mass channel (bulk) couples to the charge channel (shear). The 2026-06-20 circulator-coupling driver (\texttt{src/scripts/vol\_9\_device/node\_circulator\_coupling.py}, verdict \textbf{PARTIAL}; KB \kbleaf{ave-kb/vol9/ch3-pin-port-configuration/device-circuit-models.md} \S6.5) resolves it as follows. A conservative skew-Hermitian shear$\leftrightarrow$bulk coupling \textbf{exists} and escapes the earlier graft-v3 trilinear dead-end on all three failure modes: it does \emph{not} pump (norm conserved to $1.1\times10^{-12}$ over 40k steps), is \emph{not} isolation (bulk-only load transfers 100\% into the empty shear winding, $50\times$ the failed graft-v3 $\sim2\%$), and is \emph{not} inert (acts on the poloidal winding, ON $\ne$ OFF). What it does \emph{not} yet close: the \textbf{2-port skew is RECIPROCAL} (forward $=$ reverse, RH $=$ LH) --- it \emph{is} the reciprocal optical-activity \textbf{gyrator} (\texttt{def-0pt1ac}), not a one-way router. Genuine chiral \textbf{non-reciprocity} (a one-way circulator) requires the \textbf{3-port ring} with the EM/photon port as the third leg, whose gauge-invariant loop phase $3\chi\theta_\chi$ ($\theta_\chi = 2\pi\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}$, $\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}=2/7$) flips sign with the lattice chirality $\chi$. But the asymmetry is small ($1.75\times10^{-3}$) and the non-reciprocity \textbf{magnitude is IMPOSED} ($\theta_\chi, \tilde\kappa$ plugged) because the cubic-FDTD engine averages chirality out --- so the row reads \textbf{ECHO at the non-reciprocity magnitude} (the same FORM-deriving / VALUE-importing verdict as the rest of the node; Ch.~\ref{ch:vol9_general_description}~$\S$\ref{subsec:vol9_form_vs_value}). The coupling exists, is bounded, lossless, winding-acting, and helicity-transferring (form forced trivially), is $\alpha$-free throughout ($\tilde\kappa = 6/5$, $\theta_\chi = 2\pi\nu_{\mathrm{vac}}$), and \textbf{directional-motion$\to$mass is NEGATIVE}. Distinguish the chiral \emph{circulator} (non-reciprocal inter-tank) from the optical-activity \emph{gyrator} (reciprocal); they are different elements (\S\ref{sec:vol9_graded_vacuum_network}, device-circuit-models \S6.3).
