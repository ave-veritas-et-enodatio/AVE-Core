\section{Miller Multiplication and Regime III}
\label{sec:vol9_miller_regime_iii}

In Regime~III ($\sqrt{3}/2 \le r < 1$, $0 < S \le 0.500$), the substrate amplifies still-coherent but saturated small-signal perturbations by the Miller factor. The canonical statement is at \texttt{ave-kb/vol1/operators-and-regimes/ch7-regime-map/four-regimes.md} (\texttt{clm-b2anl4}):

\begin{resultbox}{Miller Multiplication Identity (Regime III)}
\begin{equation}
    \boxed{M(r) = \frac{1}{S(r)^2} = \frac{1}{1 - r^2}}
    \label{eq:vol9_miller_identity}
\end{equation}
The Miller factor diverges as $r \to 1$ (equivalently, as $A_0 \to A_{yield}$, $S \to 0$, $M \to \infty$). The substrate amplifies any small-signal perturbation injected into the bulk by exactly this factor.
\end{resultbox}

The identity is structurally identical to the engineering avalanche-multiplication law $M = 1/(1 - (V_R/V_{BR})^n)$ at $n = 2$. The semiconductor $V_{BR}$ is the engineering name for the substrate $V_{yield}$ at the doped-silicon scale; the same kernel governs both. At $r = \sqrt{3}/2$ (Regime II$\to$III boundary), $M(r) = 4$; at $r = 0.95$, $M = 10.25$; at $r = 0.99$, $M = 50.25$; at $r \to 1$, $M$ diverges and the substrate enters Regime~IV.

\textbf{Substrate-amplification mechanism.} The substrate's Regime~III amplification is a substrate-native response to the saturation kernel's curvature in the high-strain limit: as $S \to 0$, the local energy-trapping quality factor $Q(r) = 1/S(r)$ exceeds the sector's minimum multipole, and stored energy per cycle exceeds radiated energy. The substrate becomes an energy-storage element on the timescale of one drive cycle; any small-signal perturbation injected into the bulk during this storage period is amplified by $Q^2 = M$ on the next discharge. The amplification is substrate-natural, not engineered. Engineering practice characterizes this regime via avalanche-photodiode gain, Geiger-mode avalanche detection, and impact-ionization carrier multiplication in reverse-biased semiconductor junctions.

\textbf{Sector dependence.} Per \texttt{four-regimes.md}, the $\ell_{\min} = 2$ argument that sets the Regime II$\to$III boundary $r_2 = \sqrt{3}/2$ is specific to the spin-2 (gravitational-wave / shear-mode) sector. The photon / vector sector ($\ell_{\min} = 1$) has $r_2 = 0$ --- avalanche onset is concurrent with linear-Maxwell breakdown; the scalar sector ($\ell_{\min} = 0$) has no avalanche-onset boundary at all. The unqualified citation in this chapter implicitly invokes the spin-2 sector; see \texttt{four-regimes.md} for the full sector-dependent treatment.
