\aveentry{2026-05-31}{EXP-014}{Matched-LC coupling: $\Gamma\!\to\!0$ sweep}

\objective
Measure the reflection coefficient $\Gamma$ across a bond-junction
impedance sweep and locate the matched-coupling null predicted by Op3,
where the incident flux transfers without back-reflection.

\hypothesis
At impedance match the differential tensor line carries the full
$V_\mathrm{inc}\!\leftrightarrow\!\Phi_\mathrm{link}$ exchange, so
$|\Gamma|^2$ falls below the noise floor at $z = z_0$.

\apparatus
Single A-site driven against four tetrahedral B-neighbours
(coordination $z=4$); network analyser on the drive port; saturation
kernel active per Axiom 4.

\procedure
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=1pt]
  \item Set branch admittance $Y_b = \nu_\mathrm{vac} = 2/7$.
  \item Sweep drive impedance $z$ over $[0.6,\,1.6]\,z_0$, 41 points.
  \item Record $|\Gamma|^2$ and the converged self-consistent $S_{11}$.
\end{enumerate}

\dataheading
\begin{avedata}{@{}l r r r@{}}
\avedatahead{$z/z_0$} & \avedatahead{$|\Gamma|^2$} & \avedatahead{$S_{11}$} & \avedatahead{Note}\\
\midrule
0.80 & \meas{0.0412}{} & \meas{-0.203}{} & off-match \\
0.95 & \meas{0.0036}{} & \meas{-0.060}{} & near \\
1.00 & \meas{0.0001}{} & \meas{-0.010}{} & \textbf{match} \\
1.05 & \meas{0.0039}{} & \meas{0.062}{}  & near \\
1.20 & \meas{0.0455}{} & \meas{0.213}{}  & off-match \\
\end{avedata}

\plotframe[48mm]{Fig. 1 — $|\Gamma|^2$ vs.\ normalised drive impedance}

\analysis
The null sits at $z=z_0$ with residual $|\Gamma|^2 = (1.0\pm0.4)\times10^{-4}$,
consistent with a parameter-free fixed point ($|S_{11}|^2 \approx 0.0037$,
converged in 7 iterations).

\begin{averesult}
Matched-coupling null confirmed at $z=z_0$: $|\Gamma|^2 = (1.0\pm0.4)\times10^{-4}$,
i.e.\ $>99.98\%$ flux transfer. Forward-prediction, not a fit.
\end{averesult}

\begin{avenote}
The 7-iteration convergence ties to the back-saturation ratio
$|S_{11}|^2\approx0.0037$; see the self-consistent $M_W$ derivation.
\end{avenote}

\begin{avecaution}
Drive amplitude must stay below $V_\mathrm{yield}$ at the nearest
neighbour or Axiom-4 saturation distorts the measured $\Gamma$.
\end{avecaution}

\conclusion
Op3 matched-impedance coupling reproduces the predicted reflection null
to within measurement uncertainty. Proceed to the off-axis ($z\neq z_0$)
phase characterisation.
