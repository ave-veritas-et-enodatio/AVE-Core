## The thesis (to be challenged, then canonicalized if it survives)

1. A theory that predicts *dimensionful* numbers needs ≥1 dimensionful **RULER** — irreducible, legitimate calibration ("align our ruler with the universe's"). In natural units (c=ℏ=1) there is exactly ONE remaining dimension (mass/energy), so the minimum is **one** ruler: the mass scale (m_e / ℓ_node).
2. That license covers dimensionful **scale-setting only** — NOT dimensionless value-imports.
3. Dimensionless imports (α; G as the ratio (m_e/M_Planck)²) are **not rulers** — they are **chord-debts**: derivable structure not yet closed, or honest echoes if they can't be closed.
4. Topological integers (crossing/winding/linking) are **forced chords**, never calibration — they belong on the FORM side.
5. "Minimum correct calibration set" = (# independent dimensionful rulers) + (# irreducible dimensionless inputs). AVE's claim: **one ruler**. Operationalized by the three-route commitment (Ω_freeze → α, G, 𝒥_cosmic via u₀*).
6. Honest register: NOT "zero parameters" (over-claim) — **"one irreducible ruler + a forced dimensionless structure + named chord-debts."**

**Why foundational:** it is the framework's honest epistemic stance on the whole "how many parameters" question; it converts the "26 → 3 → zero-target" story from an over-claim into a precise, falsifiable research program; and it is the defensible register in which to state the framework's parameter economy. It is the chord-vs-echo north-star stated at the meta-level: a chord-framework claims *every dimensionless number is forced*; an echo-laden one admits irreducible dimensionless inputs.
