## Purpose

## The two-gate test (BOTH required to earn a structural-unification re-tag)

1. The SM genuinely treats the quantities as **independent free parameters** — not already unified by a symmetry the SM has (gauge unification, anomaly cancellation, dimensional transmutation are the SM's nearest analogs and DEFEAT distinctness if they apply).
2. AVE genuinely **forces** the relationship — not merely *defines* it. **Circularity trap:** if AVE sets the quantities via each other (a chain of definitions), that is import-economy, not forced structure.

## Discipline (non-negotiable — this is the most rescue-prone pass we run)

- **Adversarial-by-default:** one refute-by-default skeptic per candidate. Default verdict = **stays pure-echo**; the structural tag must be *earned*, read off the actual `constants.py` definitions + the actual SM treatment, not the appealing narrative.
