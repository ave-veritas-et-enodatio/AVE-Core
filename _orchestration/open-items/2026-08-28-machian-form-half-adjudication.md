## DECISION 1 — does the derived-FORM half survive?

Canon's `G`-ruling keeps **derived-FORM / fitted-VALUE**. The derived-form half
is what carries *"`G` is the input impedance of a cosmic transmission line
terminated at the Hubble horizon"* into `wall-taxonomy.md` row 7 and
`translation-circuit.md:134` as physics. **That mechanism had never been
audited.** Against Ax1-4:

- **The axioms supply no outer termination.** A space group has no last row;
  Ax3's `|Gamma|^2` minimization is scoped to *internal* boundaries; Ax4's two
  horizons are keyed on **local strain reaching yield**, i.e. they sit at
  *high* strain next to mass — the opposite end of the line from `R_H`.
- **A causal limit is not an impedance discontinuity.** `Gamma` is defined off
  `Z`, `Z` is a property of the medium, and at `R_H` nothing about the medium
  changes. `Gamma = 0` — not as an effective load, but because there is no load.
- **No termination gives a coupling linear in cell count.** Open and short give
  purely reactive impedances *periodic* in length; matched gives `Z_0` with no
  length in it. Linear-in-`N` is the DC lumped-ladder result — the circuit in
  which the termination does no work. Canon states the ladder reading out loud
  one line below the input-impedance claim (`translation-circuit.md:135`).
- **Row 7's `Gamma` cell is empty** — it restates the noun where rows 1-6 and 8
  each carry a real reflection condition — its anchor returns zero on
  `terminat|input impedance|transmission line|R_H|reflection` by two methods,
  and it declares channel "all" in violation of that same leaf's `:160` rule.

**The repair is a demolition, not a tune-up.** Restoring the dropped spherical
Jacobian `r'^2` gives a **convergent** accumulation with no `R_H` in it, making
`G` purely local — and vacating, as a set, the Dirac-LNH derivation,
`m_P = m_e sqrt(7 xi)`, `R_H/ell = alpha^2/(28 pi alpha_G)`, the `H_infinity`
consistency proof, and the `rho_Lambda` closure. **Every one is a rearrangement
of the same relation.** It points where canon's own open item already points:
Chain B'.

**A lighter option exists:** relabel `xi` as what the integral computes — a
radial shell-count with equal per-shell weight — and drop the impedance story
without touching the arithmetic. Whether "derived-FORM" survives that relabel
is the decision.

**Do NOT hand-set row 7's `Gamma` to close this.** `wall-taxonomy.md §10.3` is
canon's own rule: *"Computed, not chosen. The authority for a wall's `Gamma`
phase is the branch-derived indicial wall row of a certified instrument.
