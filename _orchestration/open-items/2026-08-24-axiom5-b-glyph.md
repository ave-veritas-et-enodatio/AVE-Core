**The collision.** `eq_axiom_5.tex` uses $B(M)$ for the **enclosed A1 compression charge**
in $\oint_S \mathbf{u}\cdot\hat n = 4\pi B(M)$ — a volume-dimensioned bookkeeping scalar on
the LONGITUDINAL sector. Axiom 1 assigns $\mathbf{B}$ to the three microrotational DOFs —
the magnetic field, on the TRANSVERSE/Cosserat sector.

**Why this is the highest-risk glyph in the axiom set.** The A1 ⊥ T2 sector-ownership rule
is one of the corpus's most-policed invariants (`master-equation.md` two-"3"s box, the
never-cross-wire guards). This is the same glyph on both sides of that fence, in the axiom
layer itself. Compounding: Axiom 5 is the newest axiom and its own vocabulary block rules on
$u$, $\varepsilon_{11}$ and $\mathcal{A}_g$ — and is silent on $B$.

**The question for Grant.** Rename $B(M)$, or bind it explicitly in the `eq_axiom_5.tex`
vocabulary block alongside the three terms already ruled there? (A rename is cheaper now
than after the bound-sector law accumulates consumers.)
