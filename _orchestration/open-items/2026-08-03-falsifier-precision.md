**The ask (one line):** *once no ppm label on $C_2$ is defensible, what precision does the
electron falsifier assert — or does the falsifier come down?*

The 2026-08-03 ruling struck every ppm deviation label on $C_2$ with no number substituted, but
`:236` still reads live: a 50 ppm falsification threshold. The two limiters that killed the
deviation labels kill the threshold too — with $dC_2/d\tau = -11.4555$ and $\tau_{retard}$
**asserted, not derived**, a 50 ppm threshold needs $\tau$ to 1.43 ppm, which nothing supplies.

**★ Classification:** changing a falsifier's asserted precision is a **physics decision**, not a
label sweep. Not the strike lane's call and not the repair lane's.

Four options are on the table at the source; **none picked, none recommended-as-ruled.**
