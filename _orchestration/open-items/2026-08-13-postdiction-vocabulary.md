The 2026-08-13 split created `manuscript/postdictions.yaml`. That puts the word in a tracked
filename, which is effectively a mint — and the corpus has so far declined to mint it.

**The asymmetry.** Its three sibling terms all have SOLID, Grant-ratified `def-` nodes:
`chord` (`def-ch0rd1`), `echo` (`def-ech0v1`), `mixed` (`def-fmv001`) — and **both** `chord` and
`echo` carry `open-ambiguity-flag: YES` with a first-cite qualification rule, precisely because they
collide with the harmony metaphor of the north-star.

**"postdiction" has none of that.** No `def-` node, no adjudicated meaning, no ambiguity flag —
despite being used operationally at high stakes in ~15 Rule-12 walk-backs, including the
torus-knot baryon demotion (*"Matching a predicted mass to the nearest already-cataloged state is
**postdiction (retrodiction), not forward prediction**"*) which drove a 0.85 → 0.60 solidity cut.

**The decision:** either mint `def-` properly — with the ambiguity flag and first-cite rule its
siblings carry — or rename the file for what it gates, e.g. `consistency-manifest.yaml`, matching
the README's own *"45 consistency-class entries."* Leaving it as-is mints the term by filename
without the adjudication the sibling terms each required.
