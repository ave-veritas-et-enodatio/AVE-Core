**Not a coverage hole — a standing gap.** The autonomous-HB lens audit's
charter item **A2** (*"Autonomous/oscillator HB is standard external prior art
AND its phase-normalization is the same move as clause Q"*) **was run**, in the
review phase, by a lane scoped to *"Charter item A2 only, graded as two separate
sub-claims."* Both halves returned verdicts:

- **A2a — RETRIEVED AND CONFIRMED, precondition dropped.** The lane states
  *"The record flags this claim as asserted-from-discipline-knowledge and NOT
  retrieved. **I retrieved it.**"* Three sources named: Wiley-IEEE *Analysis and
  Design of Autonomous Microwave Circuits*; Elsevier *A robust and efficient
  oscillator analysis technique using harmonic balance*; **arXiv 1006.4931**.
  The technique is as the record describes; what the transfer omits is that the
  extra-unknown/extra-equation counting yields an **isolated** solution only
  under gain-vs-loss balance — the ingredient Ax3 forbids.
- **A2b — RESEMBLANCE, NOT THE SAME OBJECT. "NOT FIT to travel to a prereg as
  written."** Clause Q is a **DC / zero-frequency** condition
  (`manuscript/common_equations/eq_axiom_5.tex`:82) removing an additive
  constant (group ℝ) of static potentials; autonomous-HB normalization removes a
  **U(1)** orbit of an oscillating phasor at ω≠0. Different group, frequency,
  sector and origin of degeneracy. R55 (`:58`, *"A ground reference is a gauge
  choice, not a material primitive"*) licenses only the **GENUS**, never
  identity of object. R43 (`manuscript/ave-kb/common/vocabulary-register.md`:500)
  is **BINDING**: *"ground reference" is the EE-analogy gloss, NEVER the
  canonical noun* — canonical noun is **"DC operating point / quiescent point
  (Q-point)"**.

**What is actually open, three things:**

1. **Neither A2 finding received the adversarial verify pass.** The verify phase
   re-tested only 6 of the review phase's 28 findings, and all six came from
   three of the six lanes. A2's lane contributed **zero**. Both verdicts are
   **single-lane and un-refuted-tested**.
2. **The retrieval did not run through the external-retrieval pipeline.** Three
   sources are named but were not gate-specified and not adjudicated in session,
   so under the standing pipeline discipline they hold **tentative standing**,
   not discharged-with-authority.
3. **The R43 breach is wider than one file and is not repaired.** The lens
   record uses *"ground reference"* as the **warrant** for the identification,
   unlabelled as an analogy — and **R55 `:56-58` does the same thing**, so the
   record inherited rather than minted it. A register sweep, not a physics
   change.

**Why it matters:** A2b is the plank that makes the lens look canon-endorsed
rather than newly minted. Carried into a prereg as written, *"the EE method and
the source law agree on the treatment"* would let a design choice — which phase
to pin, on which port, in which sector — inherit ratified-clause authority it
does not have.

**Correction of record, because the routing that produced this item was wrong.**
The verify-phase synthesis reported A2 as a *"total coverage hole … UNDISCHARGED,
BOTH HALVES"*. That is **true of the verify phase and false of the audit** — the
synthesizer never saw the review journal. Four other coverage claims in the same
synthesis section fail the same way (A4, A5, the discrimination check, and *"the
routing item is quoted by no lane"*). Full correction table: result doc §5.1.
