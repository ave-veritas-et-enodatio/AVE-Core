(`kernel-normalization-homonym`, deleted 2026-08-24) asserted that the electron's
$\Gamma=-1$ wall requires the kernel to zero at $V_{yield}$; that was **inverted** against

**What IS open — a notational collision, verified on three receipts (2026-08-24):**

1. `manuscript/ave-kb/CLAUDE.md` INVARIANT-S2 (Axiom 4, axiom-level canon) writes the kernel
   as $S(A)=\sqrt{1-(A/A_{yield})^2}$ — the argument's denominator is the YIELD scale.

   $A=0\to1$, and :44 states $E_S$ "is precisely the field at which $A=1$ … by
   construction" — the same glyph normalized to the SNAP scale, $1/\sqrt\alpha\approx11.7$
   times higher.
3. `def-vyvsn1` itself computes $S(\sqrt\alpha)=\sqrt{1-\alpha}\approx0.996$ from
   $A=V_{yield}/V_{snap}=\sqrt\alpha$. That arithmetic requires the BARE form. Fed through
   Axiom 4's own literal $A/A_{yield}$ expression, the identical physical state yields
   $A/A_{yield}=1$ and $S=0$.

So one physical state (the electron's A1 mass core) reads $S\approx0.996$ under one
canonical formula and $S=0$ under the other, purely from which normalization the glyph
carries. Both leaves are correct in their own convention; neither states its convention.
