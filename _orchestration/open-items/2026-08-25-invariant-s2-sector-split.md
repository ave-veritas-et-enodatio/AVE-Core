**The tension.** `resonant-lc-solitons.md`:41 (Grant-ratified INVARIANT-S2)
splits the two saturation reads **between sectors**: `C_0/S` is the
*longitudinal-A1 bond compliance* → `Z_tank→0` (the short), `ε_0 S` is the
*transverse-T2 permittivity* → `Z_wave→∞` (the open) — *"Orthogonal reactances,
both |Γ|=1, differing only in boundary phase."* But `master-equation.md`:105,107
writes **both** branches as WAVE impedances `√(μ/ε)`: `ε_eff→0` gives `Z→∞`,
and `μ_eff→0` gives `Z→0, Γ→−1` — so both boundary phases live on the transverse
wave channel.

**What canon's own reconciliation clause does and does not do.** The same
`:41` note closes: *"so this leaf's electric-sector Z→0 confinement and the
master-equation magnetic-branch confinement are distinct-sector statements, not
a contradiction."* That reconciles the two **confinement** statements — and
**names no sector home for the μ-branch's WAVE-CHANNEL short**, which is the
contested question. (The clearing lens flagged that the prereg's restatement had
truncated this clause; the Stage-1 result now carries it in full.)

**Aggravating fork:** the μ-at-core question is already routed open
(`saturation-rim-inversion.md`:70 — μ_eff→0 Meissner vs the INCREASING
relativistic inductor), so this item and that one are substantially ONE open
question.

**★ CANDIDATE RESOLUTION (orchestrator, WALK-grade, 2026-08-25 walk §3 — a
proposal, not a ruling):** the two are **BRANCHES, not sectors**. A transverse
wave sees a short in the magnetic-first branch (`μ_eff→0`) and an open in the
electric-first branch (`ε_eff→0`); the **A1 longitudinal tank shorts in BOTH**,
because `C_eff = C_0/S → ∞` whenever `S→0` regardless of which parameter drove
it. Under this reading INVARIANT-S2 states the electric-first branch and
master-equation states the magnetic-first branch; both are transverse-wave
statements differing by branch; A1 shorts either way.

**The check this proposes (cheap, UN-RUN):** the longitudinal channel should
behave the SAME under both loadings while the transverse channel mirrors —
Stage 1 measured the transverse half (mirror-antisymmetric to 0.45%, PR #1012);
the longitudinal half is unrun and would discriminate.

**Options for the ruling:** (a) the branch reading above — master-equation's
wave-impedance phrasing is then loose for the μ side; (b) both branches
genuinely live on T2 and INVARIANT-S2's split is about something narrower than
it reads; (c) two different objects that a shared vocabulary collapsed.

**Consequence:** Stage 1 measured both loadings on the transverse channel. What
that measurement MEANS depends on this ruling — the loadings' `ε`/`μ` labels are
currently declared as MAP names, never constitutive attributions.
