Surfaced by the 2026-08-24 symbol-collision sweep; receipts re-verified by the orchestrator
before minting.

**The collision.** `delta-strain-cosmic-tcc.md` calls **both** glyphs "the Cosserat
couple-stress modulus" — `$G_c$ is the Cosserat couple-stress modulus` (in
$\omega_m^2 = 4 G_c / I_\omega$) and `The same Cosserat couple-stress modulus $\gamma_c$`
(in the weak-range relation $\ell_c = \sqrt{\gamma_c/G_{vac}}$).

**Why it matters rather than being a typo.** A dimensional walk puts them in different
units: $[\gamma_c] = [G_{vac}]\cdot L^2$ from $\ell_c=\sqrt{\gamma_c/G_{vac}}$, whereas
$G_c$ enters $\omega_m^2 = 4G_c/I_\omega$. They cannot be the same modulus unless one of the
two expressions is mis-stated. And the leaf's **joint-falsification claim** — that
falsifying the weak-force derivation simultaneously falsifies the δ_strain mechanism,
because "the substrate primitive $\gamma_c$ underlies both" — is exactly the chain that
reads the identity as load-bearing.

**The question for Grant.** Is this (a) a genuine identity, in which case one of the two
expressions is dimensionally wrong and needs repair; (b) a relation of the form
$G_c = \gamma_c/\ell^2$ for some specified $\ell$, in which case the joint-falsification
claim has been over-reading a proportionality as an identity and needs re-scoping; or (c)
two genuinely different moduli that a shared name collapsed?
