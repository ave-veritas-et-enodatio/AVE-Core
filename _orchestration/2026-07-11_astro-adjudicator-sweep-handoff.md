## MISSION

Run the **astrophysical adjudicator sweep**: six existing-data kill-tests, each **routed to the docket/registry row it decides**. The keying-register walk (`research/2026-07-11_keying-register-walk_framing.md`) left three forks with astrophysical adjudicators (T4 gravity keying; the §5 G-provenance WHEN axis; T3 ring-down loss-character) plus three corroborating books; this sweep confronts each against real data.

**Method.** External literature comes **only** through the **external-retrieval pipeline**: a retrieval agent works from a **gate-specified brief** (you write the brief; you do not browse), returns citations, and the satellite session **adjudicates**. **Every external claim lands at TENTATIVE standing** — a retrieval result is a pointer to be verified, never a fact. The satellite **recommends** verdicts; **GRANT rules**; **nothing is canonized in-lane** (no KB / Letter / manuscript edit — research/ + _orchestration/ only).

**Testing over fiat.** Each adjudicator is chosen because *existing data can decide it.* Where the data are contested, the deliverable is the **contested state reported honestly**, not a picked winner — the sweep's value is a clean map of which branches survive which data, with the disputes named.

## ★ FREEZE-ANALOG (BINDING)

**Before ANY retrieval output is read**, the satellite **commits AND PUSHES a BRANCH-SIGNATURE MAP** — the freeze-by-push analog of a prereg freeze (git ordering = freeze proof; the push must precede the first retrieval read). Per adjudicator, the map states:

1. **what each branch predicts** (the observable signature), and
2. **what observed state would count AGAINST each branch** — the demotion condition.

**P10 fireability is binding:** an adjudicator *none of whose outcomes could demote any branch* is **decorative** and must be reworked or dropped before freeze. The map's content is **pre-seeded verbatim below** (from the D1 walk); the satellite **freezes it verbatim-or-tightened, NEVER loosened post-retrieval.** A signature relaxed after seeing the data is a freeze violation and a review-CRITICAL finding.

## THE SIX ADJUDICATORS (freeze these branch maps before retrieval)

Each block is a **frozen branch-signature map** — the satellite pushes it verbatim-or-tightened, then retrieves against it. The corpus row each decides is named. The anchors listed are what the retrieval brief must go fetch (identifiers to be verified real — see the review lenses).

### A1 — RAR ENVIRONMENTAL DEPENDENCE → decides T4 keying (internal / total / TIDE)

**Decides:** registry T4 (`research/2026-07-10_collapse-target-registry.md` T4, "needs Grant keying decision (internal vs total field)") + the D1 §4 third branch (external TIDE).

**Signatures (frozen):**
- **internal-only** (`A` keys on the source's `g_N`) → **zero environmental dependence** of the RAR.
- **total-field** (`A` keys on internal + external `g_ext`) → **deviations track `g_ext`** (systems in strong external fields pushed toward the Newtonian branch).
- **tide** (`A` keys on `∇g_ext`) → **deviations track external TIDAL stress**, not `g_ext` itself.

**Demotion conditions:** a confirmed environmental RAR deviation that tracks `g_ext` **but not tide** demotes internal-only and tide, favors total-field; a deviation that tracks **tide but not `g_ext`** demotes internal-only and total-field, favors tide; a confirmed **null** (no environmental dependence at either axis) demotes both total-field and tide, favors internal-only. **The axes separate in data:** high-`g_ext`-low-tide systems vs high-tide systems must be compared *separately*.

**Retrieval anchors to verify:** SPARC RAR scatter (`~0.11 dex` claimed); Chae et al. EFE-in-RAR claims **and their contested status** (report the dispute, do not adjudicate the dispute).

### A2 — WIDE BINARIES vs CRATER-II-CLASS → the tide-branch discriminator

**Decides:** the D1 §4 tide branch vs standard-EFE vs internal-only, on systems where `g_ext` and tide **separate**.

**Signatures (frozen):**
- **standard-EFE** (total-field, MOND-as-modified-gravity) → **suppression in BOTH** wide binaries and Crater-II-class satellites.
- **tide** → **NO suppression in wide binaries** (strong `g_ext ≈ 1.8–2.0 a₀`, negligible tide → isolated-MOND-like behavior); **suppression allowed in Crater II** (strong MW tide).
- **internal-only** → **no suppression in EITHER** (Crater II then needs another mechanism to explain any observed suppression).

**Demotion conditions:** confirmed wide-binary suppression demotes tide and internal-only (favors standard-EFE); confirmed wide-binary *isolated-MOND* behavior + Crater-II suppression demotes standard-EFE and internal-only (favors tide); no suppression anywhere demotes standard-EFE and tide (favors internal-only, leaving Crater II as a separate puzzle).

**Retrieval anchors to verify:** the **Chae-vs-Banik wide-binary dispute** — the brief **DEMANDS the current dispute state reported as contested, never a picked winner** (Chae 2023 / Pittordis–Sutherland-class analyses on opposite sides). Crater II velocity-dispersion measurements.

### A3 — HIGH-Z ROTATION CURVES / a₀(z) → decides the WHEN axis (live vs attractor keying)

**Decides:** the D1 §5 G-provenance WHEN axis — live-keyed (G / `a₀` tracks `H(z)`) vs attractor-keyed (`a₀ = cH_∞/2π`, redshift-constant).

**Signatures (frozen):**
- **live** (`a₀ ∝ H(z)`) → a **larger MOND scale at `z ~ 1–2`** (where `H` was `~2–3×` today's).
- **attractor** (`a₀ = cH_∞/2π`, the ∞ subscript, `manuscript/ave-kb/vol3/cosmology/ch05-dark-sector/derived-mond-acceleration-scale.md:15`) → **redshift-constant `a₀`.**

**Demotion conditions:** a confirmed `a₀(z)` **rising with `H(z)`** demotes attractor; a confirmed **redshift-constant `a₀`** out to `z ~ 1–2` demotes live. (Either result is also a constraint on the fossil branch, which predicts a per-node formation-era value — but the fossil branch leans dead on the storage-medium argument already; see D1 §5.)

**Retrieval anchors to verify:** Genzel-class declining-rotation-curve claims at `z ~ 1–2` **and their contested reanalyses** (report both sides).

### A4 — RINGDOWN ECHOES + ABSORPTION → decides T3 (Γ=−1 loss-character)

**Decides:** registry T3 (`research/2026-07-10_collapse-target-registry.md` T3, "needs Grant loss-character ruling") — branch (i) lossless-reflective short vs branch (ii) dissipative fuse/matched at the rupture boundary.

**Signatures (frozen):**
- **branch (i) — lossless-reflective short** (the *same* `Γ=−1` object as the electron cage) → **post-ring-down ECHOES at detectable amplitude** + **sub-unity horizon absorption.**
- **branch (ii) — dissipative fuse/matched** → **no echoes**, **horizon-grade (near-unity) absorption.** Regime-legal: above-yield dissipation does not violate Axiom-3's sub-yield losslessness (`manuscript/ave-kb/vol4/claim-quality.md:1168`).

**Demotion conditions:** a **confirmed echo detection** at credible significance demotes branch (ii); a **confirmed absorption null / horizon-grade absorption** with no echoes demotes branch (i). The D1 lean is branch (ii) (echo searches contested-but-mostly-null + horizon-grade absorption) — the sweep tests whether the current data actually support that lean or leave it open.

**Retrieval anchors to verify:** Abedi/Afshordi-class echo claims **vs their null reanalyses** (report both sides + significance levels); QNM / inspiral-merger-ringdown (IMR) consistency levels.

### A5 — GW170817 ONE-c → books the channel-speed identity (already passed)

**Decides:** formal booking of the channel-speed identity — `c_gw = c_light` to `~10⁻¹⁵` reads in AVE as the shear and EM channels riding **ONE substrate `c`.** Already passed at the physics level; this adjudicator **books the bound and states what it excludes.**

**Signature / what it excludes:** the tight bound **excludes channel-split propagation modifications** (a separate shear-channel `c` differing from the EM `c` at the `10⁻¹⁵` level). There is no live AVE branch predicting a split, so this is a **consistency book, not a fork** — but it is a real constraint on any future channel-split proposal.

**Retrieval anchors to verify:** the GW170817 + GRB 170817A arrival-coincidence bound (`|c_gw − c|/c ≲ 10⁻¹⁵`). **NB — the corpus flags this as an external bound requiring verification** (`research/2026-06-11_chiral-vacuum-reactor-framing.md:393`); the sweep is where that verification lands. **Cheap lane** (retrieval-only; no contested adjudication).

### A6 — Ġ BOUNDS vs MACHIAN-G READINGS → constrains the live/fossil/attractor fork

**Decides:** the D1 §5 G-provenance fork (live / fossil / attractor), on the `Ġ` side.

**Books (frozen):**
- **LLR** `|Ġ/G| ≲ 1.5×10⁻¹³/yr` (+ pulsar-timing and BBN bounds as cross-checks);
- the **`~500×` exclusion of naive-live** G (a naive live rate `~ H₀ ≈ 7×10⁻¹¹/yr` is `~470×` above the LLR bound);
- the **non-discrimination of the Sciama coincidence under flatness** (it holds at every epoch, so "it holds now" favors no branch).

**Demotion conditions:** the bounds **already demote naive-live**; they do **not** by themselves demote flatness-protected-live or attractor (both satisfy the bound). This adjudicator's job is to **book the bounds cleanly and state which branches survive them**, not to resolve the fork.

**★ NOTE IN BRIEF (scope fence):** the **flatness-`Ġ` self-cancellation derivation** — does the canon Machian termination form self-cancel to `Ġ = 0` under flatness, or leave an era-transition residual at the LLR bound — is a **THEORY item, explicitly OUT of this satellite's scope** (named derivation debt, D1 §5; a separate arc). The satellite books the empirical bounds only.

**Retrieval anchors to verify:** LLR `Ġ/G` limits (Hofmann/Müller-class); pulsar-timing `Ġ` bounds; BBN `Ġ` constraints.
