## 2. Method — zero-line-shift, and why it was mandatory

**Line-count first (the mandated pre-edit measurement).** Before any edit, a Python `os.walk` + `re`
enumeration of every `path.ext:NN` cite pointing INTO the 44 candidate files was run over 4111 tracked
text files: **3997 incoming line-cites** resolve into these files (`constants.py` 1003,
`translation-circuit.md` 461, `cosserat_field_3d.py` 421, `vol4/claim-quality.md` 311,
`vocabulary-register.md` 264, `port-register.md` 141, …). Inserting a note *in body* would have moved
several thousand of them.

So the batch is **append-only at the line level**:

- **Same-line status stamp** at each live-canon site — appended to the end of the line, or inserted
  before the row's final `|` (markdown table cells), or before the closing brace / `\\` (LaTeX
  captions and table cells), or before the closing `"""` (a Python docstring terminator). The claim
  text is preserved byte-for-byte inside the stamped line.
- **The full dated note at EOF** — one per file, carrying the four-clause header form, the honesty
  rider, the per-site verbatim quote + the audited rationale verbatim, and the records.
- **No line is inserted or deleted above EOF in any file.**

Where the worklist anchor is mid-sentence, the stamp is placed at the end of that sentence or block
and the note records **both** the anchor line and the stamped line. Where the anchor is display math
(`04_continuum_electrodynamics.tex:348`, `mond-hoop-stress.md:40`) the math line is left byte-untouched
and the stamp goes on the resultbox's own title or scope line.

**R39 BYTE-FENCE.** **THREE** DIES sites sit inside Rule-12 preserved spans. None is stamped; all
three are routed to the governing leaf's own EOF ledger with a pointer, span bytes untouched — see §4.

## 2.1 The preserved-span census, RE-DERIVED with a detector that catches the missed class

The first pass's detector was a **±25-line banner-prose window**. It catches a preservation banner
printed near the stamp; it cannot catch a span whose declaration sits at the far end of a
**structural container**. That is exactly the `08_gravitational_waves.tex:208` class — the box's
declaration is 32 lines below the stamp.

**The method change — container-aware, not window-aware.** Widened on three axes:

- **(A) Containers, not windows.** For `.tex`, EVERY `\begin{X}` … `\end{X}` span enclosing the
  stamp is computed, with **no environment allow-list** — the tcolorbox family (`warningbox`,
  `resultbox`, `simbox`, `examplebox`, `summarybox`, `exercisebox`, `circuitbox`, `codebox`,
  `objectivebox`) plus `figure`, `table`, `tabular`, `itemize`, `enumerate`, `equation`, everything.
  For `.md`, the enclosing blockquote run, the enclosing `^#{1,6}` section, and the enclosing bullet
  block. For `.py`, the module docstring, the contiguous comment run, and the enclosing `def`/`class`.
- **(B) The WHOLE container is scanned at any distance** — the declaration may sit anywhere inside
  it, including its closing paragraph.
- **(C) Widened declaration vocabulary** — adds the container-naming forms the first pass had no
  pattern for: *"This box is preserved verbatim"*, *"both prior boxes are preserved verbatim"*,
  *"quoted verbatim and not paraphrased"*, *"verbatim [sic]"*, *"do NOT delete"*, `byte-fence(d)`,
  `KEEP-BOTH`, `SUPERSEDED … preserv…`, alongside the original banner prose.

## 5. The 5 DRIFTED-NEEDS-REPIN rows — re-derived, and the verdict CORRECTED
